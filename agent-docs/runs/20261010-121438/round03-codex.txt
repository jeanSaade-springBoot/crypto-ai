- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** The complete next specification must include registered requirements for:

  1. Full working-tree baseline identity, deployment artefact/configuration fingerprint, migration/Flyway policy, and resolution of the FIX-154 source-versus-documentation contradiction.
  2. Pre-write whole-window eligibility and a no-write refusal test.

  11. A retirement decision table: observers must be suppressed in baseline parity now; deletion remains a separate user-approved decision.
  12. Production regression, disposable-MySQL migration, no-look-ahead, zero-I/O, refusal, resource/cancellation, random-window, and incident-window validation.
