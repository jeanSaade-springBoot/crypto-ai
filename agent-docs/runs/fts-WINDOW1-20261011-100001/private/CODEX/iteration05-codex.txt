Initial BUY execution is intended to require a fresh canonical price, revalidate price against stop/target, then re-score entry quality at that execution price. - **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** The entry-quality model scores expansion from a time-bounded opportunity base, ATR extension, risk/reward, opportunity age, rejection-zone proximity, volatility, and stop exposure. Scores below 50 block an initial chase; remaining scores may cap allocation. [ExecutionIntelligenceService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/execution/service/ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:865)
- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** A paper position may have progressive adds: its quantity and average entry price can be changed after the initial entry while retaining the original `signal_id`. Therefore, the final `paper_position.entry_price`, quantity, and current managed-position entry-quality score must not be assumed to represent the initial entry alone. Every BUY ledger row during the position lifetime is required.
- **AGENT INFERENCE:** A loss could be attributable to the initial entry, a later scale-in, or neither. The evidence currently cannot distinguish these cases.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CODEX-I5-002  
Purpose: Retrieve bounded contemporaneous entry evidence for exactly the frozen positions, including initial signal quality and every linked BUY execution. All returned timestamps are UTC and must be converted to KSA by adding three hours in reporting.  
SQL:
```sql
SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    p.symbol,
    p.signal_id AS entry_signal_id,
    p.opened_at,
    p.closed_at,
    p.entry_price,
    p.quantity,
    p.stop_loss,
    p.take_profit,
    p.realized_pnl,
    s.candle_open_time,
    s.generated_at AS signal_generated_at,
    s.interval_code,
    s.decision,
    s.original_decision,
    s.final_entry_allowed,
    s.primary_blocking_stage,
    s.total_score,
    s.confidence_score,
    s.raw_confidence_score,
    s.effective_confidence_score,
    s.latest_price AS decision_price,
    s.stop_loss AS signal_stop_loss,
    s.take_profit AS signal_take_profit,
    s.risk_reward_ratio,
    s.atr_at_signal,
    s.atr_percent,
    s.atr_overextended,
    s.atr_immediate_entry_allowed,
    s.market_regime,
    s.selected_strategy,
    s.strategy_version,
    s.strategy_entry_allowed,
    s.btc_context_status,
    s.btc_context_entry_allowed,
    s.btc_context_candle_close_time,
    s.btc_context_age_seconds,
    s.btc_context_freshness_threshold_seconds,
    s.derivatives_status,
    s.derivatives_entry_allowed,
    s.liquidity_status,
    s.liquidity_entry_allowed,
    s.order_book_target_blocked,
    s.order_book_stop_exposed,
    s.data_quality_valid,
    s.signal_mode,
    s.entry_authority,
    s.entry_authority_max_position_percent,
    p.entry_reason,
    s.final_decision_explanation
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position AS p
JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal AS s
  ON s.id = p.signal_id
WHERE p.id IN (104, 101, 96, 91, 82, 78)
ORDER BY FIELD(p.id, 104, 101, 96, 91, 82, 78);

SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    p.symbol,
    p.signal_id AS original_entry_signal_id,
    p.opened_at,
    p.closed_at,
    wt.id AS wallet_trade_id,
    wt.signal_id AS wallet_signal_id,
    wt.side,
    wt.status,
    wt.execution_type,
    wt.execution_reason,
    wt.executed_at,
    wt.execution_price_observed_at,
    wt.decision_price_usdt,
    wt.price_usdt,
    wt.quantity,
    wt.gross_amount_usdt,
    wt.fee_usdt,
    wt.net_amount_usdt,
    wt.execution_message
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position AS p
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_trade AS wt
  ON wt.symbol = p.symbol
 AND wt.side = 'BUY'
 AND wt.status = 'EXECUTED'
 AND wt.executed_at >= p.opened_at - INTERVAL 5 MINUTE
 AND wt.executed_at <= p.closed_at
WHERE p.id IN (104, 101, 96, 91, 82, 78)
ORDER BY p.id, wt.executed_at;
```
