- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** `db/CODEX/` contains no result artifacts. Therefore no selected trade has durable DB evidence for its symbol, timing, entry, exit reason, P&L, wallet effect, audit row, or price-protection route.
- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** No per-trade root cause is established. The only confirmed root cause is an evidence gap in this investigation track—not a trader defect.
- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** In current source, ordinary stop-loss exits are persisted as `STOPPED`; the frozen study selection requires `CLOSED`. Subject to DB confirmation, this cohort excludes normal stop-loss records and is more likely to contain signal exits, profit-lock exits, take-profit paths, near-TP-related closures, or manual closes. [PaperTradingService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/service/PaperTradingService.java:170) [LivePositionProtectionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/position/service/LivePositionProtectionService.java:173)
- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** A material reconciliation risk exists in the signal-close route: `completeClose` persists `paper_position` as closed before attempting the linked wallet exit, then catches wallet exceptions. A `CLOSED` paper loss is therefore not, by itself, proof of a corresponding wallet SELL. This is a source-level risk, not evidence that it occurred for any frozen ID. [PaperTradingService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/service/PaperTradingService.java:487)
- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** Conversely, live price-protection routes call mechanical wallet exit first and close the paper position only on success. This distinction makes `production_exit_audit`, wallet ledger, and managed-position evidence essential to identify the actual route. [LivePositionProtectionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/position/service/LivePositionProtectionService.java:165)
- **AGENT INFERENCE:** Until those records are available, each loss must remain classified as **insufficient evidence**—not a strategy failure, stale-data incident, execution defect, or protection defect.
- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** The snapshot metadata has a two-second inconsistency: `2026-10-11 07:00:03 UTC` converts to **2026-10-11 10:00:03 KSA**, while the supplied snapshot KSA time is `10:00:01`. The frozen IDs and intended `10:00:00 KSA` window clock are unchanged.
- **EXTERNAL RESEARCH:** None. A public source cannot establish a cause for these private, paper-trading records; the next evidence must be the bounded local DB record set.
