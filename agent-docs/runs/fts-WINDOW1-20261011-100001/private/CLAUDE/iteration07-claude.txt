# Claude, failed-trade study WINDOW1, iteration 7: self-challenge

This round I re-tested my iteration-6 conclusions against the I6 query results, the code, and Binance's public market data. Most findings held, but three did not survive unchanged:

- **AERO part of F-30 retracted:** my AERO "missed stop" was a mistake on my part. I compared an early price with a stop that was raised later.
- **F-31 downgraded:** the "bought at the top of the range" reading of F-31 is weaker than I claimed.
- **Tick size moved to a primary factor:** it is now a primary cause for three of the six trades, not just a contributing weakness.

The UNI part of F-30, a stop crossed during the restart gap, is now confirmed against Binance's own data.

This was analysis only: no code, config, DB, git, doc or deploy writes. I didn't open Codex's private or db folders, `LIVE_EVENTS.log`, `LIVE_TEAM_STATUS.md` or Codex memory. I analysed the frozen trades **104, 101, 96, 91, 82, 78** and substituted none. This session has no Write or ExitPlanMode tool, so the plan file was not written and the findings are in this message only.

**Time basis:** UTC = the value the DB wrapper displays − 3 h; KSA = the displayed value. This was checked independently this round (SC-1). DB_REQUEST literals are in KSA on purpose.

## 1. Self-challenge results

### SC-1: the time basis is confirmed independently
*PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH*

- **Binance's own data:** Binance's public API (`/api/v3/klines`, UNIUSDT 1m) reports:
  - 2026-10-10 15:00 UTC: low **7.624**
  - 15:01 UTC: low **7.628**
- **Our DB:** it shows the same lows on the candles displayed as 18:00 and 18:01 (I5-005).
- **Arithmetic check:** the epoch 1791642904 in I3-002 works out to 14:35:04 UTC on 10-10, and the wrapper displays it as 17:35:04.
- **Conclusion:** the displayed values are KSA, and the BACKFILL candles match Binance exactly.

### SC-2: the UNI stop breach (F-30) is confirmed and better pinned down
*PROJECT EVIDENCE*

- **The stop only ratchets on adds:** an add can only raise the stop, to the add signal's stop (`WalletAutoExecutionService.java:151-154`).
- **UNI's last add was before the outage:** wallet position 109 shows `last_scale_in_at` 17:45:06 KSA. Its stop moved from the entry signal's 7.62568 to **7.628665** then, and stayed there through the outage. Profit lock was inactive.
- **The breach:** Binance shows lows of 7.624 at 15:00 UTC and 7.628 at 15:01 UTC (18:00 and 18:01 KSA). Both are below the stop and both fall inside the gap in price events, 17:59:05–18:02:25 KSA (I6-002).
- **The late exit:** the next live price at or below the stop came at 18:15 KSA (low 7.627), and the exit executed at 18:15:47 KSA.
- **Financial effect:** negligible this time. Price recovered to 7.668 by 18:04.
- **Status: CONFIRMED.** During a restart, protection does not run, and backfilled lows are never checked against open stops. This is a real occurrence of the `AGENTS.md` release gate on protecting open positions during application replacement. UNI is outside the frozen set, so this does not change any trade verdict.

### SC-3: the AERO half of F-30 is retracted
*PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE*

- **My mistake:** in iteration 6 I compared the 17:57–17:58 KSA lows (0.9071 and 0.9081) with the stop **0.908416**.
- **Why it's wrong:** wallet position 110 shows that 0.908416 is the stop after the ratchet from the TREND_ADD at **18:10:07 KSA**. Before that add, the active stop was the entry signal's **0.903046**.
- **No live price crossed the active stop:**
  - lowest live price before the add: 0.9056, at 18:02 KSA;
  - lowest live price after the add: 0.9141;
  - the position then closed through profit lock at 18:13:04 KSA.
- **Nothing was missed:** the stop was never breached at any point.
- **The wick-sampling idea:** that protection misses short wicks between price updates has no support from this case, and remains unproven in general. Price events arrived 14–24 per minute (roughly every 2.5–4 s).
- **Not yet verified:** that the add signal's stop was exactly 0.908416. I7-002 checks it.

### SC-4: new F-34, stop changes on an add leave no audit record
*PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE*

- **The gap:** the ratchet at `WalletAutoExecutionService.java:151-157` silently changes `stop_loss_usdt` and `take_profit_usdt`. It writes no `position_management_event` row.
- **What I don't know yet:** I6-004 came back as an empty file, so I can't tell "no rows" from "query failed". I7-001 re-asks.
- **Why it matters:** the error in SC-3 came directly from this missing history. Anyone reconstructing a trade sees only the final stop, not the stop that was active at a given moment.
- **Classification:** an observability defect candidate. It did not cause any loss.

### SC-5: F-31 ("entered at the top of the range") is downgraded, and tick size is promoted
*PROJECT EVIDENCE: I6-003. Interpretation: AGENT INFERENCE.*

Where each entry sat in the 30 minutes of closed 1m candles before it:

| Trade | 30m low–high | Entry | Position in range | Range width in ticks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 78 PEPE | 4.02–4.06e-6 | 4.06e-6 | 100% | 4 |
| 91 SHIB | 5.47–5.48e-6 | 5.48e-6 | 100% | **1** |
| 96 PEPE | 4.04–4.06e-6 | 4.06e-6 | 100% | 2 |
| 82 XLM | 0.1977–0.1987 | 0.1984 | 70% | 10 |
| 101 AERO | 0.9349–0.9517 | 0.9488 avg (first fill 0.9504) | 83% (92%) | 168 |
| 104 ENA | 0.2228–0.2251 | 0.2248 | 87% | 23 |

- **Why F-31 is weaker:** buying near the 30-minute high is normal for a TREND_FOLLOWING entry. For 91 the whole range was a single tick, so "top of the range" means nothing there. F-31 shrinks to one point: an hourly RANGE_MEAN_REVERSION SELL is treated as a neutral signal. That point is already part of RC-1.
- **New F-35: tick size is a primary factor for 78, 91 and 96.**
  - **Coarse ticks:** all three are PEPE or SHIB, where one tick is 0.18–0.25% of the price.
  - **ATR is smaller than one tick:** SHIB's ATR of 0.09% is about half a tick. An ATR measured on prices that can only move in whole ticks says little about volatility, yet the stop and target are built from it.
  - **Fees take a whole tick:** a 0.2% round-trip fee is roughly one full tick.
  - **Effect:** the stop/target distances of 1.5–3.4 ticks (I5) are close to coin flips on single price moves. This upgrades F-26 from a contributing weakness to a primary factor for these three trades. (The tick sizes are from DB price spacing, AGENT INFERENCE; I didn't fetch `exchangeInfo`.)
