## 2. FINDINGS (iteration 1, provisional)

**F-01. The selection leaves out every stop-loss loss.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- DBQ-FTS-002 shows `STOPPED` with n=40, and all 40 have negative P&L.
- `CLOSED` has n=68, of which 19 have negative P&L.
- `closePaper(..., PositionStatus.STOPPED, "STOP_LOSS", ...)` is at `LivePositionProtectionService.java:178`.
- The `status='CLOSED'` filter therefore studies only signal, HTF and profit-lock losses. Most losing trades in Production (40 of 59 negative rows) are outside this study.
- This is a scope limitation for the Coordinator to note. It is not a defect, and I am not substituting trades.

**F-02. Four of six trades closed HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED within the same UTC hour they opened.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE for the times; AGENT INFERENCE for the mechanism)
- The trades are 104, 91, 96 and 78. 104 and 91 closed about 5 minutes after entry.
- None of these holds crosses a 1h candle boundary. So no new 1h signal could normally appear between entry and exit, unless 1h analysis was delayed.
- `HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED` requires the latest 1h signal to be SELL or STRONG_SELL, and the 5m signal to be not supportive (`PositionExitPolicy.java:25-32`).
- The inference is that the same 1h SELL was very likely already the latest 1h signal when each position opened.

**F-03. Candidate mechanism (AGENT INFERENCE, not yet proven): entry and exit read the same 1h SELL differently.**
- **Entry side, `HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:33-34`:** a quality-valid 1h `SELL`, or a `STRONG_SELL` with confidence below 70, counts as `SOFT_CONFLICT`. The BUY is allowed, the effective 1h becomes `NEUTRAL`, and size is capped at 25%.
- **Then the BALANCED profile, `TradeExecutionValidationService.java:306-308`:** 5m BUY plus effective 1h NEUTRAL gives `BALANCED_STRONG`, which the cap reduces to 25%.
- **Exit side, `LivePositionProtectionService.java:95-97,235`:** it reads the raw latest 1h signal with no freshness or "before entry" check. `PositionExitPolicy` exits as soon as 5m becomes NEUTRAL, SELL or missing.
- **Net effect:** the entry is admitted on the condition that 5m stays supportive. The first 5m close that isn't BUY/WATCH then closes the trade at a small loss, so a soft conflict at entry becomes a forced exit.
- This would be a design inconsistency between the FIX-154 entry rules and the exit rules, not a data defect.
- **Unproven parts:**
  - FIX-154 code exists in the working tree (V107, V109, `buy_htf_assessment_audit`), but AGENTS.md says it is not deployed. Whether Production ran it during these trades is unknown.
  - The same pattern could also come from the pre-FIX-154 BALANCED/AGGRESSIVE paths. For example, a 1h `NEUTRAL` at entry followed by a late 1h SELL.

**F-04. Trade 101 closed PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT at a loss.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE plus AGENT INFERENCE)
- The hard floor is `entry × 1.0005` (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:210-214`, and the rebase floor at `:191-196`).
- The exit uses the tick price that broke the floor. If that tick was below entry, a "profit lock" exit realizes a loss.
- Possible explanations, none established yet:
  - the price gapped between ticks;
  - the 50% near-TP partial harvest changed quantity or P&L (`:322-327`);
  - the lock was armed against an extended-TP geometry.
- I can't tell from the current evidence whether this was intended.

**F-05. Trades 96 and 78 (both PEPEUSDT) have identical `realized_pnl` of -0.061576354680.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- An exact match to 12 decimals suggests identical quantity × price-tick difference (PEPE tick granularity), or a fixed-size pattern.
- This needs `quantity`, `entry_price` and `exit_price` to explain. It may be harmless.

**F-06. Trade 82 is the only SELL_CONFIRMED loss.**
- It held 82 minutes and needed 1m SELL, 5m SELL and a non-bullish 1h (`PositionExitPolicy.java:35-43`).
- This looks like an ordinary signal exit. I haven't assessed it yet.

**F-07. Fees aren't in the P&L.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE from `claude-docs` and the `WalletAutoExecutionService` review)
- Automatic trades record `feeUsdt = 0`.
- All six P&L values are gross, so net losses are larger. I'll compute fees once `wallet_trade` comes back.
