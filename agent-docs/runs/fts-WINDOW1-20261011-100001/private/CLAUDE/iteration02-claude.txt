## 2. FINDINGS

**F-08 (CONFIRMED for 96 and 104; strongly supported for 78 and 91). The same 1h signal is read one way at entry and the opposite way at exit.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE, with AGENT INFERENCE on 78/91)

- **Entry side.** FIX-154 treats a quality-valid 1h SELL (confidence about 70) as `SOFT_CONFLICT` and turns the effective 1h into NEUTRAL (audits 1362 and 1558). BALANCED then admits 5m BUY plus 1h NEUTRAL.
- **Exit side.** `PositionExitPolicy.evaluateNormalExit` (`PositionExitPolicy.java:25-32`) uses the **raw** latest 1h. It exits as soon as 5m is no longer BUY/STRONG_BUY/WATCH.
- **No timing check.** Nothing tests whether that 1h came before the entry. The class Javadoc (line 14) says "a **fresh** 1h SELL may close a position", and the exit text says "1h **turned** SELL". In all four trades the 1h SELL existed 28–40 minutes before entry, so neither statement matches the implementation.
- **Result.** Each position lived exactly until the first 5m close that wasn't BUY/WATCH (1, 4, 4 and 1 bars). The exits fired 5–26 seconds after that 5m signal.
- **Classification.** This is a design inconsistency (a logic-defect candidate), not stale data. Every input was fresh (`data_quality_valid=1`, LIVE mode, ages within the limits).

**F-09. Trades 78, 91 and 104 match the `SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP` route.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE for the code; AGENT INFERENCE for the attribution)

- `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:397-457` fires when a fresh 5m BUY follows a non-bullish 5m, while an unexecuted opportunity is building (104's audit 1559 says OPPORTUNITY_BUILDING).
- It validates with `validateBuyContext(fiveMinuteTrigger, "SETUP_WAKEUP")`. Its audit row would therefore be keyed to the **5m** signal ID, which explains why I1-007 found no rows for the 1m IDs of 78 and 91.
- The size is `min(25, …)`.
- In all three trades the 5m BUY landed 0.04–0.9 s after the 1m signal, and entry followed about 3 s later.
- **Pattern:** entry authority hinges on one 5m WATCH/NEUTRAL→BUY flip and exit on the next BUY→NEUTRAL flip, while the 1h SELL stays constant. This is a whipsaw by construction.
- **To verify:** I2-001 and I2-002.

**F-10. Every loss is 1–2 price ticks; for PEPE and SHIB one tick is about one ATR or more.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE plus arithmetic)

| Coin | One tick | ATR |
|---|---|---|
| PEPE | 1e-8 = 0.246% of 4.06e-6 | 0.22–0.29% |
| SHIB | 0.18% of 5.48e-6 | 0.09% |

- I found no tick-size handling (`tickSize|PRICE_FILTER` returns nothing in `src/main/java`).
- 91's TP is 1.5 ticks away; 96's TP is 2.7 ticks and its SL 2.0 ticks.
- Any exit after zero net move realises at least one tick of loss. The ATR geometry runs below the price resolution.

**F-11. Entries with risk/reward below 1 are admitted.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- `AtrRiskService.java:34-45` sets stop = max(2·ATR, 0.50% of price). The 0.50% comes from `application.yml:143`. Target stays at 3·ATR.
- With low ATR the stop is floored at 0.5% and RR collapses: 82 has RR 0.453 (I1-002), 91 has 0.546, 104 has 1.09.
- RR appears only in display and inspector code (grep of `getRiskRewardRatio()`). No gate references it.
- Trade 78 also filled one tick above the signal price (fill 4.06e-6 vs `latest_price` 4.05e-6), while SL/TP stayed anchored to the signal price. Its RR at the fill was about 0.76 instead of 1.5.

**F-12. Fees are excluded from P&L.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `wallet_trade.fee_usdt` defaults to 0 per I1-005, plus `claude-docs` TRADING_RULES §6; AGENT INFERENCE on the rate)

- Implied notional (qty × entry): 78/82/91/96 = 25.00 USDT each, 104 = 15.00, 101 = 50.00. Total 165 USDT.
- Total gross loss is −0.2624 USDT.
- At an assumed 0.1% per side, fees would be about 0.33 USDT, more than the gross loss. 82's TP distance (0.226%) and 91's (0.273%) are barely above a 0.2% round trip.
- The 0.1% rate is general knowledge I did not fetch this iteration; it's marked to verify.

**F-05 resolved. 96 and 78 have identical P&L because their trades are identical.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
Both have qty 6157635.468 = 25 USDT / 4.06e-6, the same entry and exit price, and a one-tick loss of 0.0615764. There's no defect here.

**F-13. Trade 101 is a TP extension followed by a fast reversal through the thin rebase floor.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE plus AGENT INFERENCE)

- Paper TP 0.963551424473 equals the signal TP 0.958630559740 plus 50% of the TP-minus-average-entry distance, exactly. So the TP was extended once (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:107-128`), which means a price event reached at least 0.95863.
- About a minute later price was 0.9487, below the hard floor of entry × 1.0005 = 0.94926322 (`:191-198`). The exit ran at the tick price below entry, giving −0.0047 USDT.
- During the FIX-118 rebase the earlier lock is non-executable, so protection fell from roughly +1% open profit to the +0.05% floor. A single price-event gap then crossed it.
- The average entry (0.94879) is below the signal price (0.9505), and notional is 50 USDT, which points to more than one fill. That needs I2-002/003.
- 101's HTF "bullish" support was a 1h BUY with `confidence=49` and `final_entry_allowed=0`, a blocked signal (audit 1523: "1h is bullish"). Whether a blocked 1h BUY should count as authority is still open (AGENT INFERENCE).

**F-14. Trade 82 looks like a valid losing trade, but its entry used one-hour-old context because both hour-boundary signals are missing.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE plus AGENT INFERENCE)

- I1-003 shows no XLM 1h signal for candle 17:00 (expected about 18:00:0x) and no 5m signal for candle 17:55.
- Audit 1132 therefore used the 1h at age 3782 s and the 5m at age 482 s. Both are inside the limits (≤3 h, ≤20 min), so this follows the rules.
- The exit followed the rules: 1m STRONG_SELL plus 5m SELL plus 1h WATCH. Earlier 1m STRONG_SELLs (19:10, 19:16, 19:24) were correctly held because 5m was NEUTRAL.
- Price never reached TP 0.198849 (max 1m 0.1986). RR at entry was 0.45 (F-11).
- Whether the 18:00 gap is a data defect is unknown; I2-007 and I2-008 check it.

**F-15 (sizing, open question). The FIX-154 "cap 25%" does not show up in notional.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
96 (SOFT_CONFLICT, cap 25%) and 82 (BALANCED_STRONG 75%, no cap) both spent 25 USDT, while 104 (60% REDUCED_POSITION) spent 15 USDT. Either the cap is applied to a different base, or it isn't binding. I2-003 and I2-002 will tell.

**F-01 (carried).** The `status='CLOSED'` selection leaves out all 40 STOPPED losses. This remains a scope note, not a defect.

## 3. Self-review: what would falsify these findings
- **F-08:** a 1h signal newer than entry with `generated_at > closed_at + 1 min` couldn't have been read at exit, so the I1-003 absence proof holds. The remaining risk is that the Production JAR's `PositionExitPolicy` differs from the working tree. The audit `final_reason` text matches the working-tree FIX-154 strings, which supports the same code.
- **F-09:** proven only if an audit row with `evaluation_path='SETUP_WAKEUP'` exists on 5m 115050, 121201 and 133251, and `wallet_trade.execution_reason` matches.
- **I1-007 interpretation:** `buy_htf_assessment_audit.evaluated_at` equals the signal's `generated_at`, not wall-clock time; `created_at` runs about 1.4 s later.
