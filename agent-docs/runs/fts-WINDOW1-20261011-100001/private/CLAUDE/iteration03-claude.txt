## 0. Contradiction with the documented time rule (must be reported)

**Rule being contradicted:** `DATABASE_INVESTIGATION_RULES.md:35` and the charter say raw DB timestamps are UTC.

**PROJECT EVIDENCE that the wrapper output is KSA:**
1. **The wrapper's session isn't UTC.**

## 1. FINDINGS

**F-16 (CONFIRMED in the live log; frozen-trade link is inference): the app's clock runs about 3.1 s behind Binance, and it jumped there during the study window.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE plus AGENT INFERENCE)

*What the log shows:*
- From 06:35 to 07:19 UTC on 10-11, `ExchangeClockSynchronizer` logs `[FIX-153][CLOCK_SKEW] exchangeMinusJvmMs` between 3106 and 3159 ms (45 WARN lines, e.g. `crypto-ai-trader.log:86`). It drifts up by about 0.6 ms/min.

*What `market_price_event.created_at − observed_at` shows (I2-005):*

| Trade | Window (UTC) | created − observed |
|---|---|---|
| 78 | 14:48 | −0.22…+0.24 s |
| 82 | 16:24 | −0.02…+0.19 s |
| 91 | 18:44 | +0.04…+0.19 s |
| 96 | 19:44 | +0.03…+0.12 s |
| 101 | 23:17 | ≈ −0.02 s |
| **104** | **02:38–02:40** | **−2.86…−2.94 s** |

*Interpretation:*
- That is a step of about 2.8 s between 23:21 and 02:38 UTC. Drift alone (about 36 ms/h) can't explain it.
- 104's opportunity and audit timestamps fall just before minute boundaries (05:30:59.900, 05:33:59.971 KSA), which is consistent with the skew already being present at entry.
- Effect on the frozen trades: nothing that changed a decision.
  - Price and signal freshness use the corrected clock (`MarketPriceEventService.java:39`, `SignalProcessingFreshness.java:28`).
  - Signals are ordered by `generated_at` on a single clock.
  - Trade 104's stored times are about 2.9 s earlier than exchange time. For example, its entry price was really about 3.8 s old at the wallet fill, not the 0.87 s the raw values suggest. That is still within the 15 s limit.

*Latent risk (code fact plus inference):*
- `price-future-skew-seconds` = 2 s (`MarketPriceEventService.java:32,43`), which is smaller than the 3.1 s skew.
- If the exchange-clock observation goes stale for more than 120 s (`ExchangeClockSynchronizer.java:64-70`), every live price would be rejected as `FUTURE_TIMESTAMP`. That would blind TP/SL protection until the sync recovers.
- I saw no rejections in the frozen-trade windows: every I2-005 row is APPLIED. I3-004 checks the whole period.

*Monitoring defect (PROJECT EVIDENCE):*
- `[FIX-153][SIGNAL_SAVED] liveCloseToGenerationMs` uses the uncorrected generation time, so it under-reports latency by about 3.1 s and can go **negative**. Example: `crypto-ai-trader.log:20040`, `-1439`.
- Stored `trade_signal.generated_at` can therefore be earlier than the candle's close time.

**F-17 (CONFIRMED in the live log; attribution inside the window is inference): each mechanical exit holds the shared WebSocket thread and the wallet locks for about 1.3 s.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE plus AGENT INFERENCE)

*Log evidence (10-11, after the study window, same runtime):*
- Two AERO exits ran on `WebSocketClient-SecureIO-1` with `[FIX-125][TX_COMPLETED] SYMBOL holdMs=1308/1316` and `MUTATION(WALLET) holdMs=1301/1305`. `KlineReceipt CALLBACK_FINISHED` was 1320 and 1325 ms (lines 15244–15248 and 20058–20062).
- Other symbols' price events wait during that time and then run in a burst within 40 ms (lines 15249–15254).

*Frozen trades:*
- The gap from `wallet_trade.executed_at` to `paper_position.closed_at` was 0.77 s (78), 1.76 s (82), 1.32 s (91), 1.41 s (96), 1.46 s (101) and 1.53 s (104).
- In code, `executed_at` is set at `WalletAutoExecutionService.java:548`. Then a daily-stats portfolio valuation runs (`:553-559` → `WalletService.currentPortfolioValue:484-490`, one candle query per asset). Then `captureSnapshot` runs (`:560` → `WalletService:592-607`, a second per-asset candle query plus aggregates). Then `closePaper` sets `closed_at` (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:405`).
- Entries show the same 1.2–1.9 s gap from wallet fill to paper open, on the analysis thread.

*Impact:*
- None on the frozen trades' P&L, because the exit price is fixed before the stall.
- While the stall lasts, protection for every other symbol can be delayed by up to about 1.3 s. This is low severity and latent.
- Attributing the 1.3 s to the valuation and snapshot work is inference; I have no DB timing log for the study window.
