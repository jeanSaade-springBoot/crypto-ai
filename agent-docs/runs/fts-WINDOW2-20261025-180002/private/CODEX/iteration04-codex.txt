## FINDINGS

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** No permitted DB-result artifacts exist in `db/CODEX`; the six frozen positions therefore cannot yet be assigned an entry, exit route, or defect conclusion.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** Current source has two materially different exit routes:
  - Live-price protection: `market_price_event` → `LivePositionProtectionService` → wallet mechanical exit → `paper_position` close → `production_exit_audit`.
  - Signal processing: durable `signal_processing_work` → `PaperTradingService` → paper close plus signal-linked wallet exit/audit.
  
  `paper_position.close_reason` alone is insufficient to distinguish the actual path; `production_exit_audit`, wallet records, and price-event evidence are required.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** Entry order is wallet-first. `PaperTradingService` obtains a fresh canonical price, revalidates the BUY, writes the wallet BUY, then creates `paper_position` using that fresh execution price and executed quantity. The entry signal’s `latest_price` is decision evidence, not necessarily the fill price.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** For live protection, TP and SL are checked before profit-lock, normal confirmed exits, and near-TP handling. Mechanical exits are wallet-first and use the observed live price. A price event is marked `APPLIED` only in the effects transaction after the protection callback completes; `APPLIED` can still be a no-position/no-op callback.

- **AGENT INFERENCE — candidate temporal-integrity issue, not trade-specific proof:** `LivePositionProtectionService.onPrice(symbol, price, observedAt, eventId)` validates the event’s `observedAt`, but subsequently reads the newest 1m/5m/1h signals without constraining `generated_at <= observedAt`. Thus, if protection processing is delayed but still within the 15-second price-age limit, a profit-lock or signal-driven exit may use a signal generated after the actual price observation. Near-TP logic has a limited “not future relative to evaluation time” guard, but not an observation-time guard. This requires DB evidence before attributing it to any frozen trade.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** Paper and wallet P&L are gross-price calculations in the reviewed routes; paper close computes `(exit - entry) × quantity`, while automatic wallet trades persist `fee_usdt = 0`. The six losses are valid as negative paper P&L by the frozen selection, but their fee-adjusted economic loss cannot be inferred from current source.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** The supplied snapshot values remain inconsistent: `2026-10-25 15:00:04 UTC` converts to `2026-10-25 18:00:04 KSA`, while the supplied KSA snapshot is `18:00:02`. This has no demonstrated impact on a frozen trade.

### DB_REQUEST

QueryId: DBQ-CODEX-I4-003  
Purpose: After DBQ-CODEX-I4-001 confirms the code-mapped columns, reconstruct the durable wallet, audit, signal, and live-price route for only the six frozen positions.  
SQL:
```sql
SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    a.*
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.production_exit_audit a
    ON a.paper_position_id = p.id
WHERE p.id IN (182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137)
ORDER BY p.id, a.audited_at;

SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    wmp.*
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_managed_position wmp
    ON wmp.entry_signal_id = p.signal_id
WHERE p.id IN (182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137)
ORDER BY p.id, wmp.opened_at;

SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    wt.*
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_trade wt
    ON wt.signal_id = p.signal_id
    OR wt.signal_id = p.exit_signal_id
WHERE p.id IN (182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137)
ORDER BY p.id, wt.executed_at, wt.id;

SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    mpe.*
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
JOIN crypto_ai.market_price_event mpe
    ON mpe.symbol = p.symbol
   AND mpe.observed_at >= p.opened_at
   AND mpe.observed_at <= p.closed_at
WHERE p.id IN (182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137)
ORDER BY p.id, mpe.observed_at, mpe.id;
```
