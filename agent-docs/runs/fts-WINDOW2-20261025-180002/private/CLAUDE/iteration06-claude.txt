# CLAUDE: FTS WINDOW2, Iteration 6: Root cause and research

**Run:** `fts-WINDOW2-20261025-180002`. **Frozen trades:** 182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137, none substituted. This was analysis only: I made no code, config or DB writes, ran no git commands and deployed nothing. I did not open the other agent's files, `LIVE_TEAM_STATUS.md` or `LIVE_EVENTS.log`.

**Time rule:** the database stores UTC, but our query session displays it in KSA (F-I4-00). Values copied from DB results below are KSA; UTC = KSA − 3 h.

## Summary

- **Two trades lost because of how the system reads its own 5m/1h context.**
  - **182:** the 5m SELL was hidden, so nothing vetoed the BUY.
  - **143:** a race at the 1m/5m candle boundary let the BUY use a 5m bar that had already been replaced.
- **Four trades were valid losses under the current rules,** each with a structural weakness:
  - 162: an Entry Quality score cut-off that falls on a cliff edge.
  - 158: a target smaller than round-trip fees.
  - 172: a coin whose price step is larger than its ATR (its typical candle size).
  - 137: a long placed under a known rejection high.
- **The Entry Quality score (EQ) does not predict results across all trades.** EXCELLENT entries won 0 of 3 (I5-003), so EQ cannot be the fix.
- **New: Replay would not reproduce trade 143's entry.** Replay processes candles at a shared boundary in a fixed order, 1h → 5m → 1m, so the 1m sees the new 5m. Production's order depends on thread timing.
- **New: FIX-154 was very likely live when these trades ran.** This contradicts AGENTS.md, which calls it "not yet implemented".

## FINDINGS

**F-I6-01: Root cause of 182. One 1h trend score on a cliff edge both hid the 5m SELL and authorised the BUY. Logic defect. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)**
- **What happened:**
  - In the 30 minutes before entry, the 1m ran STRONG_SELL from 09:39 to 09:54 KSA, then flipped to BUY at 09:56 (I5-001).
  - The 5m was originally SELL or STRONG_SELL on all 12 bars, but every one was stored as NEUTRAL (F-I4-01).
  - `HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:20-22` vetoes only on `getDecision()`, not on the original decision, so the bearish 5m never reached it.
  - FIX-11G then admitted the trade at EQ 50.
- **Why it exited:** the 1h trend score fell from 16 to 13, below the bullish threshold of 15. The 5m was no longer masked, and that produced SELL_CONFIRMED.
- **So entry and exit were the same threshold flipping, not a change in the market.**
- **EQ arithmetic (AGENT INFERENCE):**
  - R:R 0.48 implies ATR ≈ 0.08% of price ≈ 0.000151.
  - The 30-minute low was 0.1875 and the signal price was 0.1884, which puts price about 5.96 ATRs above the low. That costs −20, within 0.04 ATR of the −30 band.
  - EQ = 100 − 30 − 20 = 50, matching the recorded value. One more price step would have given EQ 40 and a block.
- **Population (I5-004):** 9 closed positions entered on a masked-bearish 5m.
  - 5 wins, each between +0.03 and +0.12.
  - 4 losses (182, 181, 37, 23). Three of them were full STOPPED losses: −0.584, −0.272, −0.145.
  - Net about −0.703. The sample is small, but the pattern is asymmetric.
- **Verdict:** confirmed logic defect in the working tree. Deployment at trade time is strongly supported by F-I6-05 and is being proven by I6-001.

**F-I6-02: Root cause of 143. A boundary race plus a chase that the route overrode; Replay would not reproduce the entry. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)**
- **The chase is now confirmed with numbers (closes U-I5-01/02):**
  - The 30-minute low was 3.972 at 22:33 KSA, and the fill was 4.033: that is **+1.54% expansion**, inside the 1.5–3% band worth −10.
  - Using the 5m ATR of 0.009073 (I5-002), price was **(4.033 − 3.972) / 0.009073 = 6.72 ATRs** above the low, beyond the >6 band worth −30.
  - EQ = 100 − 30 − 10 = 60, which reproduces the recorded value.
  - The price had moved from 3.972 to 4.032 in 6 bars, up 0.78% in the last 2 minutes alone.
- **Replay ordering (closes U-I4-03):**
  - Replay sorts candles by close time, then by interval, 1h → 5m → 1m (`RegressionTestWorker.java:557-560`, `intervalOrder` at `:787-793`).
  - It sets `evaluationTime` to the candle close (`:584`), and `analyzeForRegression` stamps the signal with that time (`AnalysisService.java:190`).
  - So the same-boundary 5m has `generatedAt == evaluationTime`. It passes the `!isAfter` filter and is visible to the 1m.
  - In Replay, the 1m 159408 would therefore see the 5m WATCH (159407), and SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR would return null. **Production entered; Replay would not.** This is inferred from the code and still needs a Replay run (I6-004).
  - Production stamps signals with `Instant.now()` when analysis starts (`AnalysisService.java:150`), so its ordering depends on which thread wins.
- **How common the race is (I5-006):** it admitted 6 entries.
  - 3 were small profit-lock wins (72, 119, 255).
  - 305 (PEPE) was a full stop at −0.698, and 358 is trade 143 at −0.037.
  - 199 was an add-on with no separate position.
  - Net about −0.60, but n is too small to size the effect.
- **Verdict:** confirmed race and a confirmed Replay/Production ordering mismatch (a parity defect). It is decisive for 143. The exit was a separate single-tick gap (F-I3-03).

**F-I6-03: EQ is not a reliable filter, so raising its bar is not the fix. (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I5-003)**

| EQ class | Wins / trades | Net PnL |
|---|---|---|
| EXCELLENT | 0 / 3 | −0.968 |
| GOOD | 8 / 16 | −0.402 |
| ACCEPTABLE | 24 / 43 | +0.758 |
| LATE | 17 / 29 | +0.224 |

- **Inside FIX-11G:** n = 35, 18 wins / 16 losses, net about −0.288.
  - The EQ 50 bucket was *positive*: 8 wins / 6 losses, +0.277.
  - EQ 85–90 lost: 0 / 3, −0.829.
- **Effect on my iteration 5 finding:** F-I5-02's threshold inconsistency is real in the code and decisive for 182. But the population does not show the loose EQ 50 bar is what costs money, so I am **downgrading F-I5-02 from root cause to a contributing factor**.
- **Most likely reason:** F-I5-01. A third of the EQ range is a volatility term, which drowns out the location signal. This is inference.
- **Weakest route:** SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP had the worst win rate, 15 wins / 41 trades, net −0.905. This is trade 158's route.

**F-I6-04: Fees. Trade 158's 0.17% target cannot cover a standard Binance round trip. (EXTERNAL RESEARCH + PROJECT EVIDENCE)**
- **External:** secondary sources agree that a regular (VIP 0) Binance spot account pays 0.10% maker and taker, or 0.075% when paying in BNB. That makes a round trip 0.15–0.20%. I did not reach Binance's official fee page, so this is not authoritative.
- **Effect:** 158's 0.17% TP would have been at about breakeven or a loss after fees even if it hit. Any ATR plan with an ATR% below roughly 0.067% puts the TP under fees.
- **Unknown:** whether the paper model charges fees at all (I6-002).

**F-I6-05: Contradiction with AGENTS.md. FIX-154 appears to have been deployed before every frozen trade. (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I5-005 + code)**

- **Startup check:** `Fix154RuntimeSchemaChecks.java:21-23` refuses to start the application without `buy_htf_assessment_audit`. Flyway also runs from the application jar, so a jar containing FIX-154 started on 10-10.
- **Later jars:** V110 and V111 (10-15, 10-17) show later code was deployed after the trades too. So the working tree is newer than the code that ran them, and U-I4-01 stays open for any lines changed after 10-12.
- I am reporting this contradiction only; I have not edited AGENTS.md.

**F-I6-06: The other entry inputs are confirmed from the 30-minute windows. (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I5-001, I5-002)**
