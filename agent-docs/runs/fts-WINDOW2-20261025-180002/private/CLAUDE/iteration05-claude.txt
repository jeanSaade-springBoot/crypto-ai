## Summary

  - Three were poor or chases: **182, 162, 143**.
  - Two were marginal: **172, 158**.
  - One had a clean signal but a poor price location: **137**.
- The system's Entry Quality score (EQ) did not separate them well:
  - **EQ penalises volatility, not location (F-I5-01).** At the signal price its risk/reward input depends only on ATR% (the coin's volatility). So low-volatility coins lose 30 points automatically, whatever the entry location.
  - **The most extended entry scored joint-highest (F-I5-03).** Trade 143 scored 60 (ACCEPTABLE), tied with 172, while trades with milder price-location problems scored 50–58.

- **Trade 162 cleared the bar by 24 ms (F-I5-04).** It reached EQ 50 only because the opportunity age (20 min 59.976 s) was rounded down to 20 minutes.

## FINDINGS

**F-I5-01: EQ's risk/reward term measures volatility, not entry location. It cost 5 of the 6 trades 30 points. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)**
- **How R:R is computed:**
  - `currentRewardRisk` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1811-1817`) uses the signal's own stop and TP.
  - `AtrRiskService.java:40-45` sets stop = max(2·ATR, 0.5%·price) and TP = 3·ATR.
  - So at the signal price, R:R depends only on ATR%: 1.5 when ATR% ≥ 0.25%, and below 1 when ATR% < 0.1667%.
- **The penalty:** `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:991-993` takes 30 points off when R:R < 1, and 15 when it is below 1.25. The +5 bonus needs R:R ≥ 2, which this formula can never produce.
- **The effect on our trades:**
  - 182 (0.48), 172 (0.74), 162 (0.44), 158 (0.33) and 137 (0.64) all lost 30 points. Each started from a maximum of 70.
  - 143 (ATR 0.27%, R:R 1.50) lost nothing.
- **The penalty points the wrong way across all trades.** F-I3-07 found the R:R < 0.75 bucket at 34 wins / 20 losses (+0.234), against 21 / 42 (−4.130) for R:R ≥ 1.5.
- **Verdict:** design defect candidate. EQ is documented as "deliberately independent from signal quality" (`:845-846`), but a third of its range is a volatility term. DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-003 checks the outcome relationship.

**F-I5-02: the FIX-11G route has the weakest higher-timeframe authority but the loosest EQ bar. (code + PROJECT EVIDENCE)**

| Route | Higher-timeframe context | EQ requirement |
|---|---|---|
| FIX-112B `BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M` (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:234-236`) | 5m NEUTRAL, 1h bullish | EQ ≥ 70 and confidence ≥ 72 |
| FIX-041 `BALANCED_EARLY` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:672-684`) | 5m WATCH, 1h WATCH | LATE_ENTRY (50–54) is blocked |
| FIX-11G `BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M_WATCH_1H` (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:263-284`, `:49`) | 5m NEUTRAL, 1h WATCH (weaker than both above) | EQ ≥ 50, so LATE_ENTRY is accepted; no confidence floor |

- **Trade 182:** admitted with `entryQuality=50` (wallet 451, opportunity 40290).
  - Under the BALANCED_EARLY rule it would have been held as `BALANCED_EARLY_LATE_ENTRY_BLOCKED`.
  - Under the FIX-112B bar it would have been rejected (EQ 50 < 70; confidence 68 < 72).
- **Trade 137:** admitted with EQ 58 (ACCEPTABLE), which BALANCED_EARLY would also allow, so the gap made no difference there.
- On 182 this adds to F-I4-01: the "5m NEUTRAL" that qualified the route was a SELL/STRONG_SELL neutralised by confluence (masked).
- **Verdict:** inconsistent thresholds across routes (logic defect candidate). It is decisive for 182 only.

**F-I5-03: trade 143 was a chase that EQ itself measured, yet it was rated ACCEPTABLE. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code + AGENT INFERENCE)**
- **Recorded EQ:** 60 (opportunity 36524, wallet 358), with R:R 1.50 and therefore no R:R penalty.
- **How the 40 lost points break down.** Within the coded penalty set (`:978-1015`) the only combination worth 40 points is:
  - ATR extension above 6 (−30), using the **5m** ATR (`:634`), plus
  - expansion of 1.5–3% from the 30-minute reference (−10).
  - Rejection is ruled out: its floor would give 50. Age is 0, the opportunity being newly created. Stop-exposure does not apply (BULLISH_SUPPORT).
  - Conclusion: price was more than 6 setup ATRs and 1.5–3% above its 30-minute base. Confirmation is pending from DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-001/002.
- **What else was known at entry:**
  - The 1m signal said `WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, with 0% recommended size and "retrace near 4.01180". The fill at 4.033 was **0.52% above the system's own retracement level**.
  - RSI was about 84, and price was 4.35 ATR from SMA20.
  - The previous EGLD position had exited 22 s earlier at 3.996.
  - Funding was at the 100th percentile.
- **The stated reason for the override did not apply.** `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR` replaces the 1m ATR so that "a tiny 1m ATR cannot manufacture a false late-stage chase" (`:1510-1511`). But 143's 1m ATR (0.27%) was the *largest* of all six trades.
- The authority 5m BUY was the stale bar from the 2 ms race (F-I4-02).
- **Verdict:** poor entry. It was admitted by a route whose override bypassed the 1m retracement instruction, and EQ scored it above trades that were genuinely less extended because of F-I5-01.

**F-I5-04: trade 162 reached EQ 50 only because its opportunity age was rounded down by 24 ms. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)**
- **Inputs:**
  - Opportunity 37952 had `started_at` 04:02 −21 min = 03:41:02.836972 KSA. The signal 167668 was generated at 04:02:02.813094 KSA.
  - The gap is 20 min 59.976 s. `Duration.toMinutes()` gives 20, so the age penalty did not fire (`:987-989`; it needs more than 20).
- **Score arithmetic:**
  - Reference = best_entry 0.08675. ATR extension = (0.08705 − 0.08675) / 0.0000636 = **4.72** (−20). R:R 0.44 (−30). Total **50**, which matches the recorded "Entry Quality 50/100 (LATE_ENTRY)".
  - 24 ms later the age would have been 21 min (−5), giving EQ 45, which is a CHASE_ENTRY block (`:1173-1180`).
- **The FIX-119 late-entry guard did not apply.** It is skipped when there is any accumulated BUY (`:1275-1279`), and the evidence contained **1 BUY / 9 WATCH**.
- **Other context known at entry:**
  - The 1m signal was WATCH in the BREAKOUT regime.
  - The candle range was 2.82 ATR, overextended, with REDUCED_POSITION.
  - 5m and 1h were both WATCH (BALANCED_EARLY context via `validateBuyContext`, which has no LATE_ENTRY block, unlike the direct BUY path).
- **Two side effects:**
  - The exact reproduction suggests the deployed EQ formula matches the working tree for these terms. That partly addresses U-I4-01 for EQ only.
  - Whole-minute age buckets create cliff edges at the chase cut-off.
- **Verdict:** the entry followed the coded rules. It was a marginal pass, and its quality was poor.

**F-I5-08: decision-time EQ and execution-time EQ use different ATRs, and the execution-time EQ is never stored. (code)**
- The setup and wake-up routes score EQ with the 5m ATR (`:364, :439, :615, :634`). The FIX-056 re-check at execution price uses `current.getAtrAtSignal()`, the 1m ATR (`:857-858`).
- Re-check results are not persisted. They change nothing when the cap does not bind, and the EQ components (reference, expansion, ATR extension, age, rejection) are never stored anywhere.
- Replay calls the same method (`ShadowProductionReplayService.java:300, 335`), so the logic is shared. But without stored components, a Production EQ cannot be audited against Replay without re-running it.
- **Verdict:** an observability gap, plus an ATR inconsistency. No outcome effect is proven in this sample.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-003
Purpose: U-I5-03. Outcome of all closed positions by entry route and by EQ parsed from the initial BUY message.
SQL:
SELECT execution_reason, eq_class, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(realized_pnl > 0) AS wins, SUM(realized_pnl < 0) AS losses, ROUND(SUM(realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_sum
FROM (
  SELECT p.id, p.realized_pnl, w.execution_reason,
   CASE
     WHEN w.execution_message LIKE '%BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M_WATCH_1H%' THEN CONCAT('FIX11G_EQ', COALESCE(REGEXP_SUBSTR(REGEXP_SUBSTR(w.execution_message, 'entryQuality=[0-9]+'), '[0-9]+'), '?'))
     WHEN w.execution_message REGEXP 'Entry Quality [0-9]+/100' THEN REGEXP_SUBSTR(w.execution_message, '(EXCELLENT|GOOD|ACCEPTABLE|LATE)_ENTRY')
     ELSE 'NOT_PRINTED'
   END AS eq_class
  FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
  JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_trade w ON w.signal_id = p.signal_id AND w.side = 'BUY' AND w.status = 'EXECUTED'
  WHERE p.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') AND p.closed_at IS NOT NULL
) t
GROUP BY execution_reason, eq_class
ORDER BY execution_reason, eq_class
LIMIT 80;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-004
Purpose: U-I5-05 (lighter re-issue of I4-004). Positions whose latest 5m at or before the entry signal was masked bearish, with outcomes.
