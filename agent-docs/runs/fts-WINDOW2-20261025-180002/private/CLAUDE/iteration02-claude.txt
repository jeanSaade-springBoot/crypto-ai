## FINDINGS

**F-I2-01: confirmed structural defect. In 5 of 6 trades the risk/reward was below 1:1 when the trade was entered. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)**

`AtrRiskService.java:40-45` sets the stop distance to `max(2·ATR, 0.50%·price)` (`application.yml:143`, `minimum-stop-percent: 0.50`). The reward is always `3·ATR` and is never floored. So whenever ATR is below about 0.167% of price, the 0.5% stop floor wins and the reward/risk drops under 1. The DB values match this formula exactly:

| Pos | ATR% | Stop dist | TP dist | Reward/risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 182 | 0.08% | 0.50% (floor) | 0.24% | 0.48 |
| 172 | 0.12% | 0.50% (floor) | 0.37% | 0.74 |
| 162 | 0.07% | 0.50% (floor) | 0.22% | 0.44 |
| 158 | 0.05% | 0.49% (floor, from signal price 1.4109) | 0.17% | 0.35 |
| 137 | 0.11% | 0.50% (floor) | 0.32% | 0.64 |
| 143 | 0.27% | 0.54% (2 ATR) | 0.79% | 1.50 |

- I searched `src/main` for `riskRewardRatio` / `RiskReward`. The only hits are where the value is persisted (`AnalysisService.java:540`), the domain class, the dashboard and the inspector. I found no entry gate on R:R, but this was a bounded grep and iteration 3 must confirm it.
- The explanation text attached to these signals says "Stop uses 2.0 ATR and target uses 3.0 ATR". For the five floored trades that text misdescribes the stop that was actually used.

**F-I2-02: real fees would cancel most of the targets. (AGENT INFERENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH, unverified this run)**

Binance's standard spot fee is about 0.1% per side, about 0.2% round trip. Against that, the TP distance of trade 158 (0.17%) is a negative net reward, and 162 (0.22%) and 182 (0.24%) are close to zero. Because automated P&L is recorded gross, this exposure is hidden. Both F-I2-01 and F-I2-02 apply to all six frozen trades.

**F-I2-04: trade 143 went from a winner to a loss. The code allowed it. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)**

- Entry at 4.033, original TP 4.0647.
- 22:40:41 UTC (01:40:41 KSA): price 4.067 crossed TP. The continuation policy extended TP to 4.0805 (event 51), and again at 22:41:13 UTC to 4.1043 (event 52), instead of taking profit.
- Price peaked at 4.101 (+1.69%), which is 95.35% of the extended TP distance. Profit lock armed at 4.0758.
- `LivePositionProtectionService.java:207-231`: when the lock triggers, the code exits unconditionally only if price is below the hard floor (entry × 1.0005 = 4.0350). Otherwise it calls `evaluateProfitLockBreach(one, five, hour)`, and that call can choose to "protected hold".
- The exit was recorded at 4.027, which is below both the lock (4.0758) and the floor (4.0350). Exiting below the floor rather than at it suggests either a price gap between ticks or holds while price passed the floor. This is unproven until the tick sequence is pulled (I2-007).
- If it had exited at the original TP or at the lock price, the trade would have made about +0.79% or +1.06%.
- **Verdict:** pending the tick and log evidence, this looks rule-following but with a weak design (lock price is advisory, floor only at break-even). It is not a proven defect.
