## FINDINGS

**F-I3-01 (CRITICAL; affects every report): the timestamps we read from the DB are KSA, not UTC. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH)**
- Each entry fill message records the real UTC time of its price as a `Z`-suffixed Instant. These come from `PaperTradingService.java:383`, which uses `ExecutionPrice.observedAt()`, built from Binance event time `E` (`BinanceKlineService.java:94`). In every case that Instant is exactly 3 h earlier than `wallet_trade.execution_price_observed_at` in our results:

  | wallet_trade | Instant in message | Value in our DB result |
  |---|---|---|
  | 451 | 06:57:02.437Z | 09:57:02.437 |
  | 426 | 03:36:01.457Z | 06:36:01.457 |
  | 403 | 01:02:02.580Z | 04:02:02.580 |
  | 392 | 23:35:00.328Z | 02:35:00.328 |
  | 344 | 17:55:00.032Z | 20:55:00.032 |

- The 15 s freshness gate passed on those Instants (`ExecutionPriceAuthorityService.java:46-48`). So the application's own clock agrees with the `Z` values.
- Binance REST klines confirm it:
  - EGLDUSDT 1m candles opening at **19:40, 19:41 and 19:43 UTC** on 10-11 match the DB rows labelled 22:40–22:43. For example, the 19:41 candle high is 4.101 (our peak tick) and the 19:43 candle runs from a 4.100 high down to a 3.994 low.
  - The candles opening at 22:39–22:44 UTC sit at 3.97 and do not match at all.
  - ACEUSDT 06:57 UTC matches the DB's 09:57 ticks.
- Every result column is TIMESTAMP(6). MySQL shows TIMESTAMP values in the reading session's time zone. My best guess (unproven) is that the coordinator's client session runs at +03:00 or SYSTEM, while the app forces `+00:00`.
- **This contradicts** `DATABASE_INVESTIGATION_RULES.md:35` ("Raw MySQL timestamps are UTC") and the AGENTS.md trade-investigation rule. I am reporting the contradiction, not resolving it; DBQ-CLAUDE-I3-001 decides between a display artifact and a storage defect.
- **Impact:** no P&L or trading-logic effect is shown; the app's internal clock is consistent. But all study timelines, the snapshot labels, and any cut-off written as a literal in a query (for example I2-012's `<= '2026-10-25 15:00:04'`) are suspect.

**F-I3-02: data freshness and signal latency are clean. (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I2-001, I2-006, I2-013)**
- All 12 entry and exit signals have `signal_mode=LIVE` and `data_quality_valid=1`.
- Signals were generated 0.30–3.93 s after the candle closed.
- `btc_context_age_seconds` was 1–3 s, except exit-source signal 159487 at 60 s (a NEUTRAL signal, not used for the exit).
- At fill time the execution price was 1.48–2.98 s old (cap is 15 s).
- `signal_processing_work` shows one attempt each, `COMPLETED`, with no failures.
- **Verdict:** no stale data and no execution delay on entry.

**F-I3-03: trade 143's exit at 4.027 came from an 8-second gap between ticks, not from repeated holds. F-I2-04 is resolved. (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I2-007; EXTERNAL: Binance)**
- From the lock activating to 22:43:27.038 KSA, the lowest tick was 4.077 (22:42:47), which is above the lock at 4.0758. The lock was never breached and the hold path never ran.
- The next tick, 22:43:35.021, was 4.027, already below the hard floor of 4.035. Wallet SELL 359 filled 24 ms later.
- Binance shows 433 trades in that minute, so the market was active; it fell from 4.100 to a 3.994 low.
- **Verdict:** the protection followed its rules. The loss came from gap risk combined with the earlier TP extension.
- **Open question:** were Binance stream updates missing during 22:43:27–35? Invalid observations are only logged, not stored (`MarketPriceEventService.java:53-57`), so only logs can answer this.

**F-I3-05: SHIBUSDT is limited by Binance's minimum price step. (EXTERNAL + AGENT INFERENCE)**
- The smallest price step is 1e-8 against a price of 5.49e-6, which is **0.18% per step**. The signal's ATR was 0.12%, smaller than one step.
- TP was about 2 steps above entry and SL about 2.7 steps below. The whole loss was exactly one step (5.49 → 5.48).
- The ATR-based TP/SL plan is not meaningful on this pair at this price.

**F-I3-06: new confirmed defect — signal-path exits fill at the signal's stored price, not a fresh market price. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)**
- `WalletAutoExecutionService.java:230` sets the fill price from `signal.getLatestPrice()`. The entry path is required to use `ExecutionPriceAuthorityService`, and the FIX-056 comment says signal price "must never be treated as an execution fill".
- Trade 162 (wallet row 407) filled at 0.08675. The freshest tick when it filled was 0.08673 (04:26:02.583); 0.08675 matches the 04:26:00.083 tick.
- None of the six SELL rows records `decision_price_usdt` or `execution_price_observed_at`, so the freshness of exit prices cannot be proven.
- The effect here is about +0.003 USDT (favourable to the trade), but the mismatch in principle also matters for Replay vs Production.
- Unexplained: row 407 has `signal_id` NULL even though the code sets the signal.

**F-I3-08: the selection rule leaves out every stop-loss loss. (PROJECT EVIDENCE: DBQ-FTS-002)**

- Every frozen trade lost through an exit rule, never through its stop. The sample is structurally unable to contain a stop-loss failure.
- Frozen IDs are kept as required. This is a limitation on how far the study's conclusions apply.

**F-I3-09: the outcomes after exit are mixed (outcome evaluation only; not used to judge the entries). (I2-011)**

- **The exit gave up a win:** TP was reached later on 182 (88 min after exit, stop never touched), 172 (21 min), and 158 (TP 11 min after exit, before the stop).

**F-I3-10: trade 143's re-entry was a chase after a one-tick dip. (PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL)**
- Position 142 left via profit lock on a single tick at 3.996 (22:39:41), between ticks at 4.040 and 4.021. Binance's 19:39 UTC low of 3.990 shows the dip was real but brief.
- The system re-entered 22 s later at 4.033, which was 0.93% higher, on a WATCH signal with RSI 84 (F-I2-05).
