**I1-F03: the stored timestamps may not display as UTC, so I convert them through epoch.** [PE]
- `paper_position.opened_at` and `closed_at` are `TIMESTAMP(6)` (`V1__initial_schema.sql:181-182`).

- My queries therefore calculate UTC from `UNIX_TIMESTAMP()`, which does not depend on the session time zone, and then add 3 hours for KSA.
