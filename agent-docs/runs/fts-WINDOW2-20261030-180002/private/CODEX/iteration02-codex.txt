### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CODEX-I2-005  
Purpose: Summarize the full accepted and excluded protection-price evidence during each frozen position’s holding period, without treating candle OHLC as a substitute for absent price-event evidence.  
SQL:
```sql
SELECT
  p.id AS paper_position_id,
  e.delivery_status,
  e.source,
  COUNT(*) AS event_count,
  MIN(e.observed_at) AS first_observed_at_utc,
  DATE_ADD(MIN(e.observed_at), INTERVAL 3 HOUR) AS first_observed_at_ksa,
  MAX(e.observed_at) AS last_observed_at_utc,
  DATE_ADD(MAX(e.observed_at), INTERVAL 3 HOUR) AS last_observed_at_ksa,
  MIN(e.price) AS minimum_observed_price,
  MAX(e.price) AS maximum_observed_price,
  GROUP_CONCAT(DISTINCT e.outcome_reason ORDER BY e.outcome_reason SEPARATOR ' | ') AS outcome_reasons
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.market_price_event e
  ON e.symbol = p.symbol
 AND e.observed_at >= p.opened_at
 AND e.observed_at <= p.closed_at
WHERE p.id IN (182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137)
GROUP BY p.id, e.delivery_status, e.source
ORDER BY p.id, e.delivery_status, e.source;
```
