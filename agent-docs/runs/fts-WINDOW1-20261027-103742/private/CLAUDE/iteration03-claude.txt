### E-22: The mechanical exit is driven by price ticks and has no age bound (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code)
- `LivePositionProtectionService.java:95-97` reads the latest 1m, 5m and 1h signals with `findTopBySymbolAndIntervalOrderByGeneratedAtDesc`, with no age limit.
- `onPrice` runs only when a 1m kline WebSocket message arrives (`BinanceKlineService.java:106-108`).
- So a committed exit signal is acted on at the next price observation for that symbol.

### E-23: EGLD observation gap during the #143 hard-floor exit (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I2-005, I2-006)
- The last observations before the exit were 22:43:19 at 4.099, 22:43:21 at 4.095 and 22:43:27.038 at **4.100**.
- The next one was 22:43:35.021 at **4.027**. That is an **8.0 s gap with a −1.78% move**.
- Lock 4.0758 and floor 4.0350 were both crossed between those two observations.

### E-24: Binance kline update cadence (EXTERNAL RESEARCH)
- The Binance Spot WebSocket docs say kline update speed is "1000ms for `1s`, 2000ms for the other intervals."
- The docs do not say whether a push is skipped when nothing changed.
- So for 1m klines the best possible protection sampling is about 2 s.

### E-25: The ingestion code drops nothing silently in this window (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code)
- `MarketPriceEventService.recordPending` (`:50-74`) rejects only these cases, without inserting a row:
  - invalid price;
  - missing timestamp;
  - FUTURE (more than 2 s ahead);
  - STALE (more than 15 s old).
- Each rejection logs `[FIX-153][PRICE_NOT_CANONICAL]`.
- Equal timestamps insert a `NON_CANONICAL` row.
- There is no dedupe and no throttle.
- Stale rejection cannot explain the missing :29, :31 and :33 pushes. The :35 event was processed in under 25 ms, and any events before it would have been under 15 s old.

### E-26: 5m freshness uses `generated_at` ≤ the 1m signal's `generated_at`, with a 20-minute window (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code)

- `FIVE_MINUTE_MAX_AGE = 20 min` and `ONE_HOUR_MAX_AGE = 3 h`. Age is measured from `generated_at`, not from candle close.

## FINDINGS

**F-12: The #143 loss came from an observation gap, not from the protected-hold policy. Severity: medium (structural, low frequency).**
- PROJECT EVIDENCE: E-23, E-25, `LivePositionProtectionService.java:207-217`. This resolves U-11 and the F-03 sub-question in favour of (b), a gap in tick coverage. Hypothesis (a) is disproven: no tick fell inside the band.
- AGENT INFERENCE: if any observation between 4.035 and 4.0758 had existed, `evaluateProfitLockBreach` would have been evaluated with 1h=NEUTRAL (TP-extension text, events #51 and #52), 5m=WATCH and current=NEUTRAL.
  - None of the hold branches match: `:58` needs a bullish 1h, and `:67` needs 1h=WATCH.
  - So the result would have been `PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT` at roughly 4.04–4.07, a profit of about +0.2% to +0.9%.
- Execution after the gap was excellent: 25 ms from observation to fill.
- Classification: an inherent gap risk of tick-sampled protection. The ingestion path drops nothing in code; it may have been an upstream push gap or a WebSocket delivery gap (U-20).
- The floor is an exit trigger, not a resting order, so it cannot guarantee price.

**F-13: Trade 143's entry authority came from a superseded 5m signal, because of a millisecond race at the 5m boundary. Severity: high for 143 (decision-changing); timing design defect candidate.**
- PROJECT EVIDENCE: E-26, E-27.
- The 5m candle 22:35–22:39:59.999 had already closed. Its signal, WATCH, was generated 2.0 ms after the 1m signal, so `latestAtOrBefore` excluded it and used a BUY from the prior candle, about 5 minutes older.
- The text "fresh 5m BUY" is misleading: the code calls a 5m signal fresh for up to 20 minutes by `generated_at`.
- AGENT INFERENCE: with #159407 (WATCH) the SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR route returns null. The 1m signal was `WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, `atr_overextended=1`, so the generic path would hit `ATR_ENTRY_BLOCKED` (`:644-648`), and the entry would most likely not have happened. Other routes are not exhaustively traced (U-21).
- This also strengthens F-09: the chase entry was authorised by outdated higher-timeframe evidence.
- AGENTS.md requires freshness checks against candle close time. This design is look-ahead-safe but candle-unaware.
- The race applies to every 1m candle that closes on a 5m boundary, about 20% of minutes, and 3 of the 6 frozen entries were at a boundary.

**F-14: Trade 182 entered against a hidden 5m STRONG_SELL. Severity: medium; logic defect candidate.**
- PROJECT EVIDENCE: E-28.
- Three consecutive 5m candles had original STRONG_SELL, displayed as NEUTRAL. The BUY route read the downgraded value, while other entry routes and the exit path treat bearish evidence as authoritative.
- This is an entry/exit asymmetry: evidence ignored at entry closed the trade 8 minutes later.
- The cause of the STRONG_SELL → NEUTRAL downgrade is not identified. `FinalDecisionService.safeNeutral` is used only for invalid data, so the downgrade comes from another stage in the confluence, BTC, derivatives or liquidity chain (U-22).

**F-15: Exit latency cost nothing in this sample (PROJECT EVIDENCE, E-21).**
- The lag from exit signal to fill was 0.04–37.7 s, depending on how often each symbol ticks (SHIB and ACE are thin).
- The price impact was 0 ticks in 4 cases and ±1 tick in 2.
- No losing trade was caused by execution delay. Entry latency, 2.9–4.2 s from close to fill, also had no measurable effect.
- Latent risk (AGENT INFERENCE): the exit inputs have no age bound (E-22). After an analysis outage, stale 5m or 1h signals would still drive exits. This was not observed here.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I3-003
Purpose: Gaps between price observations during each holding period (F-12, U-20).
SQL:
SELECT trade_id, COUNT(*) AS obs, SUM(gap_ms > 2500) AS gaps_over_2_5s, SUM(gap_ms > 5000) AS gaps_over_5s, SUM(gap_ms > 10000) AS gaps_over_10s, MAX(gap_ms) AS max_gap_ms
FROM (
  SELECT pp.id AS trade_id,
         TIMESTAMPDIFF(MICROSECOND, LAG(mpe.observed_at) OVER (PARTITION BY pp.id ORDER BY mpe.observed_at, mpe.id), mpe.observed_at) / 1000 AS gap_ms
  FROM paper_position pp
  JOIN market_price_event mpe
    ON mpe.symbol = pp.symbol
   AND mpe.observed_at BETWEEN pp.opened_at AND pp.closed_at
   AND mpe.delivery_status IN ('APPLIED','LEGACY')
  WHERE pp.id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137)
) g
GROUP BY trade_id
ORDER BY trade_id
LIMIT 10
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I3-005
Purpose: How often the 5m-boundary race happens in the study period (F-13, U-25): how often a 1m signal at a 5m boundary is generated before the 5m signal for the just-closed 5m candle.
SQL:
SELECT DATE(m1.generated_at) AS utc_day,
       COUNT(*) AS boundary_1m_signals,
       SUM(m1.generated_at < m5.generated_at) AS one_m_first,
       SUM(m1.generated_at < m5.generated_at AND m1.decision IN ('BUY','STRONG_BUY','WATCH')) AS one_m_first_supportive,
       ROUND(AVG(TIMESTAMPDIFF(MICROSECOND, m1.generated_at, m5.generated_at)) / 1000, 1) AS avg_5m_minus_1m_ms
FROM trade_signal m1
JOIN trade_signal m5
  ON m5.symbol = m1.symbol
 AND m5.interval_code = '5m'
 AND m5.candle_open_time = m1.candle_open_time - INTERVAL 4 MINUTE
WHERE m1.interval_code = '1m'
  AND MINUTE(m1.candle_open_time) % 5 = 4
  AND m1.generated_at >= '2026-10-11 00:00:00' AND m1.generated_at < '2026-10-13 00:00:00'
GROUP BY DATE(m1.generated_at)
ORDER BY utc_day
LIMIT 10
