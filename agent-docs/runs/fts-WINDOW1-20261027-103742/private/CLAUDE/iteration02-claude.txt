## FINDINGS

**F-07: The stop-loss floor inverts risk-reward on low-ATR 1m entries. Severity: high (structural).**
- PROJECT EVIDENCE: E-12, E-13.
- Five of the six trades entered with an expected R:R between 0.33 and 0.74, because the 0.5% minimum stop applied while the target stayed at 3×ATR.
- Their targets sat only 0.16–0.37% above entry. Any realistic exchange fee would take most of that move.
  - The fee claim is AGENT INFERENCE based on typical spot taker fees. I have not verified it externally in this iteration.
- I found a reward/risk ≥ 1.0–1.25 gate only on specific `ExecutionIntelligenceService` routes:
  - SCOUT (`:1055`), confirmation add (`:1098`), trend add (`:1128`) and continuation (`:1757`).
  - None was found for IMMEDIATE_VALIDATION, ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE, SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP or SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR, which are the routes these trades used.
  - Whether entry-quality scoring penalises R:R < 1 is still UNKNOWN (U-12).
- Classification: a **design/configuration defect candidate**. It is not a runtime malfunction, because the code does exactly what it says.

**F-08: None of the losses were stop-outs. All are small early exits on a non-bullish higher timeframe (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE).**
- At entry, all six had 1h not bullish (WATCH or NEUTRAL) and "Higher-timeframe context: MIXED".
- The dominant exit rule is "1m SELL confirmed by bearish 5m while 1h is not bullish". Its 1h condition was therefore already true at entry.
- So the entry policy accepted the exact higher-timeframe state that the exit policy treats as non-supportive. Exits then depended only on a 1m+5m bearish flip.
- With targets of 2–19 ticks, this produces many small-loss churns. This is a hypothesis to test across the population (I2-009).

**F-03 (revised): Trade 143 is resolved as by-design behaviour, not a code defect.**
- PROJECT EVIDENCE:
  - The text "Hard protected-profit floor 4.035016500000000 was breached" is produced only at `LivePositionProtectionService.java:212`. That is the triggered-lock path, not the rebase path at `:191`, whose text differs (`DynamicProfitLockService.java:186`).
  - The floor (entry × 1.0005) is an **exit trigger**, evaluated only when `price < floor`. It is not a price guarantee. The exit fills at the tick price, here 4.027.
- AGENT INFERENCE: for the position to reach 4.027 after peaking near 4.101 (95.35% of TP), one of two things happened:
  - (a) ticks between lock 4.0758 and floor 4.0350 hit "protected hold" (`:229`) because `evaluateProfitLockBreach` held. The 5m was BUY at entry, and that is what drives holds.
  - (b) no evaluated tick fell inside that 0.04 band.
- Which one happened decides whether this is a policy weakness (a hold through a lock breach that lost the earned profit) or a gap in tick coverage. Tested by I2-005 and the logs (U-11).

**F-09: Trade 143 entry was a chase made right after a profit-lock exit (PROJECT EVIDENCE + INFERENCE).**
- The 1m signal had RSI 84.32, was 4.35 ATR from SMA20 and was marked `WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, recommended 0%.
- The SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR override used the 5m BUY as authority and still entered at 25%, 22 s after #142 exited 0.93% lower.
- The TP distance of 0.0723 (6.6× the 1m ATR) does not match 3×1m ATR. It is either 5m-ATR-derived or extended (U-10).
- Whether this was intended or a logic defect cannot be judged until the override's rules are traced (iteration 3).

**F-05 (corrected).**
- My iteration-1 claim that 158 closed via the signal path was **wrong**.
- `wallet_trade.notes` shows that five of six exits (137, 143, 158, 172, 182) went through `executeMechanicalExit` on the live-price path.
- Only 162 used the signal-driven `PaperTradingService` path, which is why only 162 has `exit_signal_id`.

**F-10: Tick granularity undermines the SHIB geometry (AGENT INFERENCE from E-10 and E-17).**
- At 0.00000549, one tick is 0.182%.
- #172's TP was 2.04 ticks away, its SL 2.75 ticks, and its loss exactly 1 tick. The paired #146 won exactly 1 tick.
- ATR-based planning on this symbol is effectively quantised to coin-flip outcomes. ACE is similar: its TP was 4.5 ticks away.

**F-11: Traceability gaps (PROJECT EVIDENCE, low severity).**

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I2-009
Purpose: Population test of F-07 and F-08: how often the 0.5% stop floor binds, how often entry R:R < 1, and outcomes by close reason, across all CLOSED and STOPPED positions.
SQL:
SELECT pp.status, pp.close_reason,
       COUNT(*) AS n,
       SUM(pp.realized_pnl < 0) AS losers,
       ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl), 6) AS total_pnl,
       SUM(ts.risk_reward_ratio < 1) AS entry_rr_below_1,
       ROUND(AVG(ts.risk_reward_ratio), 4) AS avg_entry_rr,
       SUM(ABS((ts.latest_price - ts.stop_loss) / ts.latest_price - 0.005) < 0.00001) AS sl_floor_bound,
       ROUND(AVG((ts.take_profit - ts.latest_price) / ts.latest_price * 100), 4) AS avg_tp_pct
FROM paper_position pp
LEFT JOIN trade_signal ts ON ts.id = pp.signal_id
WHERE pp.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED')
GROUP BY pp.status, pp.close_reason
ORDER BY pp.status, n DESC
LIMIT 50
### END_DB_REQUEST
