## FINDINGS

**F-01 — The selection leaves out every stop-loss loss (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE). Severity: material to how the study is read.**
- The rule `status='CLOSED'` excludes `STOPPED` rows. Those 75 rows are 74% of all losing `paper_position` rows (75 of 101).
- My docs say a price at or below the stop loss gives paper status STOPPED (`claude-docs/TRADING_RULES.md` §7). So this sample covers only signal-driven and profit-lock exits, never stop-outs.
- I am not changing the frozen set. Conclusions from this study should not be described as covering "all failed trades". DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-006 measures how much the gap matters.

**F-02 — 15 days separate the newest loss from the snapshot (PROJECT EVIDENCE + UNKNOWN).**
- The newest selected loss closed at 2026-10-12 10:05:20 UTC (13:05:20 KSA). The snapshot was taken at 2026-10-27 07:37:44 UTC.
- So either there was no trading activity, only wins, or only STOPPED losses in that period. I have not established which (DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-006).
- This matters for any claim that the study is "daily".

**F-03 — A PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT that lost money is a contradiction to explain (trade 143) (AGENT INFERENCE, unverified).**
- By my documented rule, the hard floor is entry × 1.0005, and it only arms after a take-profit-extension rebase (`claude-docs/TRADING_RULES.md` §7.3).
- An exit at or above that floor should give non-negative gross P&L. Trade 143 shows −0.0372, and it reached that exit 3m31s after entry.
- Possible causes to separate: the price gapped through the floor before the next accepted tick; the exit used a different price source than the floor; the floor or entry was rebased after a progressive add; or my rule summary is outdated.
- It is a candidate defect, not a confirmed one.

**F-04 — EGLDUSDT re-entered after a loss (AGENT INFERENCE).**
- Trade 137 closed at a loss at 21:00:12 UTC (00:00:12 KSA, 10-12). Trade 143 opened on the same symbol at 22:40:04 UTC (01:40:04 KSA), 1h39m later, and also lost.
- The FIX-122 stop-loss evidence exclusion only applies after a *stop-loss*. Trade 137 was SELL_CONFIRMED, so that guard would not apply by design. To check in iteration 2.

**F-05 — Entries line up with the 1m close; several exits do not (AGENT INFERENCE).**
- All six entries happened 4–6 seconds after a minute boundary. That fits 1m closed-candle signal handling (the 90s freshness budget).
- Exits for 162 (:04) and 158 (:06) look like signal-path closes on a candle close.
- Exits for 182 (:20), 172 (:45), 143 (:36) and 137 (:12) fall mid-minute. That fits the live-protection tick path (`LivePositionProtectionService`).
- The two paths price exits differently (signal `latestPrice` vs the tick price). I need the audit row's `close_trigger` to confirm.

**F-06 — Losses are small (PROJECT EVIDENCE + INFERENCE).**
- The six losses total −0.273187 USDT, ranging from −0.032 to −0.075 per trade.
- Percent loss and risk-reward need entry_price, quantity and SL/TP, which are not loaded yet.
- Automatic wallet fees are recorded as 0 (`claude-docs/TRADING_RULES.md` §6), so these figures are gross. Real net losses would be larger.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-004
Purpose: Wallet fills (BUY, adds, partial harvests, SELL) inside each trade's lifecycle window, for P&L recomputation and fill-price/age checks.
SQL:
SELECT wt.*
FROM wallet_trade wt
JOIN paper_position pp
  ON pp.symbol = wt.symbol
 AND wt.executed_at BETWEEN pp.opened_at - INTERVAL 60 SECOND AND pp.closed_at + INTERVAL 60 SECOND
WHERE pp.id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137)
ORDER BY wt.symbol, wt.executed_at
LIMIT 60
### END_DB_REQUEST
