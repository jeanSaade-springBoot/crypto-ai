## FINDINGS

**F1 – Stop/target geometry makes these trades lose on average (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code; the expectancy conclusion is AGENT INFERENCE)**
- **The cause in code:** `AtrRiskService.assess` (`AtrRiskService.java:34-45`) sets the stop at the larger of 2×ATR and `minimumStopPercent` (0.50%). The target is always 3×ATR, from `application.yml:143` / `AtrRiskProperties.java:32`.
- **Effect at low volatility:** when ATR is below 0.25% of price, the 0.5% minimum stop sets the stop distance, so R:R works out to 6×ATR% (ATR as a % of price). Five of the six trades had ATR between 0.05% and 0.12%, giving R:R of 0.33 to 0.74.
- **I found no minimum-R:R gate:** a grep for comparisons against a risk/reward threshold in `src/main/java` found nothing. Each entry signal's ATR_RISK stage shows PASS.
- **What that means:**
  - Before fees, these trades need to win 57–75% of the time just to break even.
  - The targets (+0.17% to +0.37%) are about the size of a typical Binance spot round-trip fee of roughly 0.2%. That fee figure is general knowledge, not verified this session. `fee_usdt=0` on every trade leg here, so recorded P&L ignores this.
- **Win rate so far:** I1-011 shows the win rate since 10-11 is only about 52%: 49 of 58 CLOSED rows have non-negative P&L, and all 37 STOPPED rows lost. That is below the break-even rate.
- DBQ-CLAUDE-I2-007 will check this with summed P&L.

**F2 – Trade 143 probably entered on a superseded 5m signal (candidate defect; AGENT INFERENCE strongly supported, still to be confirmed)**
- **The 1m signal said wait.** Signal 159408 was WATCH, with its own ATR plan saying WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT, 0% position, price 4.35 ATR from SMA20 and RSI 84.3.
- **The SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR route overrides the 1m signal with a fresh 5m BUY.** It picks that 5m signal with `latestAtOrBefore(current, "5m", 20 min)`, which keys on `generated_at <= current.generatedAt` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1485-1491` and `:2455-2461`).
- **Timing at the 01:40 KSA boundary:**
  - The 1m signal 159408 was generated at 22:40:01.633945 UTC.
  - The 5m signal for the same boundary, 159407, was generated 2 ms later at 22:40:01.635944 UTC. It was **WATCH**, which would fail the route's `isBullish` check.
- **The 5m signal actually eligible:** with 159407 excluded by 2 ms, the newest eligible 5m signal is 159285: BUY, generated at 01:35:01 KSA, closing price 3.986.
- **What followed:**
  - The entry filled at 4.033, which is +1.18% above that 5m close.
  - It came 22 s after the previous EGLD position closed via PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT at 3.996 (wallet_trade #357, 01:39:41 KSA).
- **How to confirm:** I2-004 (execution_message for #358) and I2-005 / I2-006 (signal 159285's fields and work rows).

**F3 – Trade 143's "protected profit" exit closed at a loss because the price gapped through the lock (PROJECT EVIDENCE; no ordering defect seen yet)**
- **Lock armed quickly:** the profit lock became active at 01:40:23 KSA, 20 s after entry. TP was extended twice, the highest price was 4.101 (95.35% of the way to TP), and the lock level was 4.0758.
- **Price ticks seen at exit:**

  | Tick time (KSA) | Price |
  |---|---|
  | 01:43:27.038 | 4.100 |
  | 01:43:35.021 | 4.027 |

  No other EGLD event was stored in that 8-second gap (I1-009).
- **The exit fired on the first tick below the lock.** That tick was already below both the lock (4.0758) and the hard floor (4.0350), and below the entry price (4.033).
- **The protection acted on the first price it saw, as designed.** The 2-second kline-close price feed could not protect profit through a 1.8% drop in 8 s.
- **Feed gaps:** stored ticks for this symbol also have gaps (for example 22:43:05 → 22:43:19 UTC), with only 19 events in minute 22:43. Whether these are missing data or simply no exchange pushes is unknown; see I2-001 and I2-002.

**F4 – Trades 172 and 182 were made on a price grid coarser than their ATR (PROJECT EVIDENCE, arithmetic)**
- **SHIB (172):** one price tick (0.00000001) is 0.182% of price, but ATR was 0.12%. TP needed +3 ticks, SL was −3 ticks, the highest price reached was +1 tick, and the loss was exactly −1 tick.
- **ACE (182):** one tick is 0.053%; the loss was −4 ticks.
- **Consequence:** ATR-based stops and targets on these symbols are close to noise.

**F5 – Trade 182 bought against a bearish 5m that downgrading had hidden (AGENT INFERENCE; to be checked)**
- **Context at entry:** the regime was WEAK_DOWNTREND, but TREND_FOLLOWING produced a BUY. For about the previous hour, every 5m signal had a raw `original_decision` of SELL or STRONG_SELL, yet the stored `decision` was NEUTRAL (09:05–10:00 UTC).
- **What confluence saw:** the confluence text shows "5m=NEUTRAL". The raw bearish 5m read was therefore invisible to the multi-timeframe gate.
- **Outcome:** the highest price never exceeded the entry (progress 0%).
- **Next check:** what downgraded those 5m signals (I2-005: 176529 / 176655 decision_path).

**F6 – The other entries and exits followed the coded rules; these are valid losses given F1 (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)**
- **137:** the STRONG_BUY passed through the strict expansion-transition exception right after nine consecutive 1m STRONG_SELL signals. The exit came 5 minutes later on confirmed 1m and 5m bearish signals.
- **158:** the 5m BUY 165415 arrived 21 ms after the 1m signal and woke the 1m 165412 entry (FIX-014 route). The exit was valid SELL_CONFIRMED.
- **162:** the ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE entry bought at the local high. The exit happened on the signal path.
  - The fill used the signal's `latest_price` of 0.08675, while the newest tick at 04:26:02.583 UTC was 0.08673, a 2-tick difference in the position's favour.
  - The signal price was about 4 s old at fill. This is a minor gap in which price the signal path treats as authoritative.
- **172:** the HTF exit followed `PositionExitPolicy` exactly. 1h turned SELL at 10:00:06 KSA, and the exit fired once 5m turned NEUTRAL at 10:15:04 KSA.
- **Exit latency:** on the live path, the exit waits for the next price tick (182: 15 s, 172: 39 s). The price didn't move in those waits, so P&L was unaffected.

**F7 – Timestamp authority (PROJECT EVIDENCE)**
- On the live exit path, the `wallet_trade` SELL executes 1.2–3.2 s before `paper_position.closed_at` is written.
- `paper_position.opened_at` equals `signal_processing_work.completed_at`, not the execution time.
- For timelines, use `wallet_trade.executed_at`.
- Signal work 165412 has `paper_position_id = NULL` even though position 158 references that signal, which fits an entry attributed to the 5m wakeup work (I2-006).

**F8 – The deployed state contradicts AGENTS.md (PROJECT EVIDENCE; contradiction reported, not resolved)**
- **What the database shows:**
  - Flyway (I1-010) shows `V106`/`V108 fix 153 local candle` (10-09 and 10-10), `V107`/`V109 fix 154` (10-10) and `V110`/`V111 fix 156` (10-15 and 10-17), all `success=1`.
  - Every price event carries source `BINANCE_KLINE_DIRECT_FIX153`.
  - `trade_signal.data_quality_valid` is populated.
- **What AGENTS.md says:** the local-candle design is "pending implementation" and FIX-154 is "not-yet-implemented".
- **Conclusion:** at trade time, FIX-153 direct ingestion was live in Production, and the FIX-154 schema was applied.
  - Applied migrations do not prove the FIX-154 behaviour flags were on.
  - Flyway's `installed_on` timezone is unverified.
- **Action:** this needs dual-agent review. I am not updating any document.
