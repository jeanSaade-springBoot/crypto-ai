## FINDINGS

**F1 — None of the six trades hit its stop-loss. All six are early, discretionary exits that lost money.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)
- The selection uses `status=CLOSED`. A stop-loss close is written with status `STOPPED`: see `LivePositionProtectionService.java:178` and `PaperTradingService.java:183`.
- So every frozen trade was closed by a signal, the higher-timeframe exit policy, or the profit-lock policy, before its stop was reached.
- The charter's selection rule also leaves out the 75 STOPPED losses. That is a **scope note only**; I have not substituted any trade.

**F2 — Which code path closed each trade** (code evidence plus AGENT INFERENCE from timestamps)
- `SELL_CONFIRMED` and `HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED` are produced only by `PositionExitPolicy.evaluateNormalExit` (`PositionExitPolicy.java:29,42`). That is called on the live price path (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:235-238`), which closes at the live price.
  - The signal path cannot produce these codes. It writes `signal.getDecision().name()`, i.e. SELL or STRONG_SELL (`PaperTradingService.java:207-208`).
  - **Trades 182, 172, 158 and 137 therefore closed on the live price path.**
- **Trade 162 (`STRONG_SELL`) closed on the signal path.**
  - The exit price is `signal.latestPrice` (`PaperTradingService.java:449`), after `validateSell` passed.
  - The close at 04:26:04 UTC is 4 seconds after a minute boundary, which fits a signal-driven close.
- **Trade 143 (`PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT`) needs a closer look.** The hard floor is entry × 1.0005, and it can fire in two ways:
  - (a) the position is in the `TP_EXTENSION_REBASE` state (`DynamicProfitLockService.java:164-166`), or
  - (b) the profit lock was triggered and the price is below the floor (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:210-214`).
  - For this to happen within 3m31s, the price must first have moved well toward the take-profit (lock activation is at 70% of the way, or the TP was extended), and then fallen *below entry* before the next accepted tick.
  - The close at 22:43:36 is not on a minute boundary, which points to the live path.
  - A "protected-profit" exit that ends in a loss is a candidate gap-through or ordering defect. It is **not confirmed**; DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-003, 004 and 008 below are needed.

**F3 — Entry timing** (AGENT INFERENCE)
- All six positions opened 4–6 seconds after a minute boundary, which fits a 1m close followed by analysis and execution.
- Three opened exactly on 5-minute boundaries: 158 at 02:35, 143 at 22:40, 137 at 20:55. They may have come in through the 5m wakeup routes (FIX-014/FIX-023, `PaperTradingService.java:298-311`). This needs to be checked against the signal's interval and the `execution_reason` field.

**F4 — Small losses, measured before fees** (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code)
- Each loss is between -0.032 and -0.075 USDT.
- Automatic trades record `fee_usdt = 0` (claude-docs TRADING_RULES §6), so these figures leave out fees.
- Losses this small after 3.5 to 55 minutes point to exits close to the entry price.
- Note that the selection rule cannot catch trades that were slightly positive before fees but negative after them.

**F5 — Repeated losses on one symbol** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- Trades 137 and 143 are both EGLDUSDT. Trade 143 opened 1h40m after trade 137 closed.
- Whether FIX-020/FIX-122 (no reuse of evidence from before the previous exit) applied here is an open question for a later iteration.

**F6 — Deployed version is unknown, and the docs disagree with the working tree** (PROJECT EVIDENCE — CONTRADICTION)
- The working tree contains `V107__fix_154_trusted_higher_timeframe_buy.sql`, `V109__fix_154_…` and `V110/V111__fix_156_…`.
- AGENTS.md and claude-docs say FIX-154 is not implemented.
- I cannot tell which code version was running on 10-11/10-12 until I see the Flyway history (DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-010). Every code reference in this report is to the **current working tree** and may not match what was running when these trades happened.

**F7 — No losing closes after 10-12 10:05 UTC** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- The newest frozen close is about 6 days before the snapshot (15:00 UTC on 10-18).
- I cannot yet tell whether trading stopped or simply had no losses since then (DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-011).
