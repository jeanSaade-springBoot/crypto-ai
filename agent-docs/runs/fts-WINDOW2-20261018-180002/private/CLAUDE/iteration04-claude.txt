## FINDINGS

### R1. Trade 143's last price check used the 1m plan, not the 5m authority (PROJECT EVIDENCE; answers U-T2)

- **Which signal is checked:** in `PaperTradingService.java:296-350`, the signal checked is the 1m signal (159408), not the 5m one. The final check, `revalidateAtExecutionPrice` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1150-1169`), compares the fill only against that 1m signal's stop and target.
- **Stop and target source:** the position's stop and target also come from the 1m signal (`PaperTradingService.java:408-409`).
- **The 5m plan was unused for risk:** the 5m signal 159285 had stop 3.96607 and target 4.01322. Neither was used for the position or for the "price above target" check.
- **Result:** a fill at 4.033, above the 5m signal's own target, passed because the 1m target was higher.
- **Contradiction:** the route's own message says the 5m BUY "remains the setup-risk authority". In code, the 5m signal only grants permission, caps the size, and supplies the ATR for the decision-time entry-quality score.

### R2. Entry quality uses different ATRs at decision time and at execution (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- At decision time, entry quality uses the 5m ATR (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:634`).
- The comment at `:631-633` says the 1m ATR is wrong for this route.
- The execution-time recheck, `assessEntryQualityAtPrice` (`:857-859`), still uses the 1m ATR.
- This was not the cause of 143's loss, since the entry passed anyway, but the two checks are inconsistent.

### R3. An ATR position size of 0% is treated as "not set", which becomes 100% (PROJECT EVIDENCE; intent unclear)

- `PaperTradingService.java:328-330` turns `atrRecommendedPositionPercent <= 0` into 100.
- Signal 159408's own ATR plan said WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT at 0%. Its effective size became 100 × 25% = 25%.
- This may be intended for this route; it is a design question, not a proven bug.

### R4. The 5m signal race is built into the design, and Replay cannot reproduce trade 143 (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE)

- **Production:**
  - The 5m signal is picked as the newest one generated at or before the 1m signal (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:2455-2461` and `:2606-2608`).
  - The 1m and 5m analyses run in parallel, and nothing waits for the 5m signal from the same candle close.
  - The newer 5m WATCH (159407) was generated 2 ms after the 1m signal, so the older 5m BUY 159285 was used.
- **Replay:**
  - Replay stamps every signal's generation time with the candle close time (`RegressionTestWorker.java:584`). At equal close times it orders 1h, then 5m, then 1m (`:557-560`, `:787-793`).
  - It then opens the full signal timeline for execution (`ShadowProductionReplayService.java:105`).
  - Replay's copy of 1m signal 159408 is stamped 19:40:00Z and therefore sees 5m 159407 (WATCH). The route's bullish check (`:1486`) fails, so Replay never makes the 143 entry.
- **Parity gap:** Replay produces the correct outcome but is not at parity with Production, so it can't detect this defect.
- **Precedent:**
  - FIX-141 already fixed this same ordering problem for BTC context, by selecting on candle open time (`BtcMarketContextService.java:84`, `ExecutionReplayScope.java:122-137`).
  - The 5m confirmation lookups were never moved to that method. They are in `validateBuy` (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:81`), `validateSell` (`:169`), and the setup-ATR, breakout and transition routes.
- **Asymmetry:**
  - Trade 158 shows the system handles a *late bullish* 5m signal: the FIX-023 wakeup fired when the 5m BUY arrived 21 ms after the 1m signal.
  - Nothing handles a late *non-bullish* 5m signal that replaces an older BUY, which is the 143 case.
  - Exits act on a later price tick using the newest stored signals (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:95-97`), so they normally see the same-close 5m signal. Entries do not.

### R5. One 5m BUY probably authorised two EGLD entries within 4 minutes (PROJECT EVIDENCE partial + AGENT INFERENCE; check with I4-002)

- **Sequence:**
  - #356 bought via SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR at 22:36:03 KSA (signal 159321).
  - #357 exited via PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT at 22:39:41 KSA; its message says "5m=BUY".
  - #358 re-entered 22 s later, 0.93% higher than that exit.
- **Why it points to one signal:** 159285 is the only 5m BUY in that window.
- **No check would stop a re-use:**
  - The setup route has no "authority already used" check. `BUY_CONTINUATION` covers only the direct 1m path (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:67-79`).
  - A grep found no re-entry cooldown.

### R6. Trade 182: a 5m signal that was bearish before downgrading passes every direct-BUY gate (PROJECT EVIDENCE; design gap)

- **What the gates read:** the FIX-154 assessment (`HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:20-26`) and the FIX-11G rule (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:263-284`) read only the stored `decision`.
- **Effect on 182:**
  - The 5m signal was stored as NEUTRAL; its original decision was STRONG_SELL.
  - It was downgraded by the multi-timeframe step because the 1h was WATCH.
  - So the 5m bearish veto did not fire, and FIX-11G granted 25%.
- **Minimum quality:** the entry quality was 50, exactly at the chase cutoff (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:229`, `:1173`).
- **Inconsistent across routes:** other routes do check `original_decision`, at `:1425-1426` and `:1542`.

### R7. Trade 162: the FIX-119 late-entry guard is skipped if there is a single BUY observation (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- The guard is skipped when `buyCount > 0` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1275-1280`).
- The wallet message shows "1 BUY, 9 WATCH" and entry quality 50 (LATE_ENTRY), so one BUY in ten was enough to skip it.
- The rule was followed as written; the threshold is weak.

### R8. Trade 143's exit followed the coded order (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- **Exit at 4.027:**
  - The stop was not hit (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:174`).
  - The profit lock had triggered, and the price was below the hard floor of entry × 1.0005 = 4.035 (`:210-216`). That gave PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT.
- **Cause of the loss:** the price jumped straight through the lock and the floor (T3), not the order of the checks.
- **Labelling issue:** a reason starting "PROFIT_LOCK_" can carry a negative P&L, which is misleading in reports.

### R9. Update on the 143 price gap (T3) (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE)

- **Event counts for the 22:43 KSA minute (I3-005):**
  - EGLD had 19 events, the same as BNB and ENA. BTC had 27 and ONG had 2.
  - Every symbol is below the 30 that a full 2-second cadence would give.
  - No row is anything other than APPLIED.
- **Interpretation:** the number of pushes varies with each symbol's activity. That favours "Binance sent fewer pushes" (cause a) over "our app dropped them" (cause b). It is not proof, because rejected observations leave no database row.
- **Other open positions during the exit (I3-006):** only PEPE (139) and ICP (141). There was little protection work competing on the shared thread (T4).

### R10. T5 (timing) confirmed from stored fields (PROJECT EVIDENCE, I3-004)

The execution price was 1.48–3.20 s old for every BUY, well inside the 15 s limit.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-005

SELECT wt.execution_reason, (wt.execution_message LIKE '%BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M_WATCH_1H%') AS fix11g, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(pp.realized_pnl > 0) AS wins, SUM(pp.realized_pnl < 0) AS losses, ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl), 6) AS sum_pnl, ROUND(AVG(pp.realized_pnl), 6) AS avg_pnl FROM crypto_ai.paper_position pp JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_trade wt ON wt.signal_id = pp.signal_id AND wt.side = 'BUY' AND wt.status = 'EXECUTED' WHERE pp.opened_at >= '2026-10-11 00:00:00' AND pp.status IN ('CLOSED', 'STOPPED') GROUP BY wt.execution_reason, fix11g ORDER BY n DESC;
