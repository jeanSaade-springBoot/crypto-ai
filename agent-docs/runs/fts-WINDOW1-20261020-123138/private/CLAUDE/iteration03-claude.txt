### T1: Trade 143's stale-5m race is a Production-only effect of wall-clock ordering. Replay is expected not to reproduce it. Mechanism CONFIRMED in code; Replay outcome is INFERENCE
**PROJECT EVIDENCE**
- **Production** sets `generatedAt = Instant.now()` when analysis starts (`AnalysisService.java:148-150`). The order between the 1m and 5m analyses at the same close boundary is therefore down to thread timing.
- **Replay** sets `generatedAt` to the exact candle close (`RegressionTestWorker.java:584,616`). At equal close times it processes **1h, then 5m, then 1m** (`:557-560`, `intervalOrder` at `:787-793`).
- The Replay lookup includes signals with an equal `generatedAt`: `!isAfter(ref)` (`ExecutionReplayScope.java:110-119`).
- **Precedent:** FIX-141 already moved the BTC context lookup to candle-open ordering, "so Replay cannot diverge from Production" (`ExecutionReplayScope.java:122-128`, `BtcMarketContextService.java:84`).
- The 5m and 1h confirmation lookups still order by `generatedAt`:
  - `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:2455-2462, 2606-2608`
  - `TradeExecutionValidationService.java:367-378`
  - `MultiTimeframeConfluenceService.java:107-110`
  - `LivePositionProtectionService.java:336-343`

**AGENT INFERENCE**
- In Replay, the 1m signal for the 22:39 candle would see the 5m signal for the 22:35 candle, the equivalent of WATCH 159407. `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR` would then have no BUY to rely on, so **Replay would most likely not open trade 143 by that route.**
- Trade 158 shows the mirror case. Production's 1m work item could not see the 5m BUY that arrived 21 ms later and finished without opening anything. The entry then happened through the wake-up path. Replay would see the BUY directly, so the result would be similar but by a different route and audit trail.
- **Classification:** a Production/Replay parity defect class that FIX-141 fixed for BTC but not for 5m and 1h confirmation. It affected 2 of 6 entries in this sample, and the race works in both directions (it hid a WATCH for 143 and a BUY for 158). Confidence is high on the mechanism. It has not been verified in Replay, because running Replay creates state and was not authorised.

### T2: Analysis latency rises at hour boundaries. Observation, low impact
**PROJECT EVIDENCE (I2-002)**
- Normally a signal is generated 1.5–3 s after its candle closes.
- At hour boundaries it took longer:
  - For 162, 5m signal 167616 and 1h signal 167617 were generated 9.7 s and 9.8 s after the 04:00 close.
  - For 172, 1h signal 170667 took 6.0 s.
  - For 182, 1h signal 175258 took 4.9 s.
- Trade 137's exit at the 21:00 boundary came 8.9 s after close.

**AGENT INFERENCE**
- The likely cause is load: all three intervals close at once across about 19 symbols. That is not proven, and I3-001 tests it.
- No freshness limit was breached: the 5m maximum age is 20 minutes. This did not cause any of the losses.

### T3: Signal-confirmed exits wait for the next price event. CONFIRMED in code
**PROJECT EVIDENCE**
- `SELL_CONFIRMED` and `HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED` are evaluated only inside the price-event protection callback (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:233-243`), using the rules in `PositionExitPolicy.java:20-50`.
- Trade 172 exited at 07:15:43, in the middle of a candle, 30 ms after a price event.
- Trade 182 exited 18.5 s after its 5m candle closed. ACE trades thinly: iteration 2 recorded gaps between observations of up to 57.6 s.

**AGENT INFERENCE**
- On thin symbols, the exit time and price depend on the next kline update.
- For 182 this cost nothing, because the 10:05 candle was flat at 0.1880 (I2-003).
- It is not a loss cause in this sample. I3-004 confirms which event triggered each exit.

### T4: Paper rows are written 1.2–3.2 s after the wallet execution. Observation; the cause is UNKNOWN
**PROJECT EVIDENCE**
- `closePaper` is called synchronously after `executeMechanicalExit` returns (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:237-238`). Even so, `paper.closed_at` is 1.18–3.19 s after `wallet_trade.executed_at`.
- At entry the gap is 1.49–2.24 s.
- On the separate signal path (trade 162), the paper row closed 18 ms *before* the wallet trade.

**AGENT INFERENCE**
- The likely cause is slow transaction or commit time on the wallet path. The DB-timing logs that would prove it have rotated away (L1).
- Consequence for reports: `paper_position.opened_at` and `closed_at` are not execution times. Timelines should use `wallet_trade.executed_at`.
- It did not cause any loss.
