## FINDINGS

### F1: Trade 143 entered on an outdated 5m BUY because of a 2 ms ordering race. CONFIRMED (DB + code)
**PROJECT EVIDENCE**
- The SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR route takes its 5m authority from `latestAtOrBefore(current, "5m", 20min)` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1485`).
- That calls `findTopBySymbolAndIntervalAndGeneratedAtLessThanEqualOrderByGeneratedAtDesc(..., current.generatedAt)` (`:2456`, `:2606-2608`).
- The route requires the 5m signal to be BUY with `atr_immediate_entry_allowed` true (`:1486-1487`).
- I1-007 timings:
  - The 1m signal 159408 was generated at 22:40:01.633945 KSA.
  - The **newer 5m signal 159407** (22:35 candle, WATCH, immediate entry not allowed) was generated at 22:40:01.6359 KSA. That is **2.0 ms after** the 1m, so the `<=` filter excluded it.
  - The route therefore used **159285**: the 22:30 candle, BUY score 83, generated at 22:35:01.55, about 5 minutes old.
  - 159407 was committed at 22:40:02.94, before execution at 22:40:03.115 (wallet_trade 358).
- Both candles closed at the same instant, 22:39:59.999.
- If the route had read 159407 (WATCH), `setupTimeframeAtrAuthoritySignal` would have returned null and this route could not have admitted the entry.

**AGENT INFERENCE**
- Using a 5m that had been superseded at the same candle boundary is an ordering dependency, not deliberate behaviour.
- Under "information available at that moment", the newer 5m WATCH was available before execution.
- Classification: **logic defect candidate, high confidence.** Whether a different route would still have admitted the entry is unknown.
- The setup was a chase:
  - 1m RSI 84, 4.35 ATR from SMA20, entry_quality 70.
  - The entry came 22 s after the prior EGLD position (paper 142 / wallet 171) exited by profit lock at 3.996. Paper 142 shows that exit at 22:39:42.84; wallet 171 updated at 22:39:41.05.

### F3: Trade 182's 5m STRONG_SELL was turned into NEUTRAL by a 1h WATCH, which removed both BUY vetoes. CONFIRMED (DB + code)
**PROJECT EVIDENCE**
- In I1-006, signals 176529 and 176655 (5m) both have `decision_path` seq 5 `MULTI_TIMEFRAME DOWNGRADE STRONG_SELL→NEUTRAL`. The reason given is "1h=WATCH (trend 16/25, regime WEAK_UPTREND)".
- In `MultiTimeframeConfluenceService.java:136-138`, a bearish decision combined with `counts.bullish()>0` becomes NEUTRAL.
- `isBullishContext` is `isBullish(decision) || trendScore >= 15` (`:337-339`, `:24`). So a 1h **WATCH with trend 16** counts as bullish.
- The 1m BUY 176710 was then evaluated against the **effective** 5m decision, NEUTRAL with trend 10:
  - `hasHardBearishOpposition` (`:146-151`) checks `getDecision()==STRONG_SELL`. With the original STRONG_SELL, it would have **vetoed** the BUY into WATCH (`:120-122`).
  - Instead, `hasNeutralWeakStructure` (`:157-161`, trend 10 ≤ 10) kept the BUY ("confidence was reduced without blocking").
  - `HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:20-24,50` also uses `getDecision()`, so no 5M_BEARISH_VETO fired.
- In I1-004, `highest_price_usdt` was 0.1884, equal to the entry. The trade never moved into profit.

**Corrections to iteration 1:** the recorded regimes for 5m and 1h are WEAK_UPTREND, not WEAK_DOWNTREND. The 1m regime is not yet verified (I2-001).

**AGENT INFERENCE**
- This is a confirmed chain where one 1h signal with trend 16 (just over the threshold of 15) both neutralizes a strong 5m sell and removes both BUY vetoes that the original value would have triggered.
- Whether this is intended design or a defect needs a rules decision. It relates to FIX-154's direction, but that fix concerns 1h → 1m blocking and does not address this path.
- Classification: **logic gap candidate, high confidence on the mechanism.**
