### F-I3-01: The frozen selection leaves out every stop-loss loss (high severity, a scope finding)
[PROJECT EVIDENCE: `LivePositionProtectionService.java:174-183`, I2-009]

- A live stop-loss closes the paper position with `PositionStatus.STOPPED` (line 178), not `CLOSED`.
- The charter selects only `status=CLOSED`, so no stop-loss exit can ever be chosen.
- I2-009 shows **75 STOPPED positions, all of them losers, totalling −15.876 USDT gross.** The six frozen trades total only −0.273 USDT.
- **Effect:** the study covers small signal-driven and profit-lock losses and misses the largest loss class.

### F-I3-02: Trade 143 gapped straight through the lock; there was no protected hold
[PROJECT EVIDENCE: I2-004, I2-002, I2-003, I2-001, code]

This settles U-I2-1 and corrects the implication in F-I2-04.

Timeline (UTC / KSA):

| Event | UTC (2026-10-11) | KSA (2026-10-12) | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry (wallet trade 358) | 22:40:03.115 | 01:40:03 | 4.033 |
| Profit lock activated | 22:40:23.057 | 01:40:23 | 4.056 |
| TP extended | 22:40:41 and 22:41:13 | 01:40:41 and 01:41:13 | target 4.0647 → 4.0805 → 4.1043 |
| Peak | 22:41:55.024 | 01:41:55 | 4.101 |

| Next tick, already below floor 4.0350 and entry | 22:43:35.021 | 01:43:35 | 4.027 |
| Sale | 22:43:35.046 | 01:43:35 | 4.027 |

- The lock activated **20 s after entry**: 4.056 is at least 70% of the way to the original target (4.0552).
- Ticks normally arrive about every 2 s. Here there was an **8.0 s gap** between the last tick above the lock and the first tick below the floor, so no price between 4.0758 and 4.035 was ever observed.
- The sale followed that tick by **25 ms**, so the protection reacted correctly.
- If a tick inside the band had been seen, `evaluateProfitLockBreach` (`PositionExitPolicy.java:88`) would have returned `PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT`, because 1h was NEUTRAL, not WATCH or bullish. That would have been a small profitable exit.
- **Conclusion:** the loss came from a feed or observation gap combined with a flash wick (the 22:43 candle had low 3.994 and the largest volume in the window, 6382). It was not a hold-policy defect.
- The exit text "4.027 reached the protected profit-lock level 4.0758" is still misleading.
- Position 142 shows the same pattern 4 minutes earlier: ticks went from 4.040 at 22:39:35 to 3.996 at 22:39:41 (a 6 s gap) and crossed its lock at 4.0255 in one step.

### F-I3-03: Trade 143's entry may have relied on a superseded 5m signal (defect candidate, strong)
[PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE]

- The 1m candle 22:39–22:40 closes on the same boundary as the 5m candle 22:35–22:40.
- **Opportunity 36524** was recorded at **22:40:01.633945 UTC** (01:40:01 KSA) with `five_minute_decision=BUY` and route `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR`. Its text calls this "the fresh 5m BUY".
- **The 5m signal for the boundary that had just closed, 159407, was WATCH**, generated at **22:40:01.635944 UTC**, which is **2 ms later**.
- So the BUY used was most likely 159285, from candle 22:30, generated 22:35:01. That candle had already been superseded.
- **Why the code allows this:**
  - `setupTimeframeAtrAuthoritySignal` calls `latestAtOrBefore(current, "5m", 20 min)` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1485`, `:2455-2462`).
  - That lookup is keyed on the 1m signal's `generated_at` and accepts any 5m signal up to 20 minutes old.
  - It does not check which candle the 5m signal belongs to, and it does not wait for the 5m analysis of the same boundary.
- **Counterfactual (inference):** with 5m = WATCH, line 1486 returns null, so the `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR` route closes.
  - The 1m signal itself was `WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, recommended 0%, `atr_overextended=1` (I2-007).
  - So 143 was probably not executable. Replay needs to confirm this.
- **Not yet proven:** the `generated_at` of 1m signal 159408. I3-001 fetches it.
- **Other entries checked:**
  - 137, 158, 162, 172 and 182 used 5m signals that were current at the time.
  - Only 143's entry candle closed on a 5m boundary.
  - I3-002 measures how often this happens across all executed entries.

### F-I3-04: Exit delays were real but cost nothing
[PROJECT EVIDENCE: I2-001, I2-005, I2-006, code]

- Every live exit is evaluated only inside `onPrice`, meaning on the next 1m price event for that symbol (`BinanceKlineService.java:108`, `LivePositionProtectionService.java:54-72`).
- So the exit delay equals the wait for the next tick after the signal that made the exit possible.

- **172 (AGENT INFERENCE):** the 39 s lag is probably because no SHIB price event arrived; the 07:14 candle has volume 0. I3-003 checks this.
- **172's trigger is now explained** (this answers the F-I2-06 question):
  - The 1h SELL 172201 was generated at 07:00:06 UTC (10:00:06 KSA).
  - The exit only became possible when the 5m signal changed from WATCH (supportive) to NEUTRAL at 07:15:04.
  - This follows `PositionExitPolicy.java:25-33`.
- **Classification:** the exits followed the rules, and the delay did not change any result.
