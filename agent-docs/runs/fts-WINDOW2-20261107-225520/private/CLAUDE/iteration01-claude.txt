## FINDINGS

**F-I1-01 — The frozen set is not today's trades** [PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE]
- All six closed between 2026-10-11 20:55 UTC and 2026-10-12 10:05 UTC (DBQ-FTS-005).
- That is about 26 days before the snapshot (2026-11-07 19:55:22 UTC).
- Either no CLOSED losing paper position exists after 2026-10-12, or the trader has not been trading since then. **UNKNOWN** which one. This also adds to the snapshot clock-drift note in the charter.

**F-I1-02 — The selection leaves out every stop-loss loss** [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
- DBQ-FTS-002 shows `CLOSED` with 118 rows (26 negative) and `STOPPED` with 75 rows (75 negative).
- The filter is `status=CLOSED`, so all stop-loss losses (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:178` and `PaperTradingService.java:183` close as `STOPPED`) are excluded by design.
- The frozen set is therefore only signal-driven and lock-driven losses. That is valid for this charter, but the study cannot describe the overall loss population.

**F-I1-03 — Paper and wallet loss counts match in total, not per row** [PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE]
- Paper negative rows: 26 + 75 = 101. Wallet `EXECUTED` rows with `realized_pnl_usdt < 0`: 101 (DBQ-FTS-003).
- The two totals match, but that is not a per-trade link. DBQ-FTS-003 also does not filter by `side`, although its stated purpose is "SELL pnl".

**F-I1-04 — Close reasons point to two different code paths** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code]
- `SELL_CONFIRMED` (182, 158, 137) and `HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED` (172) can only come from `PositionExitPolicy.evaluateNormalExit` (`PositionExitPolicy.java:29,42`), called by the live protection path (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:235-238`). There the wallet exit runs **before** `closePaper`.
- `STRONG_SELL` (162) comes from the signal path (`PaperTradingService.java:197-208`), where the close reason is `signal.getDecision().name()` after `validateSell`.
  - In this path the paper close commits first (`completeClose`, `PaperTradingService.java:487-508`).
  - The wallet exit runs afterwards, and any `RuntimeException` is only logged (`:525-527`), so paper and wallet can diverge.
  - The exit price is `signal.getLatestPrice()` (`:449`), not a fresh live price.
- `PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT` (143) has three possible sources:
  - `LivePositionProtectionService.java:195`: the rebase hard floor.
  - `LivePositionProtectionService.java:213`: the lock triggered and price fell below the floor of `avgEntry × 1.0005`.
  - `PaperTradingService.java:194`: the signal-path hard floor, which prices the exit at `signal.latestPrice`.

**F-I1-05 — Trade 143 lost money on an exit meant to protect profit** [PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE]
- `realized_pnl = -0.037193156459`, and the position was held only 3 m 31 s.
- The hard floor sits **above** entry (`× 1.0005`). An exit at a loss means the exit price was below entry. Possible causes:
  - price jumped through the floor between observations;
  - a stale `signal.latestPrice` carried the exit (signal path);
  - `paper_position.entry_price` differs from `wallet_managed_position.average_entry_price` (for example after a progressive add);
  - the floor fired from the rebase state.
- **This is the strongest candidate for a logic or price-authority defect. Not confirmed.**

**F-I1-06 — Repeated losses on one symbol** [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
- Trades 137 and 143 are both EGLDUSDT. 143 opened 1 h 40 m after 137 closed, and both were short holds (5 m 08 s and 3 m 31 s).
- I still need to check re-entry cooldown and opportunity-consumption behaviour (FIX-020, `completePositionOpportunity`).

**F-I1-07 — Entry timing pattern** [AGENT INFERENCE]
- Every `opened_at` is 4–6 s after a minute boundary: :57:06, :36:05, :02:06, :35:04, :40:04, :55:04.
- Three entries (158, 143, 137) land on 5-minute boundaries.
- This fits entries triggered by a closed 1m candle with about 4–6 s latency. It is not verified until the candle and signal times are retrieved.

**F-I1-08 — Paper P&L excludes fees** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code]
- `completeClose` computes `(exit − entry) × qty` with no fee (`PaperTradingService.java:495-499`).
- The real economic loss is larger than `realized_pnl`. Fees must come from `wallet_trade.fee_usdt` or be computed separately (automatic fees are recorded as 0, per TRADE_INVESTIGATION_AGENT §6).

QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-003
Purpose: Full lifecycle of the six frozen positions (prices, quantity, SL/TP, exit signal, reasons), using the schema already proven by DBQ-FTS-001. Reason text is truncated to keep the result bounded.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-004
Purpose: Entry and exit signals for the six positions: decision chain, gates, data quality and timing. Columns follow the `TradeSignal` entity mapping; if a column fails, return the error so I can correct it against DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-002.

SELECT ts.id, ts.symbol, ts.interval_code, ts.candle_open_time, ts.generated_at, ts.created_at, ts.decision, ts.original_decision, ts.latest_price, ts.trend_score, ts.strategy_version, ts.strategy_entry_allowed, ts.primary_blocking_stage, ts.confluence_status, ts.confluence_entry_allowed, ts.confluence_higher_interval, ts.confluence_higher_decision, ts.btc_context_status, ts.btc_context_entry_allowed, ts.btc_context_age_seconds, ts.data_quality_valid FROM crypto_ai.trade_signal ts WHERE ts.id IN (SELECT signal_id FROM crypto_ai.paper_position WHERE id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137) UNION SELECT exit_signal_id FROM crypto_ai.paper_position WHERE id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137) AND exit_signal_id IS NOT NULL) ORDER BY ts.symbol, ts.generated_at LIMIT 12
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-005
Purpose: Wallet executions for the six positions' symbols inside each position's lifetime (±2 min), giving fees, decision vs fill price, price-observation age and execution reason (U4). Columns follow the `WalletTrade` entity mapping.
SQL:
SELECT wt.id, wt.symbol, wt.side, wt.status, wt.execution_key, wt.price_usdt, wt.decision_price_usdt, wt.execution_price_observed_at, wt.gross_amount_usdt, wt.fee_usdt, wt.net_amount_usdt, wt.cost_basis_usdt, wt.realized_pnl_usdt, wt.realized_pnl_percent, wt.execution_type, LEFT(wt.execution_reason, 300) AS execution_reason_300, wt.executed_at, pp.id AS paper_position_id FROM crypto_ai.wallet_trade wt JOIN crypto_ai.paper_position pp ON pp.symbol = wt.symbol AND wt.executed_at BETWEEN pp.opened_at - INTERVAL 2 MINUTE AND pp.closed_at + INTERVAL 2 MINUTE WHERE pp.id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137) ORDER BY pp.id, wt.executed_at LIMIT 60
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST

SELECT pea.* FROM crypto_ai.production_exit_audit pea JOIN crypto_ai.paper_position pp ON pp.symbol = pea.symbol AND pea.created_at BETWEEN pp.closed_at - INTERVAL 1 MINUTE AND pp.closed_at + INTERVAL 1 MINUTE WHERE pp.id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137) LIMIT 20;
SELECT pme.id, pme.wallet_position_id, pme.symbol, pme.event_type, pme.old_value_usdt, pme.new_value_usdt, pme.market_price_usdt, LEFT(pme.reason, 300) AS reason_300, pme.occurred_at FROM crypto_ai.position_management_event pme JOIN crypto_ai.paper_position pp ON pp.symbol = pme.symbol AND pme.occurred_at BETWEEN pp.opened_at AND pp.closed_at + INTERVAL 1 MINUTE WHERE pp.id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137) ORDER BY pme.occurred_at LIMIT 80
### END_DB_REQUEST
