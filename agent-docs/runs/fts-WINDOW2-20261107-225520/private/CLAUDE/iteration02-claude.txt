**F-I2-02: Five of the six trades had a take-profit closer than their stop-loss** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + DB arithmetic]

This is a strong defect candidate.
- In `AtrRiskService.java:34-45`, the stop distance is `max(2×ATR, 0.5%×price)`, but the take-profit is just `3×ATR`. There is no matching floor on the take-profit and no check on the risk/reward ratio.
- `riskRewardRatio` is only stored and shown on screens (`AnalysisService.java:540`, `DashboardApiController.java:1203`). I found no decision gate that uses it.
- When ATR is below 0.25% of price, the reward is therefore smaller than the risk.
- Checked against the numbers for trade 158:
  - Signal price is 1.4109 and ATR14 is 0.000769622546, from the entry text.
  - Signal price + 3×ATR = 1.41320887, which equals the stored TP of 1.413208867638.
  - Stop 1.4038455 = 1.4109 × 0.995, the 0.5% floor.
- The same 0.5% stop holds for 182, 172, 162 and 137. Trade 143 has an ATR-based stop and target instead.

| ID | TP distance | SL distance | Reward:risk | Exit vs entry |

| 182 | 0.241% | 0.500% | **0.48** | −0.212% |
| 172 | 0.372% | 0.500% | **0.74** | −0.182% |

- **Caveat:** the paper TP can be moved later by a TP extension (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:144-149`). I2-003 and I2-007 check this against the TP stored on the signal.

**F-I2-03: Some targets were too small to profit after real fees** [EXTERNAL RESEARCH, not verified this run + AGENT INFERENCE]
- Binance's standard spot taker fee is about 0.1% per side, so roughly 0.2% for a round trip.
- Trade 158's target (0.171%) would have lost money after fees even if it was hit. Trade 162's target (0.219%) would have netted about zero.
- The six gross losses add up to −0.273187 USDT. The real economic loss is about 0.05 USDT higher per 25 USDT round trip.

**F-I2-04: Trade 143 locked in a loss through the profit lock** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-003, I1-005 + code]

Timeline (UTC / KSA):
- The previous EGLD position (wallet position 171) closed by `PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT` at 3.996.
  - UTC: 2026-10-11 22:39:41.052
  - KSA: 2026-10-12 01:39:41
- **21.9 s later** the system bought at 4.033 (wallet trade 358), 0.93% above that exit.
  - UTC: 2026-10-11 22:40:03.115
  - KSA: 2026-10-12 01:40:03
  - The signal (159408) said `WATCH`, not BUY; the route was `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR`.
  - RSI was 84.32 and relative volume was 21.93, meaning the entry was made on an overextended spike.
- The position reached 95.35% of the way to TP, which implies a high of about 4.1010.
  - The lock level was 4.07579.
  - The floor was 4.0350165, which equals entry × 1.0005.
- It exited at 4.027, below both the floor and the entry price.
  - UTC: 22:43:35.046
  - KSA: 01:43:35
- Code at `LivePositionProtectionService.java:207-231`: once the lock has triggered, a price at or above the floor can be held through `evaluateProfitLockBreach` ("protected hold"). Only breaking the floor forces an exit, and that exit fills at the current price. **By design, a "profit lock" can therefore realise a loss.**
- Still unknown: whether a price between 4.0758 and 4.035 was observed and held, or whether price gapped straight through. I2-004 and I2-005 are meant to settle this.
- **The exit-reason text is misleading.** It says "4.027 reached the protected profit-lock level 4.0758", but 4.027 is 0.049 below that level.
