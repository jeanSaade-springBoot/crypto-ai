# Iteration 6: root causes and research for the six frozen trades (Claude)

I found three new things this iteration. One changes my earlier conclusions.

1. **The 1h-SELL exit did not cause most of the HTF losses.** Trades 91 and 96 never rose above their entry price, and 78 rose by one tick. The exit was only how these losses ended; the root cause is entries with no real edge. Whether the early exit hurt or helped depends on what happened afterwards, which DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-004 checks.
2. **A new confirmed defect: the startup candle import closes open live candles without recording any analysis outcome.** This explains why the XLM 5m 17:55 and 1h 17:00 candles have no signal, outcome or audit row. It is also strong evidence that the 18:00 KSA outage on 2026-10-10 was an application restart, not a WebSocket disconnect.
3. **Paper P&L ignores exchange fees.** At Binance's normal 0.1% per side, every frozen trade had a net risk/reward of 0.88 or less. Trade 101's profit-lock floor is below the fee cost, so exiting at the floor still loses money after fees.

This was analysis only: no code, config, DB, git or deploy changes. Plan mode was on, but I had no Write or ExitPlanMode tool, so this text is the whole deliverable. I did not read the CODEX folders, LIVE_TEAM_STATUS.md, LIVE_EVENTS.log or the other agent's memory.

**Time labels:** the DB displays timestamps in KSA (proved by DBQ-CLAUDE-I3-001). Below, "KSA" means the time as the DB shows it, and **UTC = KSA − 3 h**.

*Trade 82 (I5-004):*
- Both pieces of evidence behind the entry came from before the outage.
- The 2 accumulated BUYs were signals 115479 and 115502 (1m candles 17:51 and 17:52 KSA, created 17:52:01 and 17:53:01).
- The 0.1986 rejection zone came from 115502 and 115523 (17:52–17:53).
- The entry at 18:03:02 therefore used evidence 10–11 minutes old that spanned a 3-minute feed gap. No rule checks whether accumulated evidence spans an outage. This is a gap, not a breach.

*Trades 96 and 104 (I5-003; outcome data, used to judge the entry):*
- **96 (PEPE, opened 22:28:04 KSA):**
  - From 22:18 to 22:30, PEPE traded only at 4.05e-6 and 4.06e-6. One tick is 0.246% of the price.
  - The "breakout" to 4.06 had already printed at 22:24, 22:25 and 22:27. The fill at 4.06 was the top of a two-tick range.
  - The highest price after entry stayed at 4.06 (I3-006).
  - **The BREAKOUT label, with an entry-quality score of 100, came from a single tick increment.**
- **104 (ENA, opened 05:35:07 KSA):**
  - The 05:33 and 05:34 candles rose from 0.2231 to 0.2248 (+0.76%) on 25–40× normal volume. That is about 4 times the 1m ATR of 0.18%.
  - The peak of 0.2254 came in the next candle, and the trade exited within 5 minutes.
  - It bought right after a volume spike, a classic chase. The 1m signal's own `atrImmediateEntryAllowed` flag was 1, so that check did not catch a 2-candle impulse.

**G1. Correction to E2: the wake-up path does check 1m ATR (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:427,439,631-633`)**
- `current1m.isAtrImmediateEntryAllowed()` is required at `:427`.
- Only the entry-quality *extension score* uses the 5m ATR (`:439`), and the comment at `:631-633` shows that is deliberate.
- So in E2, "ATR extension is measured only on 5m" was wrong. The rest of E2 stands: the wake-up path has no minimum risk/reward, no FIX-064 price-action check and no late-entry deferral.
- Trade 104's direct BUY was rejected for context reasons, not as a chase (audit 1558: `BALANCED_CONFIRMATION_INSUFFICIENT`, 5m WATCH). Wake-up admitted it about 1 s later when 5m signal 133251 turned BUY. That is the intended route, not a bypass.

**G2. New confirmed defect: the startup import closes live candles with no analysis outcome (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I5-005, `CandleRepository.java:99-106,173-188`, `MarketDataService.java:31-56`, `MarketDataBootstrapService.java:49-69,105-110`)**

*What the data shows (I5-005):*
- XLM 5m 17:55 and XLM 1h 17:00 (KSA, 2026-10-10) are `closed=1` with `ingestion_source=RUNTIME`. They were last updated at 18:02:10.2 and 18:02:12.1 KSA (15:02 UTC).
- Neither has a `candle_analysis_outcome` row or a `local_reconciliation_audit` row.

*Which code produces exactly this:*
- `MarketDataService.importCandles` calls `CandleRepository.upsert(..., closed=true)` with provenance `BACKFILL`.
- Its `ON DUPLICATE KEY UPDATE` closes an existing `closed=0` row (`closed=GREATEST(...)`). It keeps `ingestion_source` and the token unchanged, so the row stays `RUNTIME` with no token.
- That gives no token, no dispatch, no outcome and no audit.
- Authoritative reconciliation cannot fix the row afterwards, because `finalizeExistingOpenFromAuthoritative` requires `closed=0` (`:141`).

*Who calls it:*
- `MarketDataBootstrapService.run` (an ApplicationRunner, so it runs at startup), dynamic coin activation, and the manual `/import` endpoint.
- In OFF mode it logs `[FIX-153][BACKFILL_IMPORT_RETURNED] analysis=false` and stops there.

*Rules it conflicts with:*
- AGENTS.md: "Each candle/work path must write one durable terminal analysis outcome."
- AGENTS.md: backfill "must never use a duplicate-key update that could finalise or alter a live row."
- Its own Javadoc ("Existing candles are preserved and only missing Binance rows are inserted"). The comment at `CandleRepository.java:175-176` shows the finalising was deliberate. That design conflicts with the newer AGENTS.md direction.

*Impact:*
- The candle data is valid: REST is authoritative, and in-progress candles are skipped (`:45`).
- The defect is the missing outcome and audit. It is invisible to health checks.
- **It did not cause trade 82's loss.** Even a correct recovery is non-live by design (`SKIPPED_NOT_LIVE`), so no signal would have existed either way.

**G3. The 18:00 KSA outage was most likely an application restart, not a Binance disconnect (AGENT INFERENCE, strongly supported; this replaces R7 for this event)**
- Only startup bootstrap, coin activation or a manual import can produce the G2 fingerprint. Of these, startup runs for every symbol and interval in turn.
- The timing fits a restart:
  - runtime writes stopped at 17:59:01;
  - the import closed XLM rows at 18:02:10–12;
  - runtime 5m 18:00 rows were created at 18:02:21–26 (R5);
  - the bootstrap runner finishes before the WebSocket path writes.
- The recovery bursts at irregular times (I4-001) also fit restarts or deploys better than a fixed 24-hour disconnect.
- **Not proved:** I have no log evidence. The archives are gzipped. DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-001/002/003 test this.

**G4. Root-cause correction for the HTF_SELL exits 78, 91, 96 and 104 (PROJECT EVIDENCE plus AGENT INFERENCE)**
- Highest gain after entry (I3-006): 91 = 0 ticks, 96 = 0, 78 = +1, 104 = +6 of the 13 needed for the target.
- These positions had no favourable move. The exit read an old 1h SELL (R2). That decided *when and how* each position closed, and in three cases that was probably at a smaller loss than the stop.
- **Revised root causes:**
  - **78 and 91:** a weak entry. A 1m WATCH at the minimum score of 60, on a coin where one tick is 0.18–0.25%, so the stop and target are 2–4 ticks. No edge after fees (G5).
  - **96:** a false breakout. A one-tick move inside a two-tick range was scored as BREAKOUT with entry quality 100 (G0).
  - **104:** chasing a volume spike after a +0.76% two-candle move (G0).
- R2 (the exit ignores signal age and the entry's soft-conflict decision) is still a **confirmed design inconsistency**. Whether it lost or saved money is open until I6-004 returns.
- I am downgrading R2 from "cause of the losses" to "exit mechanism, design inconsistency".

**G5. Paper P&L is fee-blind, and net risk/reward is far below gross (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `WalletAutoExecutionService.java:173,260,349,443,540,653` all `.feeUsdt(ZERO)`; arithmetic plus EXTERNAL RESEARCH on fees and tick sizes)**

*External facts:*
- Binance exchangeInfo gives a tick of 0.00000001 for PEPEUSDT and SHIBUSDT, and 0.0001 for XLMUSDT, ENAUSDT and AEROUSDT.
- The regular Binance spot fee is 0.10% maker and taker, or 0.075% when paid in BNB. A round trip costs about 0.20%.

*Net figures with the stop and target rounded to ticks:*

| Trade | Target, gross → net | Stop, gross → net | Net risk/reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| 78 | 0.74% → 0.54% | 0.99% → 1.19% | **0.45** |
| 91 | 0.36% → 0.16% | 0.55% → 0.75% | **0.22** |
| 96 | 0.74% → 0.54% | 0.74% → 0.94% | 0.57 |
| 104 | 0.58% → 0.38% | 0.53% → 0.73% | 0.52 |
| 82 | 0.25% → 0.05% | 0.50% → 0.70% | **0.07** |
| 101 | 0.87% → 0.67% | 0.57% → 0.77% | 0.88 |

- **Trade 101:** the hard floor at entry × 1.0005 (+0.05%, R9) is below the 0.20% fee cost. An exit exactly at the floor is still a net loss of about 0.15%. This strengthens T2/R9.
- This confirms the R9 inference: `realized_pnl` excludes fees, so stored paper results overstate real performance.
- **Classification:** a design gap in how risk is modelled. It inflates every risk/reward and P&L figure. It is not a code bug against the current rules.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-001
Purpose: G2/G3. Find every candle the startup import closed around the outage: closed RUNTIME rows with no token and no analysis outcome, updated 18:01–18:04 KSA on 2026-10-10. Shows how far the defect reached and the order symbols were processed in (bootstrap fingerprint).
SQL:
SELECT c.symbol, c.interval_code, c.open_time, c.ingestion_source, c.close_transition_token IS NULL AS no_token, c.created_at, c.updated_at FROM candle c WHERE c.closed=1 AND c.updated_at BETWEEN '2026-10-10 18:01:00' AND '2026-10-10 18:04:00' AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM candle_analysis_outcome o WHERE o.symbol=c.symbol AND o.interval_code=c.interval_code AND o.candle_open_time=c.open_time) ORDER BY c.updated_at LIMIT 200;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-004
Purpose: G4/U2. What would have happened without each exit: within 240 min after closing, did price touch the take-profit or the stop first, and what were the high and low in the first 60 min? Limited to the six frozen positions. Caveat: the stored stop_loss may be the final protected stop.
SQL:
SELECT p.id, p.symbol, p.closed_at, p.exit_price, p.stop_loss, p.take_profit, (SELECT MIN(c.open_time) FROM candle c WHERE c.symbol=p.symbol AND c.interval_code='1m' AND c.open_time >= p.closed_at - INTERVAL 1 MINUTE AND c.open_time < p.closed_at + INTERVAL 240 MINUTE AND c.high_price >= p.take_profit) AS first_tp_touch, (SELECT MIN(c.open_time) FROM candle c WHERE c.symbol=p.symbol AND c.interval_code='1m' AND c.open_time >= p.closed_at - INTERVAL 1 MINUTE AND c.open_time < p.closed_at + INTERVAL 240 MINUTE AND c.low_price <= p.stop_loss) AS first_sl_touch, (SELECT MAX(c.high_price) FROM candle c WHERE c.symbol=p.symbol AND c.interval_code='1m' AND c.open_time >= p.closed_at AND c.open_time < p.closed_at + INTERVAL 60 MINUTE) AS max_high_60m, (SELECT MIN(c.low_price) FROM candle c WHERE c.symbol=p.symbol AND c.interval_code='1m' AND c.open_time >= p.closed_at AND c.open_time < p.closed_at + INTERVAL 60 MINUTE) AS min_low_60m FROM paper_position p WHERE p.id IN (104,101,96,91,82,78) ORDER BY p.id;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-005
Purpose: G5/U3. Effect of fees across all trades: P&L before fees against estimated P&L after a 0.1% fee on each side, by close reason, since 2026-10-03. Aggregate only.
SQL:
SELECT close_reason, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(realized_pnl<0) AS losers_gross, ROUND(SUM(realized_pnl),6) AS pnl_gross, ROUND(SUM(0.001*quantity*(entry_price+exit_price)),6) AS est_fees, SUM(realized_pnl - 0.001*quantity*(entry_price+exit_price) < 0) AS losers_net FROM paper_position WHERE status='CLOSED' AND closed_at >= '2026-10-03 00:00:00' AND exit_price IS NOT NULL GROUP BY close_reason ORDER BY close_reason LIMIT 50;
### END_DB_REQUEST
