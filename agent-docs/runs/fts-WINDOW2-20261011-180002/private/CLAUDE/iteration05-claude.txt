# Iteration 5: entry quality of the six frozen trades (Claude)

Five of the six entries were weak by the system's own measures at the moment of entry, and the rules let all of them through. Three bought on a 1m WATCH signal rather than a BUY. Four entered against an hour-old 1h SELL that the system had relabelled as only a soft conflict. Three had a reward smaller than the risk once prices are rounded to the coin's tick size. Trade 101 was the only one where the entry itself looks sound.

One correction to iteration 4 comes first: the population query I4-004 found that trades with risk/reward below 1 did **not** lose more than other trades. Risk/reward is therefore a gap in the entry checks, but I can no longer call it a proven cause of losses.

This was analysis only: no code, config, DB, git or deploy changes. Plan mode was active, but I had no file-write or ExitPlanMode tool, so this text is the whole deliverable. I did not read the CODEX folders, LIVE_TEAM_STATUS.md, LIVE_EVENTS.log or the other agent's memory.

**Time labels:** DBQ-CLAUDE-I3-001 proved that our query wrapper displays stored UTC values in KSA. Below, "KSA" means the time as the DB displays it, and **UTC = displayed time − 3 h**.

- **R5 is narrowed (I4-002).** The 5m 17:55 and 1h 17:00 candles were recovered (closed without analysis) only for AERO and ACE (audit 360–363). Other symbols had only the 1m 17:59 recovered or 18:00/18:01 backfilled.

**E1. Three entries were bought on a 1m WATCH signal at the minimum allowed score (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-003, `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:73,2214-2219`)**

| Trade | 1m signal | Decision | Score / confidence | Entry path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 78 | 115049 | WATCH | **60** / 72 | Wake-up |
| 91 | 121198 | WATCH | **60** / 73 | Wake-up |

- A WATCH signal counts as "supportive" when its score is at least `WATCH_EVIDENCE_MIN_SCORE=60`. Trades 78 and 91 were exactly at that boundary.
- In the wake-up path the actual buy is driven by this 1m signal (`:420-429`). The 5m BUY only confirms it (`:446-449`).

**E2. The wake-up path skips most of the entry checks that other initial entries use (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `:397-457` compared with `:1047-1059`, `:1150-1168`, `:1257-1279`)**

- The wake-up path checks only:
  - the 5m trigger flags and the previous 5m not being bullish;
  - the 1m signal being at most 45 s old and supportive;
  - the non-ATR hard risk blocks;
  - `validateBuyContext`, which treats a 1h SELL as a soft conflict;
  - the entry-quality score, but only against the 50-point chase cutoff (`:1173`).
- It does **not** apply:
  - a minimum risk/reward;
  - opportunity health or evidence momentum;
  - the FIX-064 closed-candle price-action check (`immediatePriceAction`);
  - the late-entry deferral (FIX-119 only covers `ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE`);
  - a raw 1h supportive check.
- For comparison, the scout path requires entry quality ≥80, risk/reward ≥1.25, and a supportive raw 1h (`:1054-1059`).
- It also measures how far price has moved in ATR units using the **5m** ATR (`fiveMinuteTrigger.getAtrAtSignal()`, `:439`), while risk/reward is measured on the **1m** stop and target. The 5m ATR is larger, so the move looks smaller and the entry is penalised less.
- **Classification:** a confirmed design gap. It affects 78, 91 and 104. This is AGENT INFERENCE about effect; whether these checks would have blocked those trades needs Replay.

**E3. Entering on a soft 1h conflict creates a position that can never be added to and exits on a hair trigger (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `:1044-1045,1077-1086`; `PositionExitPolicy.java:25-32`)**

- Add-ons (`CONFIRMATION_ADD` / `TREND_ADD`) require the **raw** 1h decision to be WATCH or bullish. A 1h SELL that was treated as NEUTRAL at entry therefore blocks every add.
- So trades 78, 91, 96 and 104 were structurally limited to their initial 25% (or 15%). They could never grow, but they closed at the first 5m NEUTRAL (R2).
- One rule decided all three stages, and it was read three different ways:
  - **entry:** the 1h SELL is softened to NEUTRAL;
  - **add:** the 1h SELL is enforced and blocks the add;
  - **exit:** the 1h SELL is enforced and closes the position.
- This sharpens F2/R2: a soft-conflict entry has almost no way to make money.

**E4. After rounding to the tick size, risk/reward fell below 1 for most entries (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-003, I2-008, wallet fills; arithmetic)**

The stop triggers at the first tick at or below the stop price, and the target at the first tick at or above the target price.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 78 | 4.06e-6 | 4.02e-6 (4) | 4.09e-6 (3) | **0.75** | 1.50 (signal price was 1 tick lower) |
| 91 | 5.48e-6 | 5.45e-6 (3) | 5.50e-6 (2) | **0.67** | 0.546 |

- No part of the code accounts for tick size in risk/reward. On PEPE and SHIB the stored ratio says nothing useful about the real one.
- Because of E0, this is classed as **an entry-quality weakness, with no proven link to the losses**.

**E5. Trade 82: the entry was known to be poor before it happened, and the target sat beyond a known resistance (PROJECT EVIDENCE: wallet trades 204/205 in I1-008, I3-006, `:955-1003`)**

- The fill message says "Entry Quality 50/100 (LATE_ENTRY); recent rejection zone near 0.198600". That means at least 2 recent signals were below an earlier high of 0.1986, and the entry was within 0.5% of it.
- The target of 0.198849 was **above** that resistance. The entry at 0.1984 was only 2 ticks below it.
- What happened next (outcome, kept separate from the entry assessment): the trade's highest price was exactly 0.1986 (I3-006). It never got past the known resistance, then fell to the `SELL_CONFIRMED` exit.
- Context available at entry:
  - The system had **taken profit on XLM 4 minutes earlier at 0.1983** (wallet 204, 17:59:03 KSA / 14:59:03 UTC) and re-bought **higher**, at 0.1984.
  - It was the first live signal after the feed outage.
  - It relied on a 1h signal 63 minutes old (R6).
- Why the guards let it through:
  - The rejection penalty is floored at the cutoff of 50 (`:1000-1002`), so it can never block an entry.
  - FIX-119 did not apply, because the evidence included 2 BUYs and the 5m was BUY.
- **Reclassified:** a loss allowed by the rules, with a **confirmed entry-quality design weakness**: a known resistance below the target only reduces the score. It is not a logic defect.

**E6. Trade 78: the fill was one tick worse than the signal and nothing re-checked it (PROJECT EVIDENCE)**

Purpose: E1/E2/U1. Population check since 2026-10-03: results by entry stage and by the entry 1m signal's decision. Aggregate only. Times are as the DB displays them (KSA).
SQL:
SELECT w.entry_stage, s.decision AS entry_1m_decision, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(p.realized_pnl<0) AS losers, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl),6) AS pnl_sum, ROUND(AVG(w.entry_quality_score),1) AS avg_eq FROM wallet_managed_position w JOIN trade_signal s ON s.id=w.entry_signal_id JOIN paper_position p ON p.symbol=w.symbol AND ABS(TIMESTAMPDIFF(SECOND,p.opened_at,w.opened_at))<=5 WHERE w.opened_at >= '2026-10-03 00:00:00' AND p.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY w.entry_stage, s.decision ORDER BY w.entry_stage, s.decision LIMIT 100;
