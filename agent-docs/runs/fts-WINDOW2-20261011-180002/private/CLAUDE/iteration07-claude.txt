**C5. Fees: the system is profitable before fees and much less so after (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I6-005; the fee rate is EXTERNAL RESEARCH)**
- Of the 76 closed trades, 19 lost money before fees. After an estimated 0.1% fee on each side, **39** lose.
- **Net result:** +7.19 gross minus about 4.24 in fees ≈ **+2.96**.
- **13 of 30 PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT trades** and **5 of 16 PROFIT_LOCK trades** that won before fees lose after them. The profit-lock floor sits inside the fee band, so trade 101's problem (T2/R9) is general, not a one-off.
- None of the 76 trades closed on a STOP_LOSS reason. Every loss came from a signal exit, an HTF exit or a profit-lock exit.
- **Classification:** a design gap in how risk is modelled. Paper P&L excludes fees (`WalletAutoExecutionService` always sets `feeUsdt(ZERO)`). It is not a breach of the current rules.
