## FINDINGS

- **[PROJECT EVIDENCE]** All six frozen positions use the same source-level lifecycle: signal → coordinator → `PaperTradingService`; entry uses a fresh canonical price event, wallet BUY, then `paper_position` creation. A saved signal alone is not execution proof.

- **[PROJECT EVIDENCE]** There are two materially different exit routes:
  - Live-price protection: `LivePositionProtectionService` evaluates TP, stop-loss, profit-lock policy, then normal multi-timeframe exits. It performs the wallet mechanical exit first; only a successful result closes `paper_position`.
  - Signal processing: `PaperTradingService` may close an open `paper_position` from a fresh signal price. Its profit-lock branch is unconditional once triggered; it does not call `PositionExitPolicy`.

- **[PROJECT EVIDENCE / trade 104]** Prior targeted logs show an EDUUSDT live profit-lock breach at UTC `2026-10-10 14:45:22.035` / KSA `17:45:22.035`, held under `1h=BUY, 5m=WATCH, current=WATCH`. At UTC `14:46:01.312` / KSA `17:46:01.312`, signal `115326` triggered the signal-path profit-lock close at `0.070900`. The ~39-second gap is explained by different route policies, not by the recorded 207 ms symbol-lock wait.

- **[AGENT INFERENCE / trade 104]** This is a confirmed policy-route divergence requiring trade evidence before it can be called a defect: the live path permits a protected hold when higher-timeframe support remains; the signal path closes immediately at a triggered profit lock. The application of both records to `paper_position.id=104` still needs the immutable audit and wallet linkage.

- **[PROJECT EVIDENCE]** Signal-path financial consistency is not guaranteed merely by a closed `paper_position`: `PaperTradingService` saves the paper close before calling the wallet exit and catches wallet exceptions. Conversely, live protection closes paper state only after `executeMechanicalExit(...)` returns true. Therefore each frozen loss requires wallet-trade reconciliation; no loss should yet be classified as strategy, protection, or accounting failure.

- **[PROJECT EVIDENCE]** A negative paper P&L with a final price above a managed-position average entry cannot be explained from logs alone. `paper_position` may include a different entry basis, progressive adds, or prior realized P&L effects. The exact persisted quantities, prices, and wallet fills are required.
