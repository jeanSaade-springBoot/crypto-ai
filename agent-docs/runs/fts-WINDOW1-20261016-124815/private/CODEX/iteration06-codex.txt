- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** The frozen scope remains exactly `paper_position` IDs **182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137**. No substitutions were made.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** `db/CODEX/` contains no executed database evidence. Thus no trade-specific root cause—valid loss, bad entry, stale data, protection failure, or accounting issue—can be confirmed.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** Current source supports several distinct close paths: stop loss, take profit, profit-lock, confirmed multi-timeframe exit, position-analysis stop, and manual close. A null `exit_signal_id` is not proof of a manual or unexplained exit: live-price exits preserve context in `production_exit_audit`.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** A direct live-price exit is evidence-backed only when the relevant canonical `market_price_event` is `LEGACY` or `APPLIED`; `PENDING`, `NON_CANONICAL`, and `PROTECTION_NOT_APPLIED` cannot establish an accepted protection trigger. `APPLIED` proves the protection callback committed, but can still be a no-position no-op.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** The current source applies an absolute stop-loss before profit-lock and normal signal-exit logic. It closes on a live observed price at/below the managed stop, then records paper-position and exit-audit evidence. Whether this exact implementation/version ran for any frozen trade is unproven.

- **EXTERNAL RESEARCH:** Binance documents that normal kline streams update current candles every two seconds for non-`1s` intervals and include both UTC close time and an explicit closed flag. Its historical kline endpoint identifies a candle by open/close time and interprets `startTime`/`endTime` in UTC. Therefore candle OHLC cannot replace missing accepted intrabar protection evidence for a stop/exit reconstruction. [Binance kline stream specification](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-spot-trading/api/ws-streams/~), [Binance kline API specification](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-spot-trading/api/ws-api/market)

- **AGENT INFERENCE:** The only confirmed “root cause” at this stage is an evidence gap: the required authoritative rows and their linked audit/price-event records were not supplied. It would be unsound to label any of the six losses a strategy defect or protection defect.

- **AGENT INFERENCE:** The leading mutually exclusive classifications to test once evidence arrives are:
  1. valid loss: stop threshold reached on an accepted observation and exit committed promptly;
  2. entry-quality/strategy loss: entry complied with rules but contemporaneous signal geometry was weak;
  3. stale/coverage defect: unavailable or non-canonical price/candle evidence prevented timely analysis or protection;
  4. execution/accounting defect: paper position, wallet ledger, managed position, and audit rows disagree.
