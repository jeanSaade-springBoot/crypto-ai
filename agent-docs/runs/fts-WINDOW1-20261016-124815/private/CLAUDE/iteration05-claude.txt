## FINDINGS

### F-I5-01: The stop/target geometry gives R:R below 1 on 5 of 6 entries (PROJECT EVIDENCE; confirmed design property)

I reconstructed every level exactly from the DB:
- **Target:** decision price + 3 × the 1m signal's ATR. This holds exactly on all six trades.
- **Stop:** decision price × 0.995, i.e. the 0.5% floor, on five trades. Trade 143 is the exception: its stop is decision price − 2 × 1m ATR, which is 0.54%.

| Pos | Decision price | Stop (risk %) | TP (reward %) | R:R | Break-even win rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 182 ACE | 0.1884 | 0.187458 (0.50%) | 0.18885364 (0.241%), = 3 × 0.000151214 | 0.48 | 67.5% |
| 172 SHIB | 0.00000549 | 0.00000546255 (0.50%) | 0.000005510418 (0.372%), = 3 × 6.806e-9 | 0.74 | 57.3% |
| 162 DOGE | 0.08705 | 0.08661475 (0.50%) | 0.08724072 (0.219%), = 3 × 0.0000635742 | 0.44 | 69.5% |
| 158 XRP | 1.4109 (fill 1.4108) | 1.4038455 (0.50%) | 1.41320887 (0.164%), = 3 × 0.000769623 | 0.35 | 74.3% |
| 143 EGLD | 4.032 (fill 4.033) | 4.010202 (0.54%), = 2 × 0.0108988 | 4.0647 at open, later extended to 4.1043 | 1.50 | 40% |
| 137 EGLD | 3.999 | 3.979005 (0.50%) | 4.011875 (0.322%), = 3 × 0.0042917 | 0.64 | 60.8% |

- **Where the rules live:** `AtrRiskService.java:27-75` sets the levels, and the floor was established in F-I2-02. R:R < 1 costs 30 entry-quality points but is not a gate (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:991`, `CHASE_ENTRY_CUTOFF=50` at `:229`).
- **Two routes take their levels from the 1m ATR even though a 5m was the authority:**
  - 158 (wake-up on 5m BUY 165415, whose ATR is 0.13%).
  - 143 (SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR, whose own message says the 5m ATR is the authority, `:1505-1511`). In practice only entry quality uses the 5m ATR.
- **172 nuance:** SHIB's 1e-8 tick rounds both levels to about ±3 ticks, so its effective R:R is close to 1.
- **Route results since 10-05 (I4-006, AGENT INFERENCE):**

  | Route | Trades | Win rate | Net P&L (USDT) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | IMMEDIATE_VALIDATION | 60 | 45% | −2.31 |
  | SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP | 41 | 37% | −0.91 |
  | SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR | 24 | 54% | −0.77 |
  | ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE | 44 | 57% | +0.20 |

  All four win rates sit below the 57–74% break-even rates above. That points to structurally negative expectancy for floor-dominated entries, but it is **not yet stratified by R:R**; DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-001 does that.
- **Classification:** a design issue in entry geometry. It is not a code defect, because the code does what it says.

### F-I5-02: Paper trading charges zero fees (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- **DB fact:** every `wallet_trade` row for the six trades has `fee_usdt = 0` (I1-006).
- **Why it matters (EXTERNAL knowledge, not fetched this iteration, unverified):** the standard Binance spot taker fee is about 0.1% per side, so about 0.2% round trip. That is comparable to or larger than the 0.16–0.24% targets on 158, 162 and 182.
- **Effect (AGENT INFERENCE):** paper P&L overstates live viability, and the fee-blind 30-point R:R penalty underweights the problem.

### F-I5-03: The FIX-154 audit's "raw" decision is really the effective decision, so the confluence downgrade is invisible there (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- **Code:** `BuyHtfAssessmentAuditStore.java:87` writes `signal.getDecision()` into `*_raw_decision`. `HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:50,64` likewise read only `getDecision()`.
- **DB:** audits 2424 and 2425 record five_minute_raw_decision = NEUTRAL, while `trade_signal` 176655 has `original_decision = STRONG_SELL`.
- **Two more audit gaps:**
  - The audit is asynchronous and best-effort (`:17-21, 47`), so it can record an approval for a signal whose execution rolled back (see F-I5-05).
  - `evaluated_at` is the signal's `generated_at`, not when the evaluation actually ran (`:96`).
- **Classification:** an observability defect; it does not change trading behaviour. Separately, F-I4-01 (direct-path routes ignore the raw bearish 5m) stays a semantic inconsistency. Across all entries since 10-05 whose 5m was a neutralised bearish (I4-007: 23, 37, 95, 97, 113, 138, 139, 181, 182), 5 of 9 won. **It is not supported as a systematic cause.**

### F-I5-05: The first ACE BUY was approved, then rolled back into a terminal REVIEW_REQUIRED state (PROJECT EVIDENCE; confirmed processing defect, small impact on outcome)

- **Approval:** 1m BUY 176692 (09:56:02 KSA / 06:56:02 UTC) was approved by FIX-154 (audit 2424, FIX-11G 25%).
- **Failure:** its `signal_processing_work` row is `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `PROCESSING_OR_COMMIT`, `UnexpectedRollbackException … marked as rollback-only`, attempts = 1.
- **Why there was no retry:** `SignalProcessingCoordinator.java:127` sends every exception except an initial-lock failure straight to REVIEW_REQUIRED. That conflicts with AGENTS.md's "no pending outcome may require manual review".
- **What happened next:** the next minute's BUY, 176710, executed instead (position 182).
- **Possible trigger (HYPOTHESIS):** PEPE position 181 opened at 09:56:07.589 KSA, in the same second, so there may have been wallet-level contention. DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-002 checks this.
- **Relevance:** this is not the cause of the loss. It is a reliability defect that can delay entries or change which signal enters.

### F-I5-06: Trade 162's late-entry guard is bypassed through ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE (PROJECT EVIDENCE for the code path; whether FIX-041 actually fired on 167646 is unconfirmed)

- **167646 (BUY, 04:01:02 KSA):**
  - The direct-path validation passed as BALANCED_EARLY (audit 2196).
  - The work completed but no position was opened (I4-005: `COMPLETED`, `paper_position_id = NULL`).
  - The R:R of 0.39 implies an entry quality of 70 or below. FIX-041 blocks only BALANCED_EARLY combined with LATE_ENTRY (50–54) (`:672-684`). I cannot prove it blocked here: there is no durable per-signal EI decision history. `execution_opportunity` is one mutable row per symbol (`saveOpportunity`, `:2534-2574`).
- **167668 (WATCH, BREAKOUT profile, 2.82 ATR from SMA20):** it entered through ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE with "1 BUY, 9 WATCH", BALANCED_EARLY and LATE_ENTRY 50. FIX-119 stands aside whenever `buyCount > 0` (`:1275-1280`), and the only BUY in the window is 167646 itself.
- **Effect (AGENT INFERENCE):** a direct-path late-entry block can be turned into an accumulated-path entry one minute later, at a higher price (0.08693 → 0.08705).
- **Classification:** a guard-scope gap.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-001
Purpose: Outcome by entry-signal R:R bucket and entry-quality bucket since 2026-10-05 KSA (tests F-I5-01).
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN s1.risk_reward_ratio IS NULL THEN 'z_null'
            WHEN s1.risk_reward_ratio < 0.5 THEN 'a_<0.5'
            WHEN s1.risk_reward_ratio < 0.75 THEN 'b_0.5-0.75'
            WHEN s1.risk_reward_ratio < 1 THEN 'c_0.75-1'
            ELSE 'd_>=1' END AS rr_bucket,
       CASE WHEN wmp.entry_quality_score IS NULL THEN 'z_null'
            WHEN wmp.entry_quality_score < 55 THEN 'EQ50-54'
            WHEN wmp.entry_quality_score < 70 THEN 'EQ55-69'
            ELSE 'EQ70+' END AS eq_bucket,
       COUNT(*) AS trades, SUM(pp.realized_pnl < 0) AS losses,
       ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_usdt,
       ROUND(AVG((pp.entry_price - pp.stop_loss) / pp.entry_price * 100), 3) AS avg_risk_pct,
       ROUND(AVG(s1.atr_percent), 3) AS avg_1m_atr_pct
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position pp
JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal s1 ON s1.id = pp.signal_id
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_managed_position wmp ON wmp.entry_signal_id = pp.signal_id
WHERE pp.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') AND pp.opened_at >= '2026-10-05 00:00:00'
GROUP BY rr_bucket, eq_bucket
ORDER BY rr_bucket, eq_bucket;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-004
Purpose: Tick-level move into the entry (pre-entry chase) and the first five minutes after it (MFE/MAE, outcome only) for the six positions.
SQL:
SELECT pp.id, pp.symbol, pp.entry_price, pp.opened_at,
