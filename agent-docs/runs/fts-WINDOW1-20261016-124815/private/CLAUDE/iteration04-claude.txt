## FINDINGS

### F-I4-01: The direct-BUY path treats a neutralised 5m STRONG_SELL as "not bearish" (PROJECT EVIDENCE; defect candidate, a logic inconsistency)

- **How the neutralisation happens:** `MultiTimeframeConfluenceService.trendFollowing` turns a bearish 5m into NEUTRAL, with entry still allowed, when the 15m or 1h context is bullish (`MultiTimeframeConfluenceService.java:132-138`). `FinalDecisionService` writes that NEUTRAL to `decision` and keeps STRONG_SELL in `original_decision` (`AnalysisService.java:410-411`).
- **The direct path only reads `decision`:** `HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:50,64` and `TradeExecutionValidationService.balancedBuyWithNeutralFiveException` both do this. FIX-11G's own stated premise is that "neither timeframe is bearish" (`:250-253`).
- **The EI special routes check the raw decision explicitly**, for example `isBearish(five.getOriginalDecision())` at `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:355, 1368, 1425, 1542, 1634`, and `strongerDecision(...)` at `:1865, 1915, 2100`.
- **Trade 182:**
  - Every 5m signal before entry was NEUTRAL with a raw STRONG_SELL: 176529 at 09:50, 176655 at 09:55, and 176792 at 10:00.
  - The 1m had printed seven consecutive STRONG_SELLs from 09:48 to 09:54 KSA.
  - Entry came through FIX-11G. Eight minutes later the 5m printed an unneutralised STRONG_SELL (176934), and the live policy closed the position.
- **Classification:** routes disagree about what "bearish 5m" means. I cannot call this the cause; on the information available at entry, the raw opposition was visible. Which stage did the neutralising is checked in I4-001.

### F-I4-02: R:R below 1 is a scoring penalty, not a gate, and the floors line up at 50 (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- **The penalty:** R:R < 1 costs 30 entry-quality points (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:991`).
- **The floors:** `CHASE_ENTRY_CUTOFF = 50` (`:229`) and FIX-11G's minimum entry quality = 50 (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:49`).
- **The only R:R gate:** only the deferred-continuation route requires at least 1:1 (`:533-536, 1756`).
- **This sample:** entry qualities were 50, 60, 50, 50, 70, 58. Three sit exactly on the cutoff.
- **Worked example (AGENT INFERENCE, 182):** 100 − 30 (R:R 0.48) − 20 (about 5.97 ATR above the 30-minute low) = 50.
- **Combined effect:** together with the 0.5% stop floor and the unfloored target (F-I2-02), the system can admit trades whose stop is wider than their target on low-ATR symbols.

### F-I4-03: BALANCED_EARLY's late-entry block can be bypassed through ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE (PROJECT EVIDENCE for the code; whether it happened on 162 is a HYPOTHESIS)

- **The direct-path block:** BALANCED_EARLY + LATE_ENTRY is blocked on the direct path (FIX-041, `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:672-684`).
- **The accumulated path has no such block:** it validates with `validateBuyContext` → `balancedBuy` → BALANCED_EARLY (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:157, 314-316`).
- **FIX-119 lets it through:** its guard stands aside whenever `buyCount > 0` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1275-1280`).
- **Trade 162:**
  - 1m BUY 167646 (04:01:02 KSA, 0.08693) was not executed, with 5m and 1h both WATCH.
  - One minute later, WATCH 167668 executed through ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE at a higher price (0.08705). Its explanation says "1 BUY … HTF authority=BALANCED_EARLY … LATE_ENTRY 50".
- **Hypothesis:** FIX-041 blocked 167646. That same blocked BUY then counted as the "BUY evidence" that disabled FIX-119. I4-004 and I4-005 will confirm or refute this.

### F-I4-04: A 5m BUY that its own pipeline blocked from entry still counts as bullish authority on the direct path (PROJECT EVIDENCE + INFERENCE)

- **Trade 172:** 5m 171559 is a BUY with confidence 49. Confidence is capped at 49 only when entry is blocked (`FinalDecisionService.java:159`). It still granted BALANCED_STRONG.
- **The other routes require the 5m's own entry to be allowed** (`five.isFinalEntryAllowed()`): SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR (`:1487`) and both wake-ups (`:296, 402`).
- **Classification:** inconsistent authority rules. I4-001 will check `final_entry_allowed` and `primary_blocking_stage`.

### F-I4-05: Race between the 1m and 5m analyses at shared candle boundaries (PROJECT EVIDENCE; latent nondeterminism)

- **Trade 143:**
  - 1m 159408 was generated at 22:40:01.633945 KSA, and 5m 159407 (WATCH) at 22:40:01.635944.
  - `latestAtOrBefore` therefore returned the previous 5m BUY 159285 (candle 22:30–22:35) (`:2455-2461`).
  - The BUY ran at 22:40:03.115, after the 5m had already moved to WATCH.
  - `revalidateAtExecutionPrice` does not re-check higher-timeframe context (`:1150-1169`).
- **Other trades:** in 158 the 5m arrived 21 ms after the 1m, and the wake-up route covered it. In 137 the 5m arrived 17 ms before the 1m.
- **Classification:** there is no look-ahead, but the outcome depends on commit order. That is a Replay-parity risk.

### F-I4-06: The two exit paths use different freshness rules (PROJECT EVIDENCE; latent)

- **Signal path:** `validateSell` requires the 5m to be at most 20 minutes old and the 1h at most 3 hours old (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:165-191`).
- **Live path:** it takes the newest signal of each interval with no age check (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:95-97`).
- **Here:** not a cause. Every exit input was under 1 minute old, except 172's 1h SELL at about 15.6 minutes.

### F-I4-07: There is no same-symbol re-entry cooldown in the trading path (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- **Search result:** the only cooldown or re-entry logic is in the inspector, the debug monitor and REST admission.
- **Trade 143:** it opened 22 s after position 171's PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT at 3.996, at a price 0.93% higher.
- **`requireNewBuyTransition` doesn't cover it:** this setting only reacts to consumed 1m BUYs (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:67-79`).

### F-I4-08: The exit policy ignores P&L, and that is by design (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

`PositionExitPolicy.evaluateNormalExit` (`:20-50`) closes on the first 1m bearish + 5m bearish + 1h not-bullish combination, whatever the P&L. Positions 182 and 137 closed after 8 and 5 minutes at −1 and −3 ticks. This follows the rules.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-002
Purpose: The 15m and 1h context available (as of the moment) to each entry and 5m authority signal: inputs to the confluence neutralisation.
SQL:
SELECT s1.id AS anchor_id, s1.interval_code AS anchor_iv, s1.generated_at AS anchor_gen, ivs.iv,
       h.id AS ctx_id, h.generated_at AS ctx_gen, h.decision, h.original_decision, h.trend_score,

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-006
Purpose: Route-level outcomes since 2026-10-05 KSA, to test whether the study routes lose systematically (U-I4-05).
SQL:
SELECT wt.execution_reason AS entry_route, pp.close_reason, COUNT(*) AS trades,
       SUM(pp.realized_pnl < 0) AS losses, ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_usdt,
       ROUND(AVG(TIMESTAMPDIFF(SECOND, pp.opened_at, pp.closed_at))) AS avg_hold_s
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position pp
JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_trade wt ON wt.signal_id = pp.signal_id AND wt.side = 'BUY' AND wt.status = 'EXECUTED'
WHERE pp.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') AND pp.closed_at >= '2026-10-05 00:00:00'
GROUP BY wt.execution_reason, pp.close_reason
ORDER BY entry_route, trades DESC
LIMIT 200;
### END_DB_REQUEST
