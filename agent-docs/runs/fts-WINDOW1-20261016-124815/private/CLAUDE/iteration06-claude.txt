## FINDINGS

### F-I6-01: The common root cause is single-candle 1m BUY flips inside micro-volatility ranges, closed by the symmetric confirmation exit (AGENT INFERENCE on PROJECT EVIDENCE)

**Volatility at entry (I5-003; tick sizes from E-I6-02):**

| Pos | 1m ATR % | ATR in ticks | Price at entry vs its 2-min range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 182 ACE | 0.080 | 1.5 | 100% (bought the 2-min high) |
| 172 SHIB | 0.124 | 0.68 (below one tick) | 100% |
| 162 DOGE | 0.073 | 6.4 | 83% |
| 158 XRP | 0.055 | 7.7 | 36% |
| 137 EGLD | 0.107 | 4.3 | 58% |
| 143 EGLD | 0.27 | 10.9 | 78%, and +1.15% above the 2-min low |

- **Range position source:** I5-004, `pre120_min` / `pre120_max`.
- **The signal itself was a one-candle flip with no persistence (I5-003):**
  - ACE: 16 consecutive SELL/STRONG_SELL (09:38–09:54), then NEUTRAL (raw STRONG_SELL), then BUY, BUY.
  - EGLD 137: 15 bearish signals out of 16 from 20:39 to 20:53, then a blocked STRONG_BUY, then STRONG_BUY.
  - XRP: an earlier BUY, 164880 at 02:14, was followed at once by three SELLs.
  - SHIB and DOGE: NEUTRAL, SELL and WATCH oscillating around isolated BUYs.
- **No measurable edge after entry (I5-004, outcome only):** the 5-minute maximum favourable excursion was below entry for 182, 0% for 172, +0.07% for 162, +0.035% for 158 and +0.15% for 137. All five are below the 0.2% round-trip fee.
- **Exit mechanism:** no position touched its stop or target (I2-003). Five exits were bearish-confirmation exits (`PositionExitPolicy.evaluateNormalExit` :20-50; 162 via `validateSell`) at 1.5–4.7 × the 1m ATR below entry. That is ordinary 1m noise.
- **No production veto on recent bearish history:** the direct BUY path does not check the recent 1m bearish streak. Recent bearish history is used only to *enable* reversal probes (`recentBearishReversalAnchor`, `:2442-2453`, used at `:1359`).
- **Classification:** a strategy and entry-filter design gap. It is not a runtime defect: every gate passed as written.

### F-I6-02: Contributing causes per trade, and final classification (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE)

**182 ACE: valid by code, entered under a logic inconsistency.**
- FIX-11G's premise is that "neither timeframe is bearish". The 5m was effectively NEUTRAL but raw STRONG_SELL, after 16 bearish 1m signals (F-I4-01).
- The first attempt (176692) rolled back, so the entry came 1 minute later and 1 tick higher (F-I5-05).

**172 SHIB: authority defect.**
- An entry-blocked 5m (171559, blocked by `RANGE_ENTRY_LOCATION`) granted BALANCED_STRONG authority (F-I5-04).
- The 1m ATR was below one tick, so its ATR-based levels were meaningless.

**162 DOGE: guard-scope gap.**
- The direct-path FIX-041 block can be turned into an ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE entry one minute later (F-I5-06).
- Whether FIX-041 actually fired on 167646 is still unproven (I6-004).

**158 XRP: valid loss with no edge.** It was a SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP entry on a 1m WATCH. No defect.

**143 EGLD: entry and exit compounded.**
- Entry was a chase: +1.15% in 2 minutes, 22 s after the previous EGLD exit, with no re-entry cooldown (F-I4-07).
- The 5m authority was stale because of the boundary ordering.
- On exit, the price reached +1.69%. The target was then extended twice (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:100-164`), and a 177 ms flash drop jumped past the lock and the hard floor (F-I3-02).
- The direction was right; the exit policy turned a +0.84% target hit into −0.15%.

**137 EGLD: valid loss with no edge.**
- Counter-trend mean reversion after a 15-signal bearish run, entered through the expansion exception after the range-location veto (F-I5-08).
- The target sat just under a persistent ask wall at 4.012.

**Ruled out for all six:** stale data, execution delay, ingestion loss, and a stop/target-geometry cause (I2–I5).

### F-I6-03: Low-ATR entries cannot cover fees (EXTERNAL RESEARCH + AGENT INFERENCE)

- **Fees:** third-party sources put Binance's regular-tier spot fee at 0.1% maker/taker, or 0.075% when paid in BNB. I could not reach Binance's own fee page, so the figure is unverified against the primary source.
- **Targets vs fees:** at 0.2% round trip, the targets of 0.16–0.24% (158, 162, 182) clear fees by at most about 0.04%, and 158's target is below the fee.
- **Paper P&L excludes fees:** `fee_usdt = 0` (I1-006). That hides this, so the near-break-even result of the R:R < 1 group (I5-001) would be net-negative live. That claim is quantified in I6-001.

### F-I6-04: Systemic concern outside the frozen sample (PROJECT EVIDENCE, context only)

- **Entry quality does not predict wins:** the bucket with entry quality ≥ 70 and R:R ≥ 1 loses the most (I5-001). The scoring does not separate good entries from bad ones.
- **Scope:** this is not part of the frozen-six root cause. I record it so the larger loss population is not attributed to the study pattern.

### F-I6-05: Processing failures send work straight to terminal review (PROJECT EVIDENCE; reliability defect, not a loss cause)

- **What happened:** since 10-05 there were 14 5m work items that ended in REVIEW_REQUIRED on `QueryTimeoutException`, plus the ACE rollback (I5-002).
- **When:** they cluster 7–10 s after 5m boundaries (for example PEPE 01:40:07, 02:50:08, 04:05:07, 06:15:07), which matches the boundary slowdown in F-I3-08.
- **Why they never resolve:** every such failure goes straight to REVIEW_REQUIRED with no retry (`SignalProcessingCoordinator.java:127`). That conflicts with AGENTS.md's no-manual-resolution direction.
- **Effect on the six:** none of these failures touched a frozen trade except 176692 (ACE). PEPE BUY 450 executed at 09:56:04.898, 2.03 s before the ACE failure at 09:56:06.932. Contention between them is still a hypothesis (I6-005).

### F-I6-06: Replay reproducibility (PROJECT EVIDENCE for the code; nothing was executed)

- **Replay inputs:** Replay orders stored signals by `generatedAt`, then interval (`ShadowProductionReplayService.java:68-72`). It drives protection from stored ticks (`:91-143`), so exact-price parity is possible because ticks exist for all windows (I5-004).
- **Expected to reproduce:**
  - 143's stale 5m selection, because the generation order is recorded.
  - The TP extensions and the flash-drop exit.
  - The confirmation exits for 137 and 158.
- **Expected to diverge:**
  - 182: Replay uses a simulated wallet, so the rollback of 176692 cannot happen there. Replay would likely enter one minute earlier, at 0.1883.
  - 162, if any accumulated-evidence state differs.
- **No Replay evidence exists:** `wallet_position_test` and the `replay_*` tables show `TABLE_ROWS = 0` (I2-001; that figure is an estimate, verified in I6-006). Running Replay creates state, which this study does not allow, so **Replay parity is unverified**.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-001
Purpose: Gross vs estimated fee-adjusted outcome by 1m ATR% bucket since 2026-10-05 KSA (F-I6-01/03).
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN s1.atr_percent < 0.08 THEN 'a_<0.08' WHEN s1.atr_percent < 0.15 THEN 'b_0.08-0.15'
            WHEN s1.atr_percent < 0.30 THEN 'c_0.15-0.30' ELSE 'd_>=0.30' END AS atr_bucket,
       COUNT(*) AS trades, SUM(pp.realized_pnl < 0) AS losses,
       ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl), 6) AS gross_pnl,
       ROUND(SUM(0.001 * pp.quantity * (pp.entry_price + COALESCE(pp.exit_price, pp.entry_price))), 6) AS est_fee_0_1pct,
       ROUND(AVG(pp.realized_pnl / (pp.quantity * pp.entry_price) * 100), 4) AS avg_pnl_pct,
       SUM(pp.close_reason IN ('STOP_LOSS','TAKE_PROFIT')) AS sl_tp_exits
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position pp
JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal s1 ON s1.id = pp.signal_id
WHERE pp.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') AND pp.opened_at >= '2026-10-05 00:00:00'
GROUP BY atr_bucket ORDER BY atr_bucket;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-002
Purpose: Exit-reason mix and average P&L% since 2026-10-05 KSA (do confirmation exits dominate over SL/TP?).
SQL:
SELECT pp.close_reason, COUNT(*) AS trades, SUM(pp.realized_pnl < 0) AS losses,
       ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_usdt,
       ROUND(AVG(pp.realized_pnl / (pp.quantity * pp.entry_price) * 100), 4) AS avg_pnl_pct,
       ROUND(AVG(TIMESTAMPDIFF(SECOND, pp.opened_at, pp.closed_at))) AS avg_hold_s
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position pp
WHERE pp.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') AND pp.opened_at >= '2026-10-05 00:00:00'
GROUP BY pp.close_reason ORDER BY trades DESC LIMIT 50;

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-003
Purpose: Outcome by count of bearish 1m signals (effective or raw) in the 10 minutes before the entry signal (F-I6-01 streak pattern).
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN x.bear10 >= 6 THEN 'a_6+' WHEN x.bear10 >= 3 THEN 'b_3-5' ELSE 'c_0-2' END AS bear_bucket,
       COUNT(*) AS trades, SUM(x.realized_pnl < 0) AS losses, ROUND(SUM(x.realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_usdt,
       GROUP_CONCAT(CASE WHEN x.id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137) THEN x.id END) AS study_ids
FROM (SELECT pp.id, pp.realized_pnl,
             (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM crypto_ai.trade_signal t
               WHERE t.symbol = s1.symbol AND t.interval_code = '1m'
                 AND t.generated_at >= s1.generated_at - INTERVAL 10 MINUTE AND t.generated_at < s1.generated_at
                 AND (t.decision IN ('SELL','STRONG_SELL') OR t.original_decision IN ('SELL','STRONG_SELL'))) AS bear10
      FROM crypto_ai.paper_position pp
      JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal s1 ON s1.id = pp.signal_id
      WHERE pp.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') AND pp.opened_at >= '2026-10-05 00:00:00') x
GROUP BY bear_bucket ORDER BY bear_bucket;
### END_DB_REQUEST
