## FINDING 0 (blocks every time label): the DB results appear to show KSA, not UTC

**PROJECT EVIDENCE**
- Each wallet BUY message has two timestamps:
  - a Java `Instant` written with a `Z` suffix, which is true UTC (for example `priceObservedAt=2026-10-11T17:55:00.032Z`, trade 137);
  - the `execution_price_observed_at` column in the same row, which shows `2026-10-11 20:55:00.032`.
- The gap is exactly +3h in all six trades: 137, 143, 158, 162, 172 and 182 (DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-006).
- The entry snapshot JSON for signal 156723 has `generatedAt: 1791741301.57565` (epoch seconds). Working from the known anchor 1,800,000,000 = 2027-01-15 08:00:00Z, that epoch is **2026-10-11 17:55:01.576 UTC**. The DB shows `generated_at = 20:55:01.576`.
- The application writes UTC: `application.yml:13,22,24-25,35` set `serverTimezone=UTC` and `SET time_zone='+00:00'`.
- Explanation: MySQL `TIMESTAMP` columns are converted to the reading session's time zone. The investigator wrapper (`mysql.exe` on the KSA host) is probably running with session `time_zone = SYSTEM (+03:00)`.

**Consequence (AGENT INFERENCE, pending DBQ-CLAUDE-I2-001):**
- Every `TIMESTAMP` value in the study results is already KSA. True UTC is the displayed value minus 3h.
- My iteration-1 table, which labelled the raw values UTC and added 3h for KSA, was wrong.
- This conflicts with `DATABASE_INVESTIGATION_RULES.md` ("Raw MySQL timestamps are UTC"), so I am reporting it as a contradiction under AGENTS.md.
- Frozen selection order is not affected.

## FINDINGS (iteration 2)

**F-9: the stop floor without target scaling produces R/R < 1 (PROJECT EVIDENCE)**
- `AtrRiskService.java:34-45`: the stop distance is `max(2×ATR, 0.5% of price)` (`application.yml:143`), but the target stays at 3×ATR.
- Whenever ATR is below 0.25% of price, R/R falls under 1.
- 5 of the 6 entries have R/R between 0.33 and 0.74. Only 143 has 1.5.
- R/R is never used as a gate; it is only persisted (`AnalysisService.java:540`).
- The rationale text still says "Stop uses 2.0 ATR", which hides the floor.

**F-10: targets are at or below the round-trip fee, and fees are recorded as 0 (PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH)**
- Target distances are 0.17–0.37%. `wallet_trade.fee_usdt = 0` in all 12 rows.
- The standard Binance spot fee is 0.1% per side, about 0.2% round trip. That is general knowledge, not re-verified this session.
- So trade 158 would lose money net even if it hit its target, and 162 and 182 would be roughly breakeven.

**F-11: execution can act on a superseded 5m candle at the same close boundary (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE; candidate defect, trade 143)**
- The `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR` lookup is capped by the 1m signal's `generatedAt` at millisecond resolution.
- At a 5-minute boundary, the 5m candle that just closed can be excluded because its analysis finished 2 ms later, even though it closed at exchange time before the decision.
- Trade 158's wake-up path behaves the opposite way.
- This is the only entry where information available at decision time contradicts the authority the system used.

**F-12: extending the target is what lost money on trade 143 (PROJECT EVIDENCE)**
- The original target was reached at +0.79%. The continuation policy extended it twice within 32 s.
- The trade then exited below entry, through a "hard protected-profit floor".
- The floor does not protect profit if price gaps through, or if the code holds while price is between the lock level and the floor. Which of the two happened is pending I2-002 and I2-004.

**F-13: tick size is ignored when stop and target are set (PROJECT EVIDENCE, trade 172; 182 partly)**
- The plan is computed in percent and ATR. On SHIB one tick is 0.182%, so the stop and target are 2–3 ticks from entry, and one adverse tick produced a loss exit.

**F-14: four of six entries were low quality at decision time (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE)**
- 143 and 162: chasing above the upper Bollinger band (WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT, LATE_ENTRY).
- 182: a counter-trend BUY with a very weak 5m.
- 137: a reversal bar after 12 STRONG_SELLs.
- 158 and 172: R/R or tick geometry too small for any realistic move.

**F-15: the iteration-1 stale-exit hypothesis (F-4) is REJECTED for these trades (PROJECT EVIDENCE)**
- At every live exit, the 1m and 5m signals were ≤ 41 s old and the 1h ≤ 1805 s, all inside Replay's bounds.
- The code-level freshness difference between Production and Replay is real, but it did not cause these losses.

**F-16: all five signal-driven exits followed the intended rules (PROJECT EVIDENCE)**
- 137, 158, 162, 172 and 182 each matched their rule definition at the exit tick.
- These are valid losing trades whose loss came from entry selection and plan geometry, not from a wrong exit.
