## FINDINGS

### F-37: a STRONG_SELL ends the opportunity's memory, so a first bullish bar after a sell-off is scored as a clean new opportunity (PROJECT EVIDENCE; root-cause candidate for 137, 143 and 182)

How the code behaves:
- `evidence()` stops at the most recent 1m STRONG_SELL: `break; // a strong bearish 1m event is still a true memory boundary` (`EIS:2270-2275`).
- Each hard-bearish 1m also writes its own one-signal `CANCELLED / BEARISH_REVERSAL` opportunity (`EIS:474-481`).
- The next opportunity therefore starts from `OPPORTUNITY_HEALTH_START` plus whatever the 5m and 1h contribute. None of the earlier sell streak is counted.

What I5-002 and I5-003 show:

| Trade | STRONG_SELL 1m signals just before entry | Executing opportunity | Evidence counted (buy/neutral/bearish) | Health |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 137 | 12 (20:41–20:52) | 35535, started 20:54 | 2 (1/0/1) | **85** |
| 143 | 10 (22:26–22:34) | 36481, started 22:35 | 3 (1 BUY / 1 WATCH / 1 bearish) | **85** |
| 182 | 16 (09:39–09:54) | 40290, started 09:55 | 4 (2/1/1) | **86** |

- Each of these entries came 1–6 minutes after the last STRONG_SELL.
- The opportunity rows are exactly what the `break` predicts. For example, 40290 contains BUY 176710, BUY 176692, NEUTRAL 176654 and one bearish signal. The other 15 STRONG_SELLs are invisible.
- **AGENT INFERENCE:** this is a design choice, not a bug. Its consequence is that "opportunity health 85" means "healthy since the last STRONG_SELL", not "healthy". Whether this loses money across the system is pending I6-002.
- **EXTERNAL RESEARCH, context only:** published crypto studies find short-horizon reversal in illiquid coins and momentum in the most liquid ones. None covers minute-level data. So I can make no evidence-based claim that first-bar reversal entries have an edge.

### F-38: confirmed inconsistency. The direct-BUY path cannot see a 5m's original bearish decision, but the other EIS routes can (PROJECT EVIDENCE; root cause for 182)

- **Why 176655 became NEUTRAL (I5-005, resolves U-30):** at `decision_path` step 5 (`MULTI_TIMEFRAME`), its STRONG_SELL was downgraded to NEUTRAL because "Bearish signal conflicts with bullish higher-timeframe structure… 1h=WATCH (trend 16/25, regime WEAK_UPTREND)".
- **The same 1h WATCH was then counted a second time.** FIX-11G grants authority for "5m NEUTRAL + 1h WATCH" (`TEVS:263-284`). So the 1h WATCH first erased the 5m bearish evidence and then served as the bullish half of the authority.
- **The direct path only receives the effective label.** `validateBuy` passes `assessment.effectiveFiveMinute()` as a bare `SignalDecision` (`TEVS:102-108`), and FIX-154 recorded `five_minute_raw_decision = NEUTRAL` (I4-004).
- **Other EIS routes do check the original decision of the 5m,** using `isBearish(five.getOriginalDecision())` or `strongerDecision(decision, original)`. Examples: `EIS:1542`, `:1634`, `:1865`, `:1915`, `:2100`.
- **The code contradicts its own stated premise.** The FIX-11G comment says "neither timeframe is bearish" (`TEVS:252-253`). That is false for 176655.
- **182 also missed the stricter exception.** It had confidence 68 (< 72) and EQ 50 (< 70), so it skipped `BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M` (`TEVS:234-236`) and fell through to FIX-11G, which has no confidence floor and only requires EQ ≥ 50.
- **System-wide (I4-002):** BUYs whose 5m was a downgraded bearish signal numbered 8, with 3 losers and −0.431 USDT. That is −0.054 per BUY, against −0.015 for all other BUYs. The sample is small.
- **Status:** a confirmed logic inconsistency, and a candidate defect for dual-agent agreement.

### F-40: Replay-parity candidate. The age arithmetic for trade 162 depends on milliseconds of wall-clock time (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE)

- In Production, `started_at` (03:41:02.837) and the current `generatedAt` (04:02:02.813) are both wall-clock times about 2.8 s after their candle closes. The second came 24 ms "earlier" relative to its close, which is why the truncated age is 20.
- Replay sets `evaluationTime` to the exact candle close (`RegressionTestWorker.java:584`, F-18). That gives an age of exactly 21:00, so −5 and EQ 45, and **Replay would likely block 162**, provided the opportunity starts on the same signal.
- This joins F-18 (143) and F-24 as a class: wall-clock ordering and timing feed decisions that Replay evaluates on candle time. Not confirmed without a Replay run (U-21).

### F-42: the frozen six belong to a loss class that is roughly break-even gross; fees decide the sign (PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH; reframes F-10 and F-31)

**Geometry.** For all five 1m entries, `stop_loss = price × 0.995` exactly. For example, 0.1884 × 0.995 = 0.187458, which is the stored value for 176710. Take-profit = 3 × the 1m ATR. With the 1m ATR at only 0.054–0.124% of price, the stop sits 4–9 ATR away.

**Consequence:**
- These positions almost never reach the stop.
- The take-profit < 0.40% buckets show 8 stopped out of 49.
- They end through confirmed SELL or HTF exits at −0.13% to −0.35%.

**System-wide, take-profit < 0.40% (I5-004):**
- **CLOSED:** 41 trades, 13 losers, +0.312 USDT gross.
- **Including STOPPED:** −0.580 USDT gross.
- `fee_usdt = 0` on every row, so paper P&L ignores fees.

**External research (third-party sources):**
- The standard Binance spot fee for a regular user is 0.10% per side (0.075% if paid in BNB), so a round trip costs 0.15–0.20%.
- At those rates this class is clearly net-negative. The exact figure is pending I6-003.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-002
Purpose: U-33 / F-37. Outcome by the number of 1m STRONG_SELL signals (effective or original) in the 15 minutes before each entry signal, across all closed paper positions.
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN x.ss15=0 THEN 'SS0' WHEN x.ss15<5 THEN 'SS1-4' ELSE 'SS5+' END AS bucket, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(x.realized_pnl<0) AS losers, SUM(x.status='STOPPED') AS stopped, ROUND(SUM(x.realized_pnl),6) AS pnl_sum FROM (SELECT p.id, p.status, p.realized_pnl, (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM crypto_ai.trade_signal s WHERE s.symbol=e.symbol AND s.interval_code='1m' AND s.generated_at >= e.generated_at - INTERVAL 15 MINUTE AND s.generated_at < e.generated_at AND (s.decision='STRONG_SELL' OR s.original_decision='STRONG_SELL')) AS ss15 FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal e ON e.id=p.signal_id WHERE p.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED')) x GROUP BY bucket ORDER BY bucket;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-003
Purpose: U-34 / F-42. Confirm the schema, then compare floor-stop and ATR-stop classes by status and close reason, with gross P&L and an estimated 0.2% round-trip fee.
SQL:
SELECT COLUMN_NAME, DATA_TYPE FROM information_schema.COLUMNS WHERE TABLE_SCHEMA='crypto_ai' AND TABLE_NAME='paper_position' ORDER BY ORDINAL_POSITION;
SELECT CASE WHEN ABS((p.entry_price-p.stop_loss)/p.entry_price*100-0.5)<=0.02 THEN 'FLOOR_STOP' ELSE 'ATR_STOP' END AS stop_type, p.status, p.close_reason, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(p.realized_pnl<0) AS losers, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl),6) AS gross_pnl, ROUND(SUM(p.quantity*p.entry_price)*0.002,6) AS est_fee_0_2pct, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl)-SUM(p.quantity*p.entry_price)*0.002,6) AS est_net_pnl, ROUND(AVG((p.take_profit-p.entry_price)/p.entry_price*100),3) AS avg_tp_pct FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p WHERE p.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') GROUP BY stop_type, p.status, p.close_reason ORDER BY stop_type, p.status, p.close_reason;
### END_DB_REQUEST
