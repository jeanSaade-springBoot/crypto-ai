**Rule for the rest of this report:** times are written as **KSA (UTC)**. Literals in my SQL are read in the wrapper's +03:00 session, which matches the displayed values.

## FINDINGS

### F-17: trade 143's loss below the floor was a gap between price samples, not a protected hold or an execution delay (PROJECT EVIDENCE; resolves U-10 and U-12)

What the EGLDUSDT price events show (I2-002):
- Every event between the 22:41:13 extension and the exit was at or above **4.077**, above the lock level 4.075790. No event was ever observed between the lock (4.0758) and the hard floor (4.0350).
- The last event before the drop was 4.100 at **22:43:27.038 KSA (19:43:27.038 UTC)**.
- There were no EGLD events for **8.0 s**.
- The next event was **4.027 at 22:43:35.021**, below the floor.

What the system did:
- The wallet SELL ran at 22:43:35.046, **25 ms after that tick**.
- `production_exit_audit` 141 confirms the reason: "Hard protected-profit floor 4.0350165 was breached".
- The fall was a real market move, not a bad price: ticks of 4.018, 4.016 and 4.007 followed, and the 60-minute minimum close was 3.944 (I2-007).

**EXTERNAL RESEARCH:** Binance kline streams push at most every 2000 ms for 1m-and-longer intervals, and each payload carries the candle's high and low. A tick-level stop on kline pushes can therefore never fill between pushes.

**AGENT INFERENCE:**
- The loss comes from two things together: the target extensions (F-12) gave up a +0.79% target exit, and the 2-second sampling cannot defend a level the market crosses within one quiet interval.
- There is no logic defect in the exit path itself.
- I2-008 shows the same pattern across the whole system: all 4 `PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT` losers (17, 101, 131, 143) had at least one target extension.

### F-18: a candidate defect in the timing of the 1m/5m race, both in Production and in Replay parity (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE)

**PROJECT EVIDENCE: Production orders signals by wall clock.**
- `generatedAt = Instant.now()` (`AnalysisService.java:150`).
- The 5m and 1h lookups are capped at the current signal's `generatedAt` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:2455-2461`, `:2606-2608`).
- For trade 143:
  - The 1m signal 159408 was generated at 22:40:01.633945.
  - The newer 5m WATCH 159407 was generated 2 ms later, at 22:40:01.635944.
  - 159407 was **inserted at 22:40:02.9406, before the 1m (02.9487) and 175 ms before the wallet BUY (03.115)**.
  - The cap still excluded it. The decision used the 5m BUY 159285 from the earlier 22:30–22:35 candle.

**PROJECT EVIDENCE: the race is systemic.**
- Across 10-11 and 10-12 there were 10,467 boundary pairs of a 1m signal and the 5m signal for the same close (I2-005).
- In **8,998 (86%)** the 5m was generated after the 1m.
- In **3,331 (32%)** the newly closed 5m changed decision compared with the prior 5m.
- At a boundary, the 1m-driven routes therefore usually see the superseded 5m.

**PROJECT EVIDENCE: Replay orders signals deterministically.**
- Replay sets `evaluationTime` to the exact candle close (`RegressionTestWorker.java:584`), so a 1m and a 5m closing together get identical `generatedAt`.
- Ties sort 1h, then 5m, then 1m (`:557-560`, `:787-791`).
- `ExecutionReplayScope.recent` is inclusive (`!isAfter(ref)`, `ExecutionReplayScope.java:112`).
- So in Replay, the 22:40 1m sees the 22:35–22:40 5m (WATCH). `isBullish(WATCH)` is false, so the `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR` route should return null.

**AGENT INFERENCE:**
- **Trade 143 is likely not reproducible in Replay.** The Production decision depended on a 2 ms wall-clock race that Replay structurally removes.
- **Trade 158 should also diverge.** The wake-up path is driven by the 5m signal and looks for a 1m at or before the 5m's `generatedAt` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:420-424`). In Replay the 02:35 5m is processed before the 02:35 1m, so it would find the 02:34 1m instead of 165412.
- **There is a precedent.** FIX-141 already moved the BTC lookup to candle-open-time ordering for this same reason (`ExecutionReplayScope.java:122-137`). The 5m and 1h execution lookups were not moved.

**Status:** candidate defect, not yet confirmed. It needs a Replay run over the 143 and 158 windows, plus I3-003.
