## FINDINGS (iteration 1, provisional)

**F-1 (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE): Five of six exits came from multi-timeframe sell logic, not from a hard stop.**
- None of the six is a stop-loss loss. All P&L is small: −0.032 to −0.075 USDT, gross. Automatic trades record fee = 0 (claude-docs observation 2), so net losses are larger by an amount not yet computed.
- These trades are "thesis-invalidated" exits above the stop. Whether each one was a valid losing trade or premature exit noise needs the 5m and 1h signal state at the exit tick.

**F-2 (AGENT INFERENCE, needs evidence): Fast exits on trades 137, 143 and 182 may mean the 5m/1h context turned bearish almost right after entry, or was already borderline.**
- Trade 137 closed at 21:00:12 UTC, 12 s after a 1h/5m boundary. That fits a new 5m or 1h signal flipping the exit.
- Entry validation has `5M_BEARISH_VETO` and `1H_BEARISH_VETO`. A SELL_CONFIRMED exit 5–8 minutes after entry means 5m flipped bearish within one or two 5m closes.
- If the 5m/1h signals used at exit predate the entry, that is a consistency defect between entry and exit validation.

**F-3 (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE): Trade 143 is an anomaly to explain: a "hard floor" exit with a loss.**
- The floor is entry × 1.0005, which is above entry, yet realized_pnl is negative.
- It needs one of two things: a ≥70% TP-progress lock (or a TP-extension rebase) within 3.5 minutes, then a fill below entry; or a signal-path close at a stale `latestPrice`.
- The candidate explanations are gap-through slippage, the signal-path latestPrice, or the floor being compared against a different entry basis (for example a progressive add changing the average entry). **Not yet resolved.**

**F-4 (PROJECT EVIDENCE): Production and Replay check sell-context freshness differently.**
- The production live normal exit does not check freshness. Replay's sell validation does: 5m ≤ 20 min, 1h ≤ 3 h.
- For any of the four live-path exits (182, 172, 158, 137), a stale 5m or 1h signal would trigger an exit in production that Replay would reject.
- This is a code-level fact. Whether it affected these trades depends on DB-I1-004.

**F-5 (PROJECT EVIDENCE): The selection rule leaves out every stop-loss loser.**
- The rule `status=CLOSED` excludes all 75 STOPPED rows, every one of which is a loser (DBQ-FTS-002).
- So the study covers only "soft-exit" losers. This is a scope note, not a substitution; the frozen set stays as given.

**F-6 (AGENT INFERENCE): Most entries open within 4–6 s of a 1m close.**
- Trades 158, 143, 137 and 172 opened at :x5:04, :x0:04, :x5:04 and :36:05, which fits normal 1m-close latency.
- Three of those (158, 143, 137) land on 5-minute boundaries, which may mean the 5m close is what releases the 1m entry. To be verified.

**F-7 (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE): EGLDUSDT re-entered after a loss.**
- Trade 137 closed at 21:00:12 UTC; trade 143 opened on the same symbol 1h40m later and lost again.
- I need to check whether any re-entry cooldown or FIX-122 stop-loss evidence exclusion should have applied. FIX-122 covers stop-loss only, and 137 was not a stop.

**F-8 (PROJECT EVIDENCE): All six losers are 5+ days old.**
- The newest one closed 2026-10-12 10:05 UTC, but the snapshot is 2026-10-17 07:00 UTC.
- Either no position has closed with a loss since then, or trading/ingestion has been idle. Needs a recency check (DB-I1-008).

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-004
Purpose: Latest 1m/5m/1h signal at or before entry and at or before exit. This reproduces what the live path loaded (LivePositionProtectionService.java:95-97) — U-3, F-2, F-4.

SELECT p.id AS position_id, x.phase, x.ref_time, iv.interval_code, s.id AS signal_id, s.decision, s.signal_mode, s.generated_at, s.candle_open_time, s.latest_price, TIMESTAMPDIFF(SECOND, s.generated_at, x.ref_time) AS age_seconds FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p JOIN (SELECT 'ENTRY' AS phase UNION ALL SELECT 'EXIT') ph JOIN (SELECT '1m' AS interval_code UNION ALL SELECT '5m' UNION ALL SELECT '1h') iv CROSS JOIN LATERAL (SELECT ph.phase AS phase, CASE WHEN ph.phase='ENTRY' THEN p.opened_at ELSE p.closed_at END AS ref_time) x LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal s ON s.id = (SELECT t.id FROM crypto_ai.trade_signal t WHERE t.symbol = p.symbol AND t.interval_code = iv.interval_code AND t.generated_at <= x.ref_time ORDER BY t.generated_at DESC LIMIT 1) WHERE p.id IN (182,172,162,158,143,137) ORDER BY p.id, x.phase, iv.interval_code;
