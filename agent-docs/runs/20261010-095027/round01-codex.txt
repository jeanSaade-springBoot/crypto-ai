## OBJECTIONS

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** RPR-0.4 does not meet the required implementation-contract granularity. RPR-01 through RPR-12 are work areas, not a complete modify/create/delete/config/migration/test registry; neither `ShadowProductionReplayService`, `RegressionTestWorker`, `RegressionTestService`, controller/UI, wallet services, observers, repositories, nor exact migrations are assigned authorized method-level changes. This permits unregistered refactoring and prevents later reconciliation. [agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md](C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md:218)

- **AGENT INFERENCE:** “Complete price coverage” is not operationally defined. The draft requires every needed observation and gaps, but defines neither expected event cadence, maximum permitted gap, WebSocket-disconnect evidence, nor how a missing observation is detected. An accepted-event count cannot prove that no unrecorded price would have crossed TP/SL/profit-lock. A parity certificate could therefore be falsely positive. [agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md](C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md:107)

- **AGENT INFERENCE:** The pre-execution rejection contract is incomplete. Eligibility requires coverage from the first Replay open through terminal close, but those boundaries emerge only after signal generation and shadow execution. The specification must define a pure, non-persisting preflight/dry-run phase, its inputs, and refusal behavior; otherwise it conflicts with its own “before generated signals or shadow wallet state are written” requirement. [agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md](C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md:93)

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** The certificate lacks a defined oracle. “Field comparisons” does not specify the compared production records, identity keys, fields, precision/tolerance, missing-record handling, configuration identity, or how a virtual account can be compared with a deliberately non-reproduced historical wallet. This is material because the current Replay has a synthetic account and one-symbol lifecycle. [agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md](C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md:232), [ShadowProductionReplayService.java](C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/regression/service/ShadowProductionReplayService.java:75)

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** Deterministic ordering remains under-specified. The draft says to use event IDs and a tie order, but does not define ordering among simultaneous 1m/5m/1h signals, signal/work commit sequence, or the schema/API change needed to retain the required identifiers. Current Replay’s `PriceEvent` has no ID. [agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md](C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/agent-docs/FINAL_CHANGE_SPECIFICATION.md:187), [MarketPriceEventService.java](C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/market/service/MarketPriceEventService.java:173)
