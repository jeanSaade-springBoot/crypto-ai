**F-31: These entry routes have no minimum reward/risk.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE] Severity: high, design gap. It explains part of trades 137, 158 and 182.
- The only place `IMMEDIATE_VALIDATION`, `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR`, `SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP` and `ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE` use reward/risk is the Entry Quality score (`ExecutionIntelligenceService:991-993`). There, a value below 1 subtracts 30 points.
- Only the breakout-retracement and reversal-retracement routes enforce a real minimum (lines 1539 and 1631).
- The ACE (182) arithmetic works as follows:
  - Taking 30 points off 100 still leaves 70.
  - The penalty for a recent rejected high cannot push the score below the cut-off of 50 (lines 995-1002).
  - So a trade whose target is half the size of its stop can still reach the minimum score of 50 needed for FIX-11G.
- [AGENT INFERENCE] The two trades that scored exactly 50 (162 and 182) fit that floor, but the code does not record the score's components. See DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-003 and the log request.
- Trade 158 entered with reward/risk 0.33. The wake-up route checks the 1-minute signal's freshness, entry permission and ATR permission (lines 422-427). It never checks reward/risk.

**F-32: Trade 162. The earlier BUY was probably blocked as too late, and that same BUY then unlocked a weaker entry one minute later.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE for the code paths; the reason 167646 was blocked is AGENT INFERENCE] Severity: high, the routes contradict each other.
- **Inputs at 01:01 UTC (04:01 KSA):**
  - 1-minute signal 167646 was BUY 76, `STANDARD_ENTRY`, with final entry and ATR entry both allowed.
  - The 5-minute and 1-hour signals were both WATCH.
- **Why it did not execute:**
  - The direct route `validateBuy` → `balancedBuy` returns `BALANCED_EARLY` when both timeframes are WATCH (lines 314-316).
  - FIX-041 (`ExecutionIntelligenceService:672-684`) then holds back any BALANCED_EARLY entry graded `LATE_ENTRY`.
  - That is the most likely reason 167646 did not execute.
- **What happened next, at 01:02 UTC (04:02 KSA):**
  - Signal 167668, a WATCH, entered through `ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE`.
  - It had the **same BALANCED_EARLY permission and the same LATE_ENTRY 50 grade** that FIX-041 blocks on the direct route.
- **Why the late-entry check did not stop it:**
  - FIX-119 (`applyAccumulatedLateEntryTimingGuard`, lines 1271-1279) normally defers a late accumulated entry.
  - It is bypassed when the opportunity's evidence includes at least one BUY.
  - The opportunity counted "1 BUY, 9 WATCH" (row 37952), and that one BUY is almost certainly 167646.
- **Net effect:** the direct route refused a late, early-profile entry, and the opportunity-memory route then entered the same setup, one minute later, at a higher price (0.08705 vs 0.08693).
- **What proves it:** for 167646, a `buy_htf_assessment_audit` row with path `DIRECT_BUY` and final result `BALANCED_EARLY` would show that the profile check passed. The blocking step afterwards is only stored in an opportunity row that was later overwritten. See DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-001.

**F-33: Trades 182 and 137 both entered through the FIX-11G 25% rule, and that rule ignores reward/risk.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
- The FIX-11G rule (lines 263-284) requires:
  - final entry allowed on the 1-minute signal;
  - Entry Quality of at least 50;
  - no BTC conflict, strong conflict or stale BTC context.
- The code comment says deliberately "Do NOT add a second confidence threshold."
- In 182 the earlier BUY 176692 (06:56 UTC / 09:56 KSA) was not executed, but 176710 a minute later was. The 5-minute and 1-hour inputs were the same for both, since no new 5-minute signal had closed.
- The likeliest cause is that 176692's Entry Quality was below 50, so FIX-11G was "not granted" for it. The code logs that decision at INFO level (lines 289-295).
- Two of the six losses came through this one rule. Whether that is unusual across all trades is unknown; see DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-002.

**F-34: Replay sees the same-candle 5-minute signal, Production usually does not. This is a structural Replay/Production gap.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE] This settles U-14 at the code level.
- **Replay:**
  - Every Replay signal's `generatedAt` is set to the candle close (`RegressionTestWorker:584, 616`).
  - The Replay timeline is ordered by close time, then 1h → 5m → 1m (lines 557-560 and 787-793).
  - So the 5-minute signal for a boundary is added before the 1-minute signal, with an **equal** timestamp. The `!isAfter(ref)` check (`ExecutionReplayScope:112`) therefore includes it.
- **Production:** generation uses the wall clock, and in 28 of 32 cases the 5-minute signal was generated *after* the 1-minute one (I2-008), so it is excluded.
- **Consequence:** where both candles close at the same moment, Replay decides against the current 5-minute bar and Production against the previous one (up to 5 minutes older). The "fresh" limit is 20 minutes (`FIVE_MINUTE_MAX_AGE`, lines 66 and 42).
- **Cases where the recorded 5-minute input differs from the same-candle 5-minute signal:** at least five, not counting the wake-up routes, whose trigger is the newer 5-minute signal by design (I3-006):

  | Position | Route | 5-minute used | Same-candle 5-minute |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 100 | — | STRONG_BUY | WATCH |
  | 123 | — | BUY | WATCH |
  | 143 | — | BUY | WATCH |
  | 148 | SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR | WATCH | BUY |
  | 175 | SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR | NEUTRAL | BUY |
  | 179 | IMMEDIATE_VALIDATION | WATCH | BUY |

  - In 143, Replay would see WATCH. `setupTimeframeAtrAuthoritySignal` requires a bullish 5-minute signal (line 1486), so the route would return nothing. Trade 143 would most likely not happen in Replay.
  - The gap cuts both ways: positions 148, 175 and 179 missed a newer bullish 5-minute signal.
- Still unverified by an actual Replay run.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-002
Purpose: Win/loss and P&L by entry route and decision code, for every position opened since 10-11 (are FIX-11G and the other routes over-represented among losers?)
SQL:
SELECT o.execution_source, o.decision_code, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(p.realized_pnl < 0) AS losers, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl),4) AS pnl_sum, ROUND(AVG(p.realized_pnl),4) AS pnl_avg

GROUP BY o.execution_source, o.decision_code
