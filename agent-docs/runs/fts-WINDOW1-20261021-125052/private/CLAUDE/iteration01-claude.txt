## FINDINGS

**F-01: The snapshot's KSA time doesn't match its UTC time.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
- The charter says snapshot UTC `2026-10-22 10:28:18` and snapshot KSA `2026-10-21 12:50:52`. UTC+3 would be **2026-10-22 13:28:18 KSA**.
- `DATABASE_QUERY_LOG.md` agrees with the UTC value: DBQ-FTS-001…005 ran from `2026-10-22T13:28:13+03:00` to `13:28:18+03:00`.
- The "KSA" value looks like the run-ID clock (`20261021-125052`), not the time the snapshot was taken. [AGENT INFERENCE]
- This doesn't change the frozen IDs, but the window metadata isn't reliable.

**F-02: The selection leaves out all stop-loss losses.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
- DBQ-FTS-002 shows 75 `STOPPED` rows, and all 75 have negative P&L. Only 26 of the 118 `CLOSED` rows are negative.
- The code shows why: `LivePositionProtectionService.java:178` and `PaperTradingService.java:183` close stop-loss exits as `PositionStatus.STOPPED`.
- So `status='CLOSED'` picks only signal-driven and profit-lock losses. The six trades are not "the six latest failed trades". Any `STOPPED` loss closed after 2026-10-11 20:55 UTC was skipped.
- I'm not substituting trades (frozen set), but the comparative study must say this.

**F-03: The newest selected loss is about 10 days older than the snapshot.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE + INFERENCE]
- The newest `closed_at` is `2026-10-12 10:05:20 UTC`, and the snapshot was taken on 2026-10-22.
- Possible reasons: no trading since then, only profitable or `STOPPED` closes since then, or the database is a stale copy. I can't tell which yet (DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-003).

**F-04: Close reasons tell us which code path closed each trade.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE, code]
- `SELL_CONFIRMED` and `HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED` come only from `PositionExitPolicy.evaluateNormalExit` (`PositionExitPolicy.java:29,42`). It runs on the live 1m tick path (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:235-238`). Trades 182, 158 and 137 use the first; trade 172 uses the second.
- `STRONG_SELL` (trade 162) comes from the signal path: `PaperTradingService.java:197-208`, `closeFromSignal(... signal.getDecision().name())`, after `validateSell`.
- `PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT` (trade 143) can come from either path: `LivePositionProtectionService.java:195,213` or `PaperTradingService.java:193`.

**F-05: The live exit path uses the newest 1m, 5m and 1h signals without checking their age.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE, code; impact unconfirmed]
- `LivePositionProtectionService.java:95-97` takes `findTopBySymbolAndIntervalOrderByGeneratedAtDesc` for each interval.
- `evaluateNormalExit` doesn't check how old those signals are. A 1h SELL that is up to about an hour old can trigger `HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED` (`PositionExitPolicy.java:25-28`).
- This also applies to a 1h SELL that already existed when the position opened. That raises a question for trade 172: was the 1h signal already bearish at entry? If so, the BUY was admitted against a bearish 1h, or the 1h turned within the 40-minute hold.
- This is a hypothesis to test, not a defect.

**F-06: Entries are 4–6 seconds after a minute boundary.** [AGENT INFERENCE]
- All six `opened_at` values fall at hh:mm:04–06 UTC. That fits 1m-close-driven entries with about 4–6 s from candle close to entry.
- This needs signal and work timestamps to confirm.

**F-07: Four of the six holds are short.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: timing]
- Holds were 3m31s (143), 5m08s (137) and 8m14s (182). Trade 162 was 23m58s, 172 was 39m40s and 158 was 55m02s.
- Exiting on a confirmed SELL within 3–8 minutes of a BUY suggests the entry admitted a BUY while the 5m was already weakening, or it chased a spike. [AGENT INFERENCE, to be tested against the 5m/1h signals available at entry]

**F-08: Trade 143 lost money on a hard-floor exit that should only fire at a small profit.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE + INFERENCE] **Highest priority.**
- The hard floor is `entry × 1.0005` (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:210`).
- For this exit the profit lock had to be active within 3.5 minutes of entry, which needs 70% progress toward the take-profit.
- A loss on this exit means one of four things:
  - a price jump through the floor, filling at the tick price below entry;
  - the floor being measured against a different entry price than the paper P&L (wallet average vs paper entry);
  - a fill and P&L mismatch;
  - a logic defect.
- Two earlier observations bear on this but are unconfirmed (INVESTIGATION_LOG items 3 and 7): sizing differs between the paper row and the wallet, and a wallet exit failure doesn't block the paper close.

**F-09: Trade 137 is EGLDUSDT again, and trade 143 opened 1h40m after it closed.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
- Two losses in a row on the same symbol.
- Check whether a same-symbol re-entry guard was evaluated. The `crypto_account_configuration` limits aren't read by the paper entry path (INVESTIGATION_LOG item 4). [AGENT INFERENCE]

**F-10: The working tree has a FIX-154 migration, while AGENTS.md calls FIX-154 not implemented.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
