## How the score is built [PROJECT EVIDENCE, `ExecutionIntelligenceService:871-1003`]

The I5 errors came from not checking two of these rules:

- **Recent low.** The score's reference low is the lowest price among the newest 20 one-minute signals within 30 minutes. If a still-fresh opportunity (≤120 min) has a lower anchor or best price, that is used instead.
- **Recent high, and when the rejected-high penalty can apply.**
  - The recent high is updated only by a **strictly higher** price (line 888).
  - If the current price is the highest in the window, the recent high's time is the current signal's own time, and the check `recentHighAt.isBefore(now)` fails (line 962).
  - So **a trade entering at its window high can never get the rejected-high penalty.**
- **Opportunity age** is `Duration.toMinutes()`, which drops partial minutes (line 950).
- **Stored evidence:** when the penalty applies, `applyInitialEntryQualityGuard` adds the text "recent rejection zone near …" to the explanation (lines 1208-1210). Explanations without that text are direct evidence the penalty did not fire.
