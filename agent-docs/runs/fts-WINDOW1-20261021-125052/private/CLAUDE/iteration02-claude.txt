**F-11: Five of six entries had planned reward/risk below 1, because of a 0.50% minimum stop with a 3×ATR target.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE + arithmetic] Severity: high, systemic.
- `AtrRiskService.java:35-45` sets the stop distance to the larger of 2×ATR and the minimum stop (`ATR_MINIMUM_STOP_PERCENT:0.50`, `application.yml:143`). The target stays at 3×ATR.
- When ATR is below 0.25% of price, reward/risk is therefore below 1.5, and it shrinks as ATR falls.
- Stored `trade_signal.risk_reward_ratio` (DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-007):

- The paper rows' stop-loss and take-profit values reproduce these distances exactly (DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-002).

- Some entry routes in `ExecutionIntelligenceService` require an Entry Quality reward/risk of 1.15–1.25 (lines 1055, 1098, 1128). That is a different calculation from the stored signal value. Which reward/risk each entry was actually checked against is still unknown (U-10).
- [AGENT INFERENCE] With these plans, the trades needed win rates of about 57–75% just to break even before fees. This is entry-plan design and is the main driver of the losses. It is not one bad trade.

**F-12: Fees are not modelled, and several targets are smaller than realistic round-trip fees.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH]

- [EXTERNAL RESEARCH: Binance standard spot fee is about 0.1% per side, so about 0.2% per round trip. I did not verify this in this iteration.] On that basis, a take-profit win on 158 (0.16%) would have been net negative, and on 162 (0.22%) or 182 (0.24%) about break-even.
- Net losses are understated by roughly 0.03–0.05 USDT per trade.

**F-13: Trade 143's entry used a 5m signal that had already been replaced.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE for the code path and timestamps; AGENT INFERENCE for the selection] Severity: highest. Likely defect, to be confirmed with I2-001 and I2-002.
- The entry 1m signal was 159408: WATCH, `atr_entry_type=WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, recommended position 0%, `atr_immediate_entry_allowed=0`. It also said to wait for a pullback to about 4.0118. RSI was 84.3, and price was 4.35 ATR above SMA20 and above the upper Bollinger band.
- Even so, the position opened at 4.033 with 25% allocation and `entry_stage=SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR`.
- That route (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1472-1514`) lets a fresh, bullish, entry-allowed 5m signal stand in when the 1m is ATR-extended. It finds that 5m signal with `latestAtOrBefore`, which compares `generated_at` (wall-clock time), not candle time (lines 2455-2462 and 2606-2608).
- The two signals for the same 22:40:00 UTC candle close:
  - 1m signal 159408: generated 22:40:01.633945 UTC (01:40:01 KSA).
  - 5m signal 159407 (WATCH 76): generated 22:40:01.635944 UTC, **2 ms later**.
- So the query would have skipped 159407 and picked 5m signal 159285 (BUY 83). That signal was generated at 22:35:01.55 UTC (01:35:01 KSA) and is within the 20-minute limit (`FIVE_MINUTE_MAX_AGE`, line 66).
- [AGENT INFERENCE] The entry's authority came from a 5m BUY that the newly closed 5m candle had already downgraded to WATCH. Which signal wins depends on a millisecond race between threads.
- Replay picks signals through `replayScope.latestAtOrBefore`, which may order ties differently, so this may not reproduce in Replay (U-14).
