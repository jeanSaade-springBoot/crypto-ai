- **A floor on the rejected-high penalty can hold a score at exactly 50.** Trade 162 provably landed on it, and trade 182 probably did. The code's own FIX-119 comment calls this pattern a defect, but the fix covers only one route.

## How Entry Quality is scored [PROJECT EVIDENCE, `ExecutionIntelligenceService:865-1030`]

The score starts at 100. These terms adjust it:

| Term | Effect on the score |
|---|---|
| Distance above the recent low (%) | −10 above 1.5%, −22 above 3%, −35 above 5%, −50 above 8% |
| Distance above the recent low, in ATRs | −10 above 2.5, −20 above 4, −30 above 6 |
| Age of the opportunity | −5 above 20 min, −10 above 40 min, −15 above 60 min |
| Reward/risk | **−30 below 1**, −15 below 1.25, +5 at 2 or more |
| Near a recent rejected high | −12, but **never below 50** if the score was 50 or more before this penalty (lines 995-1002) |
| `HIGH_VOLATILITY` regime | −10 |
| `STOP_EXPOSED` liquidity | −15 |
| `BREAKOUT` regime, if the distance is at most 3% and at most 2.5 ATR | +5 |

- **Hard block:** below 50 (`CHASE_ENTRY_CUTOFF`, line 229).
- **Size cap** (line 1183): 50% at 85 or more, 40% at 70 or more, otherwise 25%.
- **Grades:** 85+ excellent, 70+ good, 55+ acceptable, 50-54 late, below 50 chase.

- This replaces part of F-31: reward/risk below 1 cannot block alone, and the floor then keeps an entry that is also extended and near a rejected high exactly at the cut-off.

**F-44: The "30-minute" look-back actually covers only the last 20 signals.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE] Severity: low, affects parity.

SQL:
