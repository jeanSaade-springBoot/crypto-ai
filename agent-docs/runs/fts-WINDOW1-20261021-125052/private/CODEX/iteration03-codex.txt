- Position **182** is associated in logs with `ICPUSDT`.
- Its apparent entry path was driven by 1m signal `164053`:
  - Candle close UTC `2026-10-11 22:40:59.999`; KSA `2026-10-12 01:40:59.999`.
  - Signal generated UTC `22:41:01.677`; KSA `01:41:01.677` — 1.678 s after candle close.
  - Signal row creation UTC `22:41:02.996`; KSA `01:41:02.996` — 2.997 s after candle close.
  - Execution evidence logged `BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION_ENTRY allowed=true` at UTC `22:41:03.047`, followed by its price recheck at `22:41:03.060`.
  - The execution transaction completed UTC `22:41:04.863`; KSA `01:41:04.863`, with `paperPositionOpened=true`.
- The first position-specific protection log is UTC `22:41:05.405`; KSA `01:41:05.405`: position 182, entry `3.493`, target `3.537460443272`, current `3.494`. A second at UTC `22:41:07.414`; KSA `01:41:07.414` records current `3.497`; the profit lock remained inactive.
- Entry contention was observed but recovered:
  - A protection callback observed UTC `22:41:03.516`; KSA `01:41:03.516` waited 1,401 ms for the ICP symbol lock.
  - The entry-side symbol transaction held that lock for 1,859 ms.
  - The protection event then committed `APPLIED` as event `1835452`; its protection stage returned in 1,416 ms.
  - This proves a short protection delay during entry serialization, not a missed or failed callback.
- The likely stop-loss sequence for 182 is:
  - `market_price_event` `1837489` was logged `APPLIED` with observation UTC `2026-10-11 22:49:33.460`; KSA `2026-10-12 01:49:33.460`.
  - `Live STOP_LOSS executed` for ICPUSDT at UTC `22:49:36.971`; KSA `01:49:36.971`, price `3.465000000000`, stop `3.465026371152`.
  - The price was below the logged stop by `0.000026371152` (about `0.000761%`).
  - The symbol transaction committed 2,438 ms later in the same logged stop path; price-event `1837489` was recorded `APPLIED` at UTC `22:49:36.974`; KSA `01:49:36.974`.
- The observation-to-commit interval for that stop path is about 3.514 s. The log attributes 2.438 s to the symbol transaction; the remaining roughly 1.076 s is not decomposed in the available logs.

**AGENT INFERENCE**

- Log order strongly indicates that the ICPUSDT stop-loss line belongs to position 182: position 182 is the only frozen position correlated with ICPUSDT in the available archive and was recorded open immediately before the stop path. This still requires database linkage confirmation.
- No timing evidence currently establishes a defect. The entry-time lock wait and stop-path latency are real, but both paths ended with committed/APPLIED evidence.
