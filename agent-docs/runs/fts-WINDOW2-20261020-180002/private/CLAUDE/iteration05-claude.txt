## FINDINGS

### F27. How the entry-quality score is built (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

`ExecutionIntelligenceService.assessEntryQuality` (`EIS:865-1030`) starts at 100 and adjusts:

| Factor | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Expansion above the reference | −10 / −22 / −35 / −50 at >1.5 / >3 / >5 / >8 % |
| ATR extension | −10 / −20 / −30 at >2.5 / >4 / >6 ATR |
| Opportunity age | −5 / −10 / −15 at >20 / >40 / >60 min |
| **R:R below 1** | **−30** (1–1.25: −15; ≥2: +5) |
| Recent rejected high | −12, but **floored at 50** if the score was ≥50 before this penalty (`:995-1003`) |
| HIGH_VOLATILITY regime | −10 |
| STOP_EXPOSED liquidity | −15 |
| BREAKOUT regime, small extension | +5 |

**Classes:** ≥85 EXCELLENT, ≥70 GOOD, ≥55 ACCEPTABLE, ≥50 LATE, otherwise CHASE. `CHASE_ENTRY_CUTOFF=50` (`:229`).

**The guard** (`:1171-1223`):
- It blocks only below 50.
- Otherwise it caps size: 50% at ≥85, 40% at ≥70, 25% otherwise.

**Inputs that matter here:**
- The reference low is the lowest **persisted 1m signal `latest_price`** in the last 30 minutes, or the fresh opportunity anchor or best price if lower (`:871-933`).
- These are close-price samples, not candle lows or highs.

**Two scores exist per trade:**
- A decision-time score, at the signal price. On the setup-ATR and wakeup routes it uses the setup timeframe's ATR.
- The recorded `wallet_managed_position.entry_quality_score`, which is `assessEntryQualityAtPrice` at the fill price and always uses the **1m** ATR (`PaperTradingService.java:349-385`, `EIS:857-858`).

**Consequence:**
- An R:R below 1 costs 30 of the 50 points of headroom, so on its own it **never blocks** an entry. It only lowers the size cap.
- Trades 137, 158, 162, 172 and 182 all had R:R below 1 (iteration-2 F4), so every one carried the −30.

### F31. 162: BALANCED_EARLY plus LATE_ENTRY is blocked on the direct route but allowed on the accumulated route (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

**The rules:**
- FIX-041 (`EIS:672-684`) blocks `BALANCED_EARLY` with `LATE_ENTRY` on the direct BUY route only.
- FIX-119 (`:1257-1280`) defers an accumulated LATE_ENTRY only when buyCount=0 **and** the 5m is not bullish.

**What happened on 162:**
- HTF authority was BALANCED_EARLY (FIX-154 audit 2199).
- Quality was 50, LATE_ENTRY.
- The evidence had 1 BUY and 9 WATCH, so the single BUY bypassed FIX-119.

**Classification:**
- This is the combination FIX-041 was written to prevent ("BALANCED_EARLY requires an actually early price"). It was admitted through a route that FIX-041 explicitly leaves alone.
- It is a route inconsistency, not a defect against current rules.
- When that one BUY observation occurred is unknown (I5-001).

**Population data (I4-003):** ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE is 31 trades, 6 losses, +3.38 USDT, so the route as a whole is profitable.

### F32. Quality is measured from close-price samples, not where the fill sits in the real range (PROJECT EVIDENCE for code; effect is AGENT INFERENCE)

- Rolling low, high and rejection are derived only from 1m **signal close prices** (`:877-892`).
- Intra-candle extremes and fill location are invisible to the model. Example: 182 filled at 0.1884, the signal candle's high, and the next candle traded lower (F16).
- On low-ATR symbols (XRP 0.055%, DOGE 0.073%, ACE 0.080% ATR), a 0.2–0.3% rise from the 30-minute close-low already exceeds 4 ATR (−20).
- So for this sample, the score is driven by volatility at least as much as by entry location.
- I5-003 will measure where each fill sat in its 30-minute high/low range.
