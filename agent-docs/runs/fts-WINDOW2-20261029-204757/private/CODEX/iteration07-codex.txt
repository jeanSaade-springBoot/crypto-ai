- **PROJECT EVIDENCE — correction to prior hypothesis:** `completeClose(...)` saves `paper_position` before invoking the signal-linked wallet exit. The wallet method can return normally without a SELL when an execution key already exists, no open managed position exists, or quantity is non-positive. This remains a real reconciliation risk for a signal-path close. However, prior wording that a caught wallet exception necessarily leaves a durable closed paper row was too strong: the business body runs inside `SignalProcessingCoordinator`’s `REQUIRES_NEW` transaction, and a propagated transactional exception may mark that transaction rollback-only. Durable rows—not source order alone—must decide this for each trade.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** The same distinction applies to profit-lock closes: `PaperTradingService.closeFromProfitLock(...)` saves the paper close before calling `executeProfitLock(...)` and does not inspect its boolean result. A normal `false` return could leave a paper-only close; an exception requires transaction-outcome evidence.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** `production_exit_audit.close_trigger` is useful route evidence, but it is not financial-settlement proof. Its current write occurs after the wallet call and relies on current code/schema being applicable to the historical trade. Missing audit data cannot by itself prove no exit occurred.

- **PROJECT EVIDENCE:** `market_price_event` status `APPLIED` means the protection callback completed; it does not prove a paper or wallet position existed or closed. It cannot replace managed-position and wallet-trade reconciliation.
