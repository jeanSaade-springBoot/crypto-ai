**AGENT INFERENCE:** In low-volatility conditions the system takes trades whose win rate would need to exceed about 60–75% just to break even, before fees. This is a **strategy/configuration design weakness**, not a code defect: the code does what it is configured to do. It is also not a stale-data problem.

### I2-F02 — Trade 143's entry authority most likely came from a superseded 5m signal (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE; not yet proven)

**Evidence:**
- **The entry path.** Wallet trade 358 was `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR`. That path comes from `setupTimeframeAtrAuthoritySignal` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1472-1492`) and requires `five = latestAtOrBefore(1m)` to be a BUY or STRONG_BUY.
- **The lookup.** It uses `findTop…GeneratedAtLessThanEqual(1m.generatedAt)` (`:2455-2461`, `:2606-2608`).
- **The two 5m candidates:**
  - 1m #159408 was generated at 22:40:01.633945 UTC.
  - 5m #159407 (candle 22:35, closed 22:40:00, **WATCH**) was generated at 22:40:01.635944. That is **2.0 ms later**, so the lookup excludes it.
  - 5m #159285 (candle 22:30, **BUY 83**) was generated at 22:35:01.552, about 5 minutes earlier. This is the signal `latestAtOrBefore` returns.
- **The confluence record agrees.** The 1m entry reason records `5m=BUY (trend 18/25)`, which matches the old candle, not the current WATCH.
- **The current 5m would have blocked the entry.** `isBullish(WATCH)` is false (`:2634`), so with #159407 the route returns null.
- **The 5m wake-up path is excluded.** It needs the triggering 5m to be bullish (`:401`, `:295`), and #159407 was WATCH.

**The 1m signal's own ATR plan said not to enter now:** `WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, "Recommended position is 0%", retrace target ≈4.0118, RSI 84.3, price 4.35 ATR above SMA20. The route is designed to override that 1m ATR deferral when the 5m allows entry. Here the override rested on a 5m candle that had already been replaced.

**The race is non-deterministic:** at 5-minute boundaries the 5m signal is sometimes generated before the 1m and sometimes after. 137 at 20:55 and 162 at 04:05 had 5m first; 143 at 22:40 and 158 at 02:35 had 5m after.

**Conclusion:** This is a **candidate logic defect**: a boundary race in higher-timeframe selection. It is not yet confirmed, because the `confluence_higher_signal_generated_at` evidence and #159285's executability flags are still missing (see I2-001 and I2-002).

**Context:**
- The previous EGLD position (wallet position 171) closed on `PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT` at 3.996 at 22:39:41 UTC, on a one-tick dip: 4.040 → 3.996 → 4.021.
- 143 then re-entered 22 s later at 4.033, which is 0.93% higher.

### I2-F03 — Trade 143's loss on a profit-lock exit came from a price gap; protection behaved as coded (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-005, I1-008, I1-009, I1-006)

**Sequence (UTC → KSA):**
1. 22:40:23 → 01:40:23 — profit lock activated.
2. 22:40:41 and 22:41:13 — TP extended twice (events #51, #52): 4.0647 → 4.0805 → 4.1043.
3. 22:41:55 → 01:41:55 — highest price 4.101 (95.35% of the extended TP distance). Lock set at 4.07579; hard floor at entry×1.0005 = 4.035017.
4. Ticks: 22:43:27.038 at **4.100**, then 22:43:35.021 at **4.027**. Nothing was recorded in between.
   - That is −1.78% in 8 s. It jumped through the lock (4.0758), the floor (4.0350) and entry (4.033) in one step.
   - The next ticks kept falling: 4.018, 4.016, 4.007, then 3.994 at 22:43:45.
5. The wallet sold at 22:43:35.045, 24 ms after the 4.027 tick (`POSITION:172:PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT`). This was the first observed tick, and the exit price equals the tick price.

**Classification:** a gap loss, consistent with the coded rule (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:210-213`). It is not a delay: the reaction to the tick was immediate.

**Open question:** EGLD ticks normally arrive about every 2 s, but none exist at :29, :31 or :33. That could be ordinary Binance coalescing when nothing changed, or an ingestion pause (I2-004, I2-005).

**Rule note:** the profit lock gives no price guarantee. A tick-based paper fill gives back the whole lock on a gap. That is realistic for a market exit, but the "protected-profit" wording overstates the guarantee.
