## FINDINGS

### I7-F01: Low planned reward/risk did not cause losses in paper trading (SELF-CORRECTION; PROJECT EVIDENCE: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-001, I6-004)

**What still holds (I6-004):** the mechanism is exact. For each of 137, 158, 162, 172 and 182, the stop is 0.5000% below entry and the stored `risk_reward_ratio` equals 6 × ATR% to four decimals. Trade 143 is the exception: its stop came from 2 × ATR (0.5406%) and its reward/risk was 1.5000.

**What is refuted (I6-001, all 193 closed or stopped positions):**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

AGENT INFERENCE, based on the arithmetic of the configured stop rule:
- When ATR is low, the 0.50% floor puts the stop many ATRs away. For trade 182 it was about 6 ATR; a normal stop is 2 ATR. So these trades rarely hit their stop and usually exit on a sell signal or a profit lock.
- In fee-free paper trading, the floor therefore helps.
- My iteration 6 claim (I6-F01) that this was "ROOT CAUSE rank 1" was wrong. The break-even table there assumed outcomes split only between target and stop, but most of these trades exited on signals instead.

**What remains (AGENT INFERENCE, not yet measured):**
- With a fee of 0.10% per side (secondary sources, I6), a round trip costs 0.2% of trade size.
- At 15–25 USDT per trade (I2), that is roughly 0.03–0.05 USDT per trade. Across 54 trades that would likely erase the +0.234 USDT surplus.
- So low reward/risk is a **risk to profit under real fees**, not the reason these paper trades lost. DBQ-CLAUDE-I7-001 measures it.

### I7-F02: The Entry Quality inversion is largely explained by ATR% and reward/risk (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I6-001; mechanism from I5-F02)

SELECT CASE WHEN s.risk_reward_ratio < 0.75 THEN 'a<0.75' WHEN s.risk_reward_ratio < 1 THEN 'b0.75-1' WHEN s.risk_reward_ratio < 1.5 THEN 'c1-1.5' ELSE 'd>=1.5' END AS rr_band, COUNT(*) AS n, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_sum, ROUND(SUM(p.entry_price * p.quantity), 4) AS notional_sum, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl) - 0.002 * SUM(p.entry_price * p.quantity), 6) AS pnl_after_0p2pct_roundtrip FROM paper_position p JOIN trade_signal s ON s.id = p.signal_id WHERE p.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') AND p.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY rr_band ORDER BY rr_band
