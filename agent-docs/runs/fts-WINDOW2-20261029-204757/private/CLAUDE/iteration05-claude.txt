### I5-F01: Every stored Entry Quality score reproduces from code (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-003, I1-004, I1-005, I1-007, I2-001, I2-003, I4-002, I4-006)

**How the score is built** (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:865-1030`):

| Component | Penalty or bonus |
|---|---|
| Starting score | 100 |
| Price expansion above the reference low | >1.5% −10, >3% −22, >5% −35, >8% −50 |
| ATR extension above the reference low | >2.5 −10, >4 −20, >6 −30 |
| Opportunity age in whole minutes, `toMinutes()` | >20 −5, >40 −10, >60 −15 |
| Reward/risk at the given price | <1 −30, <1.25 −15, ≥2 +5 |
| Recent rejected high (FIX-091) | −12, but clamped at 50 if the score was already ≥50 (`:995-1003`) |
| HIGH_VOLATILITY regime | −10 |
| STOP_EXPOSED liquidity | −15 |
| BREAKOUT regime, small extension | +5 |

- **Reference low:** the lowest price among the recent 1m signals. A fresh opportunity's anchor or best price can lower it further (`:877-933`).
- **Fill-time recheck:** it rescores at the fill price using the 1m ATR (`revalidateAtExecutionPrice` at `:1167`).
- **What gets stored:** `PaperTradingService.java:377-385` stores `assessEntryQualityAtPrice(fill)`. So `wallet_managed_position.entry_quality_score` is the fill-time score.

### I5-F02: The reward/risk penalty dominates but cannot block (DESIGN WEAKNESS; PROJECT EVIDENCE; corrects I4-F07)

- In five of six trades, a reward/risk between 0.33 and 0.74 cost exactly 30 points, the same as a reward/risk of 0.99 would. The penalty has no gradation below 1.
- With reward/risk below 1 and nothing else wrong, the score is 70 (GOOD_ENTRY). Blocking needs at least 21 more penalty points.
- Size caps barely differentiate either (`:1183`): every score below 70 gets the same 25% cap. All six trades were sized at 25% before the ATR multiplier, which brought 162 and 182 down to 15%.
- **Correction to I4-F07:** "never reads `rewardRisk()`" is literally true of the guard itself. But reward/risk **is** part of the score, as a soft −30 penalty rather than a minimum.
- The design weakness stands. A 0.33 reward/risk trade (158) needs about a 75% win rate just to break even, and it passed.

### I5-F03: Three entries at the passing threshold; FIX-091 clamp decided trade 182 (DESIGN WEAKNESS; PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- **Trade 182:** 100 − 20 − 30 = 50 before the rejection penalty. The −12 would have made it 38. The clamp `Math.max(CHASE_ENTRY_CUTOFF, score)` (`:1000-1001`) raised it back to 50. The −12 therefore had **zero effect**, and the trade passed both the FIX-11G "≥ 50" rule (I4-F05) and the guard.
- **What was knowable at entry (182):**
  - 17 consecutive 1m STRONG_SELL signals from 09:38 to 09:54.
  - Two BUYs bouncing 0.48% off the low, into a rejected-high zone 0.21% above.
  - WEAK_DOWNTREND regime.
  - The 5m signal was originally STRONG_SELL.
- **Outcome (evaluated separately):** price never traded above the entry.
- The clamp is documented intent ("must not by itself push … below the hard cutoff"), so this is **not a code defect**. Its effect is that rejection evidence is ignored exactly where the score is weakest.
- **Trades 158 and 162:** 50 from several soft penalties, none of which blocks alone.

### I5-F04: Trade 162 escaped an age penalty by 24 ms; Replay most likely blocks it (PARITY RISK; PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE)

- **Age calculation:** age = `Duration.between(opp.startedAt, current.generatedAt).toMinutes()` (`:948-951`).
  - `startedAt` is the creating signal's `generatedAt` (`:2541`). Both values are wall-clock analysis-start times.
  - Opportunity #37952 started at 03:41:02.836972. Signal #167668 was generated at 04:02:02.813094.
  - The gap is 20 min 59.976 s, which rounds down to 20, so there was **no penalty** (">20" is false).
- **Replay:** signals are timed at the candle close, `openTime + interval` (`RegressionTestWorker.java:584`). That gives 03:41:00 to 04:02:00, which is exactly 21 minutes, so the −5 penalty applies.
  - The score becomes 45, below 50, which produces `CHASE_ENTRY_BLOCKED` (`:1173`).
- **Effect:** Production's result depended on a 24 ms difference in dispatch lag (2.837 s versus 2.813 s after the close). The same jitter can flip any minute boundary at 20, 40 or 60 minutes.
- **Classification:** candidate Replay/Production **parity defect** (wall-clock jitter inside a deterministic scoring bucket). This is the second such case after trade 143's 5m race (I3-F02/I3-F03).
- **Not yet proven:** that Replay builds the same opportunity starting at 03:41 (DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-004 looks for existing Replay evidence). Running Replay is out of scope.
