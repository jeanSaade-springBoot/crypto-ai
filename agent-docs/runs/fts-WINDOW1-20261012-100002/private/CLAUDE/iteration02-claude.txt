## FINDINGS

**F-I2-01: The snapshot time and the database clock disagree by at least 15 min 42 s.** *(PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE)*
- DBQ-FTS-005 ran at host time 2026-10-12T10:00:03.79+03:00 (`DATABASE_QUERY_LOG.md`). It already returned trade 172 as CLOSED with `closed_at = 07:15:45.484`.
- At the same time, the run labels the snapshot as 07:00:03 UTC.
- Inside the database, timestamps are consistent with Binance: market-price ingest lag is at most 1.9 s, and signals are generated about 1.4 s after candle close.

Either the coordinator host clock is about 16 min or more behind, or the MySQL session time zone isn't UTC. A whole-hour offset is possible; this would mean the displayed values aren't raw UTC. Until DBQ-CLAUDE-I2-001 resolves this, every KSA label in this study is provisional, including the snapshot label "10:00:02 KSA".

**F-I2-02: The frozen sample leaves out every stop-loss loss.** *(PROJECT EVIDENCE: DBQ-FTS-002)*
- STOPPED: 70 rows, all 70 with negative P&L.
- CLOSED: 110 rows, 25 with negative P&L.

The selection rule only takes `status='CLOSED'`, so the 70 STOPPED losses can never be chosen. These six trades describe losses from signal and profit-lock exits only. They say nothing about stop-loss losses. I'm keeping the frozen IDs as instructed, but the comparison should state this bias.

**F-I2-03: The 0.5% minimum stop combined with a 3×ATR target lets in trades with R:R below 1. This is a configuration and design issue, not a code bug.** *(PROJECT EVIDENCE)*
- `AtrRiskService.java:35-45`: `stopDistance = max(2·ATR, 0.5%·price)` while `rewardDistance = 3·ATR` stays unchanged. `application.yml:143` sets `minimum-stop-percent: 0.50`.
- Whenever ATR is below about 0.25% of price, the 0.5% floor applies and R:R falls under 1.5. When ATR is below about 0.167%, R:R falls under 1.
- Trades 137, 158, 162 and 172 all have a stop exactly 0.5% from price, with R:R of 0.64, 0.33, 0.44 and 0.74. Trades 131 and 143, whose ATR was 0.31% and 0.27%, get 1.5.
- The only minimum R:R gate I found is `BREAKOUT_RETRACEMENT_MIN_RR = 1.15` at `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:212`, and it applies to one route only.

**F-I2-04: Trade 143 entered even though the signal said to wait for a retracement. This is a candidate logic defect.** *(PROJECT EVIDENCE: code and data)*
- Signal 159408 had `atr_entry_type=WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, `atr_overextended=1`, RSI 84.3, price 4.35 ATR from SMA20, and the reason text "Recommended position is 0%. Wait for price to retrace near 4.0118".
- It still stored `final_entry_allowed=1` with NORMAL_ENTRY 100%, and the wallet bought 25% of the budget at 4.033.
- Cause: `FinalDecisionService.java:80` only applies the "no immediate entry" downgrade when `isBullish(current)`, which means BUY or STRONG_BUY (`:300-302`). A WATCH signal skips it, and then the WATCH-evidence routes in `ExecutionIntelligenceService` can enter.
- The exact route that opened 143 is still **UNKNOWN**; DBQ-CLAUDE-I2-003 and -008 request it.
- Re-entry came 22 s after the previous EGLD position (wallet position 171) closed with PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT at 22:39:41 UTC. No cooldown is visible.

**F-I2-05: In trade 131 the "protected-profit" floor sits inside a single price tick, so the floor exit realises a loss. Design defect.** *(PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH)*
- The hard floor is entry × 1.0005 (`claude-docs/TRADING_RULES.md:102`): 0.000004210951 × 1.0005 = 0.0000042130564755. This matches the recorded floor exactly.
- PEPEUSDT's tick size is 0.00000001 (Binance exchangeInfo), which is 0.24% of price.
- The floor therefore lies between the 421 and 422 ticks. The first price below the floor is 0.00000421, which is already below the average entry, so a PROFIT_LOCK exit closed at a loss by construction.
- The average entry was raised by a third scale-in at 0.00000424 at 20:29 UTC (signal 156071). That fill was above the original signal take-profit of 0.000004229321. Whether the add was allowed is **UNKNOWN**.

**F-I2-06: Trade 143's profit lock didn't protect the gain; price went past the lock by about 1.2%.** *(PROJECT EVIDENCE)*
- The position peaked at 4.101, which is 95.35% of the way to the extended take-profit of 4.1043.
- The lock level was 4.0758 and the hard floor 4.0350, yet the exit filled at 4.027. That is 8 ticks under the floor and 1.2% under the lock.
- Only 57 price events arrived in 211 s, about one every 3.7 s.
- So either the price gapped between consecutive kline updates, or the lock evaluation was late. DBQ-CLAUDE-I2-004 asks for the event sequence. The trade went from +1.7% to −0.15% in under four minutes.

**F-I2-07: SHIB and PEPE trade below the resolution of their own indicators.** *(PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH)*
- For SHIB in trade 172: one tick = 0.18% of price, and ATR14 = 0.0000000068, about 0.68 of a tick.
- Price stayed between 0.00000548 and 0.00000550, plus or minus one tick, for the whole 40 minutes.
- The take-profit effectively needed 3 ticks (+0.55%). The exit came at −1 tick.
- ATR-based stop, target and extension measures don't mean much at this scale. I also infer that fills don't follow the exchange lot size: quantities such as 6.2516 EGLD don't fit EGLD's 0.01 lot step. This is minor because the trades are paper.

**F-I2-08: Fees are confirmed as zero for all 16 automatic wallet trades, so P&L is gross.** *(PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-007, all `fee_usdt = 0`)*
