## EVIDENCE

### E23: How Entry Quality is scored (PROJECT EVIDENCE, code)

`assessEntryQuality` (`:865-1030`) starts at 100 and subtracts penalties:

| What is measured | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Expansion from the 30-minute low (or the opportunity anchor) | >1.5% −10, >3% −22, >5% −35, >8% −50 |
| ATR extension (distance above that reference, in ATRs) | >2.5 −10, >4 −20, >6 −30 |
| Opportunity age | >20 min −5, >40 min −10, >60 min −15 |
| Reward/risk | **below 1: −30**; below 1.25: −15; 2 or more: +5 |
| Recent rejection of a high nearby | −12, but this penalty can never push the score below 50 (`:995-1003`) |
| BREAKOUT regime with small expansion | +5 |

- A score below 50 (`CHASE_ENTRY_CUTOFF=50`, `:229`) blocks the entry. Scores 50–54 are classed LATE_ENTRY but are still allowed.
- **AGENT INFERENCE:** the reward/risk penalty is flat. A ratio of 0.33 costs exactly the same −30 as 0.99. Because the stop is held at a 0.5% minimum while the target shrinks with ATR (iteration-2 E2), every low-volatility entry starts at 70 at most. Combined with the rejection floor, this lets scores collect exactly at the cutoff of 50.

### E25: Trade 143 has two different Entry Quality scores (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I2-007, I4-004, code)

- The route message says "Entry Quality 60/100 (ACCEPTABLE_ENTRY)". That score was computed at decision time with the **5m** ATR (`:631-635`). The code comment there says the 1m ATR must not be used for this route because it can "falsely label a valid 5m setup" as a chase.
- `wallet_managed_position.entry_quality_score` is **70**. That value comes from `PaperTradingService.java:377-385` → `assessEntryQualityAtPrice` (`:857-858`), which uses the **1m** `getAtrAtSignal()`.
- The execution-time recheck `revalidateAtExecutionPrice` (`:1167-1168`) also uses the **1m** ATR. This applies to every route, including the setup-ATR (`:634`), wake-up (`:439`) and transition (`:615`) routes, which deliberately use the 5m ATR at decision time.
- With a smaller ATR the score should be lower, but here the 1m score is higher (70 against 60). That points to a different reference price at the two moments, for example the opportunity anchor being reset when the opportunity was saved. Its `started_at` equals the signal time while it already held 7 pieces of evidence. This is unresolved (U21).

- **F21, new (rule question, not a confirmed defect): the FIX-119 late-entry deferral was skipped on 162 because of one old BUY.**
  - The guard (`:1275-1279`) skips the deferral when `buyCount > 0`.
  - 162 had 1 BUY against 9 WATCH and 10 NEUTRAL, and the current signal was WATCH. So the code's own example of a defective case (WATCH-only accumulated evidence at a LATE_ENTRY price) was let through by a single earlier BUY.
  - It is behaving as coded. Whether one BUY should be enough is a design question.
  - **Effect after the fact:** if the entry had been deferred, there would have been no position. Without the signal exit, price would have hit the stop at 07:29:22.

- **F19, new (rule gap, PROJECT EVIDENCE: code): no gate stops a long entry in a downtrend regime.**
  - `MarketStrategyService.java:70` maps STRONG_DOWNTREND and WEAK_DOWNTREND to the same TREND_FOLLOWING profile as uptrends, with entry allowed.
  - No other code refers to the downtrend regimes. My search found only `MarketRegime`, `MarketRegimeService` and this switch.
  - 182 was a trend-following BUY inside a WEAK_DOWNTREND.
  - It is not documented as intended in TRADING_RULES, AGREED_KNOWLEDGE or DECISIONS. I5-004 measures its effect across the population before I rate its severity.

- **F17, new (code inconsistency, low; not a cause of loss): the execution-time Entry Quality uses a different ATR from the routes that use 5m ATR.**
  - `revalidateAtExecutionPrice` and the stored `entry_quality_score` use the 1m ATR (`:857-858`, `:1167`).
  - The setup-ATR, wake-up and transition routes deliberately score with the 5m ATR (`:364`, `:439`, `:615`, `:634`).
  - **Effects:** the cutoff check (score at least 50) and the size cap can disagree between decision time and execution time. The stored score (70 on 143) also does not match the logged score (60).
  - It did not block or resize 143. Any audit that reads `entry_quality_score` gets the 1m-ATR score.

- **F18, new (Replay parity risk; INFERENCE from E26 and E27): context selection at the 5-minute boundary.**
  - The 1m and 5m signals for the same boundary are generated within milliseconds of each other, but each becomes visible 1.3–2.3 s after its `generated_at`.
  - Production can only see committed rows. Replay selects by `generated_at` at or before the 1m signal (`latestAtOrBefore`).
  - **Example:** 137's 5m #156721 was generated **17 ms before** its 1m #156723. Replay would include it. Production may not have seen it before the commit.
  - On 137 the stored context agrees (NEUTRAL in both, per the opportunity row), so the outcome there is unaffected. The general mechanism can still make Replay pick a different 5m than Production did. I5-001 tests this.

- **F15, confirmed in iteration 4 and now sized (E28).** The masking is systematic: 963 relabelled bearish 5m signals in 4 days, only 5.4% of them backed by a real bullish higher-timeframe decision. The impact on entries is unchanged: 5 of 95 entries, 1 of them a frozen loss (182).
