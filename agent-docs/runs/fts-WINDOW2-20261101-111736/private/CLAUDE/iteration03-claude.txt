## FINDINGS (Iteration 3; I have reviewed only my own work)

- **F10, resolved: not a protection defect.** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E10).
  - On the first tick that breached the lock, the exit committed in 25 ms. The loss came from a −1.78% market move between ticks.
  - It is a design limitation: a "hard floor" cannot guarantee a price through a gap, and paper fills do not model slippage.
  - The exit avoided an immediate stop and a further −2.3%.
- **F13, new: on trade 143 the target extension turned a winner into a loser.** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E11) + INFERENCE.
  - This is behavior as designed (continuation policy), so it is a trading-rule question, not a code defect.
  - It is the single biggest swing in the frozen set: about +0.84% versus −0.15%.
  - After an extension, the protected floor is only entry × 1.0005, which is far below the original target. Proposed for the root-cause phase.
- **F14, new: confirmed exits wait for the next price tick.** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E9).
  - The wait was 2.8–38.9 s, and is longest on illiquid symbols (ACE, SHIB).
  - The cost was zero in all six trades, because missing ticks here mean the price had not moved.
  - Severity: low. It is a latency and observability gap, not a cause of loss.
  - 158 shows a possible gap between a signal's `generated_at` and when it becomes visible (U14).
- **F15, new, possible entry-logic defect (not yet confirmed): a bearish 5m reading was masked as NEUTRAL at entry (182).** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E12).
  - The exploratory route treated a 5m whose original decision was STRONG_SELL with score 25 as "NEUTRAL".
  - The entry happened in an illiquid market and never went above the entry price.
  - Whether this is a defect depends on which stage did the overwrite and why (I3-001).
  - I will also check whether 137's 5m NEUTRAL (#156721) was overwritten the same way.
- **F8, downgraded: low risk/reward does not lose more.** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E15).
  - Low risk/reward buckets had the best gross results. The ≥1.5 bucket held most of the stopped trades and the largest losses, which is consistent with higher-volatility entries.
  - What stands: there is no risk/reward gate, and with `fee_usdt=0` the small targets (+0.17–0.37%) are close to break-even after typical round-trip fees. The fee level is an external assumption, not fetched this session.
  - The "structurally negative edge" claim is withdrawn as a confirmed defect.
- **F3, closed: no stale signals in the live exit path.** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E13). Every exit used signals from the current candle. 172's 15-minute HTF wait is the documented rule (1h SELL while 5m is still WATCH means hold).
- **F11, refined: exits limited every loss to below the stop.** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E14). Of the six exits, three avoided a stop, two gave up small targets (158, 172) and one had no effect.
