## EVIDENCE

### E16: How 182's 5m STRONG_SELL became NEUTRAL (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I3-001 and code)

- The 5m signals #176403, #176529, #176655 and #176792 are each base STRONG_SELL with score 22–25. At the MULTI_TIMEFRAME stage (sequence 5) each was DOWNGRADED from STRONG_SELL to NEUTRAL, with the reason "Bearish signal conflicts with bullish higher-timeframe structure… 1h=WATCH (trend 16/25)".
- In code, `trendFollowing` (`MultiTimeframeConfluenceService.java:136-138`) neutralizes a bearish signal when `counts.bullish() > 0`.
- `isBullishContext` (`:337-339`) counts a context as bullish if its decision is BUY or STRONG_BUY, **or if its trend score is at least 15** (`BULLISH_TREND_MINIMUM=15`, `:24`).
- The 1h was **WATCH**, not BUY. Its trend score of 16 alone made it count as "bullish structure".
- The other stages (BTC, derivatives, order book) passed and did not change the decision. U15 is closed: **MULTI_TIMEFRAME did the overwrite.**

### E17: How the 1m BUY #176710 consumed the overwritten 5m (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I3-001 and code)

1. The 1m MULTI_TIMEFRAME stage reads the stored `getDecision()` of each context, through `contextAtOrBefore` at `:105-113`.
2. The hard veto `hasHardBearishOpposition` (`:146-151`) fires only on `decision == STRONG_SELL`. Because the stored decision was NEUTRAL, the veto did not fire.
3. The 1m BUY went down the "NEUTRAL/WATCH with weak structure" branch (`:128-130`). The stored path for #176710 confirms this: "5m=NEUTRAL (trend 10/25…)". The 5m trend of 10 is also at the bearish-context threshold (`BEARISH_TREND_MAXIMUM=10`).
4. `validateBuy` (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:81,93`) takes the same final decision; `HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:50,64` also reads `getDecision()`.
5. `balancedBuy` rejects 5m NEUTRAL with 1h WATCH (`:300-320`).
6. The FIX-11G exception then grants `BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M_WATCH_1H` (`:263-284`) when `finalEntryAllowed`, entry quality ≥ 50 and BTC is not in conflict.
7. 182's entry quality was **50, exactly the cutoff** (`BALANCED_TRANSITIONAL_MIN_ENTRY_QUALITY=50`, `:49`; entry quality from I1-004).

**AGENT INFERENCE (counterfactual):** if either check had read `original_decision`, the 1m BUY would have been vetoed to WATCH as STRONG_CONFLICT (`:120-122`), and no position would have opened.

### E18: Exit side of 182 (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I3-001 and I3-002)

- So the same 5m bearish evidence was suppressed for the entry and acted on for the exit eight minutes later. The 1h trend score dropping 16 → 13 is what flipped it.

### E20: 143's lock geometry, recomputed (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `ProfitLockPolicy.java:30-51`, I3-002, I3-004)

## FINDINGS (Iteration 4; I have reviewed only my own work)

- **F15, confirmed: an entry-logic defect in how context is read.** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E16–E18) and INFERENCE for the counterfactual.
  - The MULTI_TIMEFRAME stage rewrites a bearish 5m `decision` to NEUTRAL so that it does not act as a SELL. Downstream BUY gates then read that rewritten value as if it were genuine neutrality:
    - the 1m confluence hard veto at `:146-151`;
    - `validateBuy` and the FIX-154 assessment at `:81,93`;
    - the FIX-11G transitional authority at `:263`.
  - The trigger is weak: a 1h **WATCH** counts as "bullish structure" on trend score ≥ 15 alone (`:337-339`).
  - On 182 this removed a hard STRONG_SELL veto, and the entry passed at the exact entry-quality cutoff of 50.
  - **Severity: medium.** It is a logic defect, but only 5 of 95 entries were affected, and the loss on 182 was −0.21%.
  - It is not documented as intended in AGREED_KNOWLEDGE, DECISIONS or claude-docs (my grep found nothing).
  - Possible direction for the root-cause phase, not authorized: BUY context gates should take `original_decision` into account, or the neutralization should not count as non-opposition. I am not proposing a design here.
- **F13, revised and downgraded.** PROJECT EVIDENCE (E20).
  - **The extension did not remove protection.** The lock in force was 4.0758, about +1.06%, higher than taking the original target (+0.84%).
  - **The loss came from the price gapping through the lock.** A 1.8% move between two ticks 8 s apart was filled at the tick price.
  - The remaining design question is whether the paper fill should model a gap, and whether a stop-limit or lock-price fill assumption is wanted. That is a trading-rule question, not a defect.
  - **Reclassification of 143:** a valid loss caused by a market gap, with no code defect.
- **F14 stands (low): exits are evaluated only when a price tick arrives.** E21 confirms the 38.9 s on 172 with no price change, so the cost was zero.
- **New, F16 (observational): the server clock appears to run about 60–130 ms behind Binance.** It does not affect these trades. It matters for any check that compares `observed_at` with local time.
