## EVIDENCE

### E31: Entry Quality works differently from what I assumed (PROJECT EVIDENCE, code)

- **The reference price is the lowest of three values** (`:874-926`): the lowest 1m signal price in the window, the opportunity's anchor price, and its best price.
- **The window is the last 20 1m signals, not 30 minutes.** Production loads `findTop20BySymbolAndIntervalOrderByGeneratedAtDesc` (`:2603`), which is not bounded by the decision time. Replay loads `recent(...,20)` (`:2602`). The 30-minute `EVIDENCE_WINDOW` is only a secondary limit, so in practice the window is about 20 minutes.
- **The rejection penalty only applies in a narrow case** (`:961-974`). The earlier high must come before the current signal, at least 2 later prices must be below it, and the current price must be within 0.5% of it. The current price only replaces the high if it is strictly above it.
- **Opportunity age is rounded down to whole minutes** (`:950`).
- **There is a separate penalty of −15 when the stop is exposed** (`:65, :1010`).

### E34: How stop and target are built (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `AtrRiskService.java:34-45`, `application.yml:137-144`)

- The stop distance is the larger of 2×ATR and 0.50% of price, capped at 8%. The target distance is always 3×ATR.
- So whenever ATR is below 0.25% of price, reward/risk works out to 3 × ATR% ÷ 0.5. It falls below 1 once ATR is below 0.167% of price.
- Applying this to each trade's ATR% reproduces every reward/risk exactly: 137 → 0.64, 158 → 0.33, 162 → 0.44, 172 → 0.74, 182 → 0.48, 143 → 1.50 (its ATR of 0.27% is above the 0.25% point).
- Only one route has a minimum reward/risk: `BREAKOUT_RETRACEMENT_MIN_RR=1.15` at `:212` and `:1539`. In every other route, a reward/risk below 1 costs a flat −30 Entry Quality points and is never blocked.
- I found no record that this was intended in AGREED_KNOWLEDGE, DECISIONS or claude-docs.

### E37: How Replay prices exits (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `ShadowProductionReplayService.java:87-159`). U19 closed

- When production price events exist, Replay replays them, sorted by `observedAt`, as the clock for exits. It fills at the tick price with no slippage, so 143's gap fill at 4.027 reproduces.
- **Signals are ordered differently.** Replay processes every tick up to a signal's `generated_at` and only then makes that signal visible (`:127-168`). In Production a signal becomes visible at `created_at`, which is 1.3–2.3 s later (I4-001).
- **Concrete case, 158:**
  - The STRONG_SELL #166819 was generated at 06:30:01.397 and created at 06:30:02.688.
  - The tick was observed at 06:30:02.320.
  - Replay would evaluate that tick with the STRONG_SELL already visible. Production did not see it yet (E27).
  - The exit price was 1.4090 either way, so the P&L is the same. Only the timing differs.

## FINDINGS (Iteration 6; I have reviewed only my own work)

### Corrections to my own earlier work

- **E25 / U21 retracted and closed.** I said trade 143's two scores pointed to an anchor reset. That was wrong. The 60 used the 5m-authority ATR of 0.009073; the stored 70 used the 1m ATR of 0.010899, which the burst had inflated. The 1m ATR was larger, not smaller, and the anchor plays no part (E32). F17 stands, and this is its concrete instance.
- **E24, trade 162 corrected.** I suggested its 50 came from the rejection floor. It did not: 50 = −30 (reward/risk) −20 (ATR extension), with no age penalty and no rejection (E32).
- **E24 breakdowns for 137, 172 and 182 confirmed exactly.** On 182, a real rejection signal was raised back to 50 by the floor.
- **F20 ranking qualified.** My entry ranking in iteration 5 leaned partly on Entry Quality. E36 shows that score has not been validated as predictive, and in this sample it runs the wrong way.

### New findings

- **F22, root-cause candidate: the 1m decision at a 5-minute boundary reads 5m authority that is one bar old** (E33; PROJECT EVIDENCE plus a counterfactual INFERENCE).
  - The newer 5m candle has closed but has not yet been analyzed. `FIVE_MINUTE_MAX_AGE` is 20 minutes (`:66`), so a 5-minute-old 5m counts as fresh.
  - On 143, the old 5m granted the route's immediate-entry claim. The current 5m (WATCH, pullback, immediate entry not allowed) committed 174 ms before the BUY.
  - I have not verified whether the execution-time recheck rereads the 5m.
  - **Severity: medium.** It authorized one frozen entry, but 143 lost because of a market gap, not because of the entry itself.

- **F23, root-cause candidate: the stop/target geometry builds reward/risk below 1 into every low-volatility entry, with no gate against it** (E34, E35).
  - 5 of 6 frozen trades had reward/risk 0.33–0.74 and gross targets of +0.165% to +0.37%.
  - At standard fees, 158's target was below zero, and 162 and 182 needed win rates of 94–97%.
  - This is designed behavior (an undocumented interaction between configuration and code), not a code defect. Whether it is intended is a trading-rule decision.

- **F24, systemic (INFERENCE from E36): the population shows almost no edge before fees and a loss after fees.**
  - About −0.03% per trade gross, about −0.23% per trade at real fees.
  - The frozen losses are typical of this pattern, not outliers. I6-001 tests whether reward/risk or ATR% separates winners from losers.

- **F25, calibration (INFERENCE): Entry Quality runs opposite to outcomes in this sample, and so does the 1m BUY decision compared with WATCH.**
  - Both point to 1m BUY signals firing at the close of an impulse candle, the burst-top pattern behind 137, 162 and 182.
  - There are confounders (route mix, F17). This needs a Replay or population study before anyone relies on it.

- **F26, low code observation:** the documented 30-minute window is actually the last 20 signals. In Production the query is not bounded by the decision time; Replay's is (E31). Parity holds when the deciding signal is the newest one, so I found no loss effect.

- **F18, extended to exits:** Replay makes signals visible at `generated_at`, 1.3–2.3 s earlier than Production relative to the ticks (E37). Exits and entries near a candle close can diverge. No P&L effect is proven yet (158's price was the same).

- **F15 (defect), F19 (rule gap, small population effect), F21 (rule question) and F7 (contradiction with AGENTS.md, needs both agents) are unchanged.**

**Common root cause (AGENT INFERENCE, strong project evidence):**

- Five of six losses share one pattern: low-volatility 1m entries (ATR 0.055–0.124% of price), bought at the top of a micro-burst through an exploratory route, under a stop/target geometry with reward/risk below 1.
- The trades needed immediate continuation. It did not come: largest gains before exit were +0.18% or less. The signal exits then closed them at small losses of −0.13% to −0.35%.
- No exit hit the −0.5% stop. The exits did their job; the losses come from the entries.

### Recommended direction (not authorized; for the spec phase)

1. **For F15:** BUY hard vetoes should take a relabelled bearish `original_decision` into account. Regression: 182 must veto in Replay.
2. **For F23/F24:** use Replay to study a fee-aware viability gate. Candidates are a minimum reward/risk of 1, a minimum ATR% of about 0.167%, or a target distance of at least a multiple of round-trip fees. I6-001 provides the evidence for this.
3. **For F22:** when a 1m decision falls on a 5m boundary, use or wait for the 5m analysis of the bar that just closed. This lines up with AGENTS.md's "freshness from persisted candle close time" direction. Regression: 143 should not be entered.
4. **For F18/E37:** make Replay signal visibility follow commit time (or a measured delay), not `generated_at`.
5. **For F25:** validate Entry Quality against Replay outcomes before using it to size positions.
6. **Reporting:** keep calculating fees separately in every study, since paper P&L is gross.
