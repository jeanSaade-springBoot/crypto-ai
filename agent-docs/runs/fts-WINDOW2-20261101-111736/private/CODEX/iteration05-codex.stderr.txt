## Local-candle implementation safety

When implementing or reviewing the local-candle design:

- Keep `shared-market.fix142.enabled=false`,
  `shared-market.fix142.acknowledgment-enabled=false`, and scheduled analysis
  disabled for the OFF-mode cutover unless a reviewed change explicitly says
  otherwise.
- Preserve the input -> protection -> observer -> close-dispatch transaction
  order. Candle close analysis must not run on the Binance ingestion thread.
- A closed candle needs durable/recoverable analysis admission; an in-memory
  event/queue alone loses work across a crash. Recovery must distinguish fresh
  execution-capable work from historical-only analysis.
- Candle updates must be monotonic: a late open update cannot reopen or
  overwrite a closed candle. Publish close work only for an atomic
  open-to-closed transition.
- A WebSocket reconnect can occur at a candle-close boundary and Binance may
  not replay that final closed-kline event. Candle recovery is automatic: use
  an exact authoritative Binance REST candle only after exchange-time
  finality and the measured live-close grace. It may either finalise a matching
  existing `closed=0` row or insert a wholly absent identity inside the active
  data-quality window. A recovered open row preserves provenance; an inserted
  gap row uses `BACKFILL` provenance. Neither path may alter a completed row,
  create a close-transition token, dispatch analysis, create a live signal,
  or execute a trade. The recovery must have durable retry/audit evidence,
  bounded rate-limit handling, health visibility, and no manual repair step.
- Automation direction: no pending candle or analysis outcome may require
  manual approval, manual database repair, or a persisted trading fence.
  Reconciliation must retry and resolve automatically, or age out to a durable
  explicit outcome with a visible reason. This does not permit unverified data
  to masquerade as valid or force an entry: a genuine strategy/risk veto or
  unavailable authoritative data remains non-executable evidence, not a
  silently stuck pending signal.
- Gap backfill must be a pure insert-if-absent operation. It must never use a
  duplicate-key update that could finalise or alter a live row which appeared
  while the authoritative REST request was in progress. Recovery candidates
  are bounded by the active quality window and terminal audit outcomes are not
  retried. Each candle/work path must write one durable terminal analysis
  outcome; transient failures remain retryable and write no terminal outcome.
- Any future universal trading-mutation barrier is an in-memory drain
  participant only. Outside an active drain it must not block, delay, queue,
  or persistently fence fresh trading, signals, or protection. It may reject
  new mutations only while draining and must then wait for admitted mutations
  to finish before shutdown.
- Historical-only recovery must be structurally unable to reach execution,
  wallet, position, order, or executable-work services. Quarantined work with
  possible financial effects may be concluded automatically only from proven
  durable effect evidence (`EFFECT_COMMITTED` or `EFFECT_ABSENT`); unknown
  atomicity remains a release blocker. Binance REST rate-limit/ban handling
  must be shared by reconciliation, clock sync, bootstrap, and other REST
  callers.
- Enforce freshness from persisted candle close time before analysis and again
  before execution. Historical-only signals must be explicitly marked and
  excluded from live-latency monitoring.
- Price protection must receive the Binance observation timestamp and validate
  age/order inside the protection transaction. Paper-trading effects and their
  accepted price-event state must commit atomically; any future live-exchange
  effect needs a separate idempotent design.
- Do not add manual approval, persisted review fences, or database locks that
  block new analysis, signals, entries, or protection unless the user changes
  that direction. In-process coordination and normal database constraints are
  distinct from persisted trading fences.
- OFF-mode health/admin endpoints must not query or write retired shared-market
  tables. Existing `REVIEW_REQUIRED` history must not block OFF-mode work.
- Keep production and Replay evidence aligned. Replay must fail a parity claim
  for unresolved direct price-event evidence, while deliberate exclusions stay
  excluded.

# Trade Investigation Agent

## Role

Investigate one Crypto AI Trader trade from durable evidence. Explain what
happened, why the system entered and exited, whether it followed the intended
rules, and whether the entry/exit was supported by the information available
at that time.

Investigations are read-only. Do not change code, configuration, services,
database records, review states, Replay work, or deployment state.

## Required input

Obtain or resolve:

- Environment and application: local, test, or production.
- Database host, port, and schema.
- One trade reference: `paper_position.id`, `wallet_trade.id`, or a symbol plus
  a bounded approximate entry-time window.
- Optional question or suspected issue.

Before analysis, confirm the symbol, trade/position ID, running version or FIX
level, and ingestion mode. Read the actual schema before choosing tables or
columns. Do not assume a table is in `crypto_ai` when it may belong to
`crypto_ai_v2`.

## Evidence rules

- Use configured database and SSH/log connections without displaying secrets.
- Use bounded, read-only queries and targeted log searches.
- Treat stored timestamps as UTC; display both UTC and KSA (`UTC+03:00`).
- Attach record IDs and timestamped log evidence to every factual conclusion.
- Distinguish **confirmed fact**, **supported inference**, and **missing or
  conflicting evidence**.
- Missing logs or records do not prove that no execution/effect occurred.
- A saved signal does not prove execution. A callback return does not prove a
  transaction committed. Under FIX-153, `market_price_event.APPLIED` proves a
  committed successful protection callback, but can be a no-op when there was
  no open position; find the position/trade mutation separately.
- Current configuration does not prove which settings were active at entry. If
  no configuration history exists, report that limitation.

## Investigation workflow

### 1. Resolve identity and environment

Link the trade reference to, where available:

- `paper_position`
- `wallet_trade`
- entry and exit `trade_signal`
- `signal_processing_work`
- wallet/position execution records

Record entry and exit times in UTC and KSA. Confirm whether the trader ran in
local-candle OFF mode or a legacy shared-market mode.

### 2. Collect a small evidence bundle

Start with exact IDs and a narrow time window. Expand only when the evidence
requires it. Collect:

| Evidence | Establishes |
| --- | --- |
| `paper_position` | lifecycle, entry/exit, stop, target, status |
| `wallet_trade` | executed quantity, prices, fees, linked signal |
| `trade_signal` | decision, strategy, scores, entry eligibility, reasons |
| `signal_processing_work` | claim, attempt, completion, expiry, uncertainty |
| `candle` / `technical_indicator` | market data and analysis inputs available then |
| `market_price_event` | intra-candle protection observations and outcomes |
| configuration/version evidence | applicable rules, when historically available |
| application and timing logs | analysis, execution, protection, exceptions, waits |

Avoid fetching large signal explanation fields until they are needed.

### 3. Reconstruct the timeline

Build a timeline, explicitly showing missing stages:

`candle closed ƒ+' signal saved ƒ+' work claimed ƒ+' locks acquired ƒ+' entry committed ƒ+' protection observations ƒ+' exit decision ƒ+' exit committed`

Measure separately:

- candle close to signal generation;
- signal generation to processing/entry;
- observation time to protection completion;
- exit-trigger evidence to recorded exit.

Correlate delays only with logs/events for the same symbol, position, signal,
or price-event identity. A nearby slow SQL query is not proof of causation.

### 4. Assess entry correctness and quality

Use only evidence that was available at entry. Check:

- signal mode, closed-candle identity, freshness, and data quality;
- final decision and entry-allowed result;
- BTC context, higher-timeframe, ATR, liquidity, risk/reward, sizing, and
  strategy gates that applied at that time;
- correct signal/position linkage and absence of duplicate/stale-retry entry;
- quantity, wallet balance, risk limits, fees, and fill/reference-price gap.

Return two separate conclusions:

- **Execution correctness:** followed or violated the implemented rules.
- **Entry quality:** supported, weak, or unverifiable from contemporaneous
  evidence.

Subsequent price movement may evaluate the outcome; it must never be presented
as information the entry decision had.

### 5. Reconstruct protection and stop-loss behavior

For FIX-153, inspect accepted `LEGACY`/`APPLIED` price events and retain
`PENDING`, `NON_CANONICAL`, and `PROTECTION_NOT_APPLIED` records as evidence
gaps or exclusions. Never substitute candle OHLC for absent intra-candle price
evidence.

For each relevant accepted observation:

1. Determine the then-active stop, target, trailing stop, and profit-lock state.
2. Find the first accepted observation that met an exit threshold.
3. Confirm that protection evaluated it and identify the resulting position or
   wallet execution record.
4. Explain delays, rejections, stale/out-of-order classification, exceptions,
   lock waits, or pool pressure.

Classify the exit path accurately: fixed stop-loss, trailing stop, profit-lock,
take-profit, signal-driven exit, manual exit, or unknown.

### 6. Assess exit correctness and quality

Compare the recorded exit reason, quantity, and price with trigger evidence.
Check threshold reach, accepted-observation identity, protection timing, and
the committed execution. Calculate maximum favourable/adverse movement during
the holding period separately from the contemporaneous exit assessment.

### 7. Reconcile financial results

Recalculate using execution records:

- bought/sold quantities and weighted entry/exit prices;
- gross P&L, fees, and net P&L;
- partial exits, remaining quantity, and realized versus unrealized values.

Flag discrepancies among wallet records, position records, and UI output.

### 8. Diagnose cause and use Replay carefully

Classify findings as one or more of:

- strategy decision;
- data freshness or coverage;
- analysis delay;
- execution failure;
- protection failure;
- database, lock, or connection-pool contention;
- accounting/UI discrepancy;
- insufficient evidence.

Run Replay only as a controlled comparison with equivalent candles, accepted
prices, strategy version, and settings. State every unavailable input or
material difference. Never claim parity by substituting candle OHLC for missing
price evidence or treating `PENDING` as accepted.

## Required final report

1. **Trade identity and environment** ƒ?" IDs, symbol, version/mode, scope.
2. **UTC/KSA timeline** ƒ?" evidence-backed sequence and latency breakdown.
3. **Entry** ƒ?" execution correctness and entry quality.
4. **Exit** ƒ?" execution correctness and exit quality.
5. **Stop-loss/profit-lock** ƒ?" worked, failed, or unverifiable, with evidence.
6. **P&L reconciliation** ƒ?" quantities, prices, fees, and discrepancies.
7. **Root cause and confidence** ƒ?" confirmed defect, valid loss, configuration
   issue, delay, or insufficient evidence.
8. **Evidence references** ƒ?" record IDs, query/log timestamps, code/config
   references.
9. **Missing evidence** ƒ?" exactly what prevents a stronger conclusion.
10. **Recommended correction and regression checks** ƒ?" only when justified.

A profitable trade can violate the rules, and a losing trade can be a correct
execution. State those independently.

# FIX-154 higher-timeframe BUY rule ƒ?" 2026-10-10

- `data_quality_valid=true` is required before a bearish 1h signal can be
  treated as trusted. `NULL` is unknown and fails closed for bearish evidence.
- A trusted `STRONG_SELL` at `confidence_score >= 70` blocks the BUY.
- A trusted `SELL`, or trusted `STRONG_SELL` below 70, is a SOFT conflict:
  profiles receive effective 1h `NEUTRAL`, and any allowed initial BUY is
  capped at 25%.
- A bearish 5m still returns `5M_BEARISH_VETO` before any 1h result.
- Existing progressive/add rules retain their raw 1h requirement; a SOFT 1h
  never authorizes an add.

# FIX-153 candle continuity rule ƒ?" 2026-10-10

- Missing price evidence never becomes invented OHLCV data and never bypasses
  the data-quality gate.
- Automatic reconciliation may restore only one exact, final Binance candle.
  Its recovered/backfilled row has no close token and cannot directly cause
  analysis, a signal, or a trade.
- A later normal live close is the only route that resumes ordinary signal
  generation after a repaired quality window.
