### Answers

**Q1. Why did it exit on profit lock instead of take-profit?** [PE]
- **TP was never reached.** TP 0.00000543373 is entry plus 3 × the 1m ATR of 0.00000000791 (`DBQ-007:6`). On the 0.00000001 price grid, reaching it needed 0.00000544, and protection never received that (R4-1 to R4-3).
- **The lock armed and then triggered.**
  - The 0.00000543 tick was 84.2815% of the way to TP.
  - That passed the 70% activation threshold and completed one 10% trail step, so the lock was set at 50% of the TP distance: 0.000005421865 (`ProfitLockPolicy.java:39-49`).
  - The next different price, 0.00000542, was at or below the lock and above the 0.000005412705 hard floor, so the exit policy ran (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:207-219`).
- **The exit policy saw:**
  - 1m WATCH (89795, 42 s old);
  - 5m WATCH (89724, 3 m 39 s old);
  - 1h NEUTRAL (88327, the newest closed 1h candle, 58 m 37 s old).
- **Neither hold rule matched.** A hold needs either a 1h BUY/STRONG_BUY, or a 1h WATCH; both also need a supportive 5m (`PositionExitPolicy.java:58, 67-69`). A NEUTRAL 1h fails both, so the default `PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT` applies (`:88`). Trade 107's stored message records exactly this (`DBQ-002:11`).
- **No delay.** 94 ms passed from observation to decision and 105 ms to the SELL. The inputs were fresh.
- **Classification:** a valid exit that followed the rules. It was not a defect, stale data, an execution delay or a configuration problem.

**Q2. Why was there no continuation around 00:38 KSA?** [PE]
- Continuation is evaluated only in the live TP branch (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:100-107`), and that branch was never entered. The signal-path TP exit (`PaperTradingService.java:170-174`) never calls continuation at all.
- The position closed at 23:58:43 KSA, 39 min 16 s before the 00:38 KSA candle, so nothing was open to extend.
- "12:38" means 00:38 KSA. The investigation was already running at 00:54 KSA on 10 Oct (`DATABASE_QUERY_LOG.md:6`), so 12:38 PM had not yet happened.

**Q3. Why didn't protection re-check the remaining upside?** [PE]
- **The live rule reads three labels, once.** The lock-breach rule reads three decision labels once (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:219`). It does not look at the distance to TP, momentum, or the advisory analysis.
- **The advisory said HOLD, but it can't act.**
  - The position-management analysis 40 s earlier said HOLD, exit score 0/25, `advisoryOnly=true` (log line 45271).
  - Advisory output can only execute a stop-loss (`PositionManagementService.java:125, 130-133`).
  - Live protection doesn't depend on that service at all (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:39-48`).
- **The signal path reads no labels at all.** Its profit-lock exit reads no signal labels before selling (`PaperTradingService.java:176-180`).
- **[AI from PE] Grid geometry left one tick of room.** TP sat 2.373 ticks above entry. The lock armed on the first +2-tick price and sat 0.81 tick below the high, so any one-tick pullback breached it.
- **[AI from PE] The two rules treat the same 1h label differently.**
  - At TP, continuation accepts a NEUTRAL 1h (`PositionContinuationPolicy.java:47, 72-73`).
  - At a lock breach, a hold needs 1h WATCH or better.
  - With these labels, touching TP might have extended the target; that would depend on the 1m trend and momentum scores, which aren't in the bundle. A breach had to sell.
  - This is a design observation, not a defect claim.
- **[AI] Counterfactual:** if the live path had held, signal 89815 (price 0.00000542) would most likely have closed the position through the signal path about 19 s later, at the same price.
- The later rise is outcome only; the exit could not have known it.
