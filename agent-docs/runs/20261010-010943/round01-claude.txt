### 2. Q1 — why it exited on profit lock instead of take-profit

1. **The take-profit sat between ticks.** TP = entry + 3 × the 1m ATR = 0.00000541 + 0.00000002373 (`AtrRiskService.java:41,44`) [PE]. SHIBUSDT's tick is 0.00000001 [ER], and every stored price is on that grid [PE]. So TP needed a trade at 0.00000544, three ticks up; the target distance was only 2.373 ticks [AI].
2. **The protection never saw a price at or above TP while the position was open.** The position's high-water mark was 0.00000543 [PE]. Any accepted price at or above TP enters the TP branch (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:100`), which either extends the target or exits as TAKE_PROFIT. Neither happened: TP was unchanged at close and the close reason is PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT [PE+AI].
3. **One tick armed the lock at 50%.** A price of 0.00000543 is 84.28% of the target distance, which passes the 70% activation. The step rule in `ProfitLockPolicy.java:41-49` then adds one 10% step to the 40% starting lock. That gives 0.00000541 + 50% × 0.00000002373 = **0.000005421865**, exactly the stored value [PE, recomputed].
   - That single tick crossed both the 70% activation and the 80% step.
   - At the starting 40% lock (0.000005419492), a print at 0.00000542 would not have triggered [AI].
4. **The next print of 0.00000542 triggered it.** 0.00000542 is at or below the lock (`ProfitLockPolicy.java:51`) and above the hard floor of 0.000005412705 (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:210-211`), so the breach check ran (`:219`) [PE].
5. **The breach check found no reason to hold.** A hold needs either 1h BUY/STRONG_BUY with a supportive 5m, or 1h WATCH with a supportive 5m and a non-bearish 1m (`PositionExitPolicy.java:58-75`). The 1h was NEUTRAL, which matches neither path and is not bearish, so it fell through to `PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT` (`:88-92`).
