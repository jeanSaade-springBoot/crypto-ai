## FINDINGS

### F3-1: Trade 143's 8-second price gap was a real absence of trades, followed by a single sweep through the order book [PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH]

**Feed was alive** [PROJECT EVIDENCE, I2-004]:
- In 22:43:20–22:43:40, 17 symbols delivered APPLIED events, for example BTC ×9, ICP ×10 and SOL ×10.
- No EGLD event was rejected (NON_CANONICAL) or left PENDING.
- EGLD event `created_at − observed_at` was 4–10 ms, so there was no ingestion backlog.

**Binance trades in the gap** [EXTERNAL RESEARCH]: Binance REST `aggTrades` for EGLDUSDT, from 19:43:27.000 to 19:43:35.100 UTC:
- **There were no trades between 22:43:27.000 and 22:43:34.341.**
- At 22:43:34.341–.345: 5 trades at 4.097–4.095.
- **At 22:43:34.816 a single sell order took the book from 4.094 down to 4.073 in the same millisecond**, about 1,500 EGLD across 23 price levels. The profit-lock level 4.0758 fell inside that one millisecond.
- From 34.816 to 34.993 the price slid to 4.025.
- At 35.017–35.020: trades at 4.027–4.028. Binance's kline update stamped 35.021 carried close = 4.027, which the system recorded as event 1789409.
- By 35.023 the price was back at 4.040.

**Why the system saw no prices in between** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code]:
- `BinanceKlineService.java:91-106` turns every 1m kline message into a price event. There is no throttling.
- So the missing updates at :29, :31 and :33 came from Binance sending nothing. Binance documents a 2000 ms kline update speed. The observed data matches "no trades, no push", though Binance's docs do not state that rule.

**Implications** [AGENT INFERENCE]:
- **Feed stall and delivery failure are ruled out for trade 143.**
- Protection reacts only to 2-second kline snapshots, and only when trades occur. A sweep that lasts under a second is seen only at its end. Here the snapshot landed close to the local low: 4.027 was printed 3 ms before a rebound to 4.040.
- Counterfactual (uncertain): a trade-level or bookTicker feed would have seen the lock breach at about 34.816 while price was still above the hard floor 4.035. The code then calls `exitPolicy.evaluateProfitLockBreach` (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:219`), which may *hold*. So a different feed does not guarantee a better result. Live slippage is also not modelled.
- **Correction to iteration 2:** I said "prices kept falling". That held only for about 6 s, down to 4.007 at 22:43:41, with a 1m low of 3.994. The 22:44 and 22:45 candles recovered to 4.058–4.070, still below the 4.0758 lock (I2-003).

**Classification for 143:** the exit followed the rules, and there was no data defect. The loss came from a design limitation: kline snapshots as the protection feed, plus a profit lock that cannot survive a sweep through the order book.

### F3-4: Trade 182's mask now traced to code: the same 1h context neutralises the 5m SELL and then lets the BUY through [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + I2-005]

**How the 5m signals became NEUTRAL:**
- 5m signals 176529, 176655 and 176792 have `original_decision = STRONG_SELL`, `decision = NEUTRAL`, `confluence_status = CONFLICT`, higher interval 1h = **WATCH**.
- In `MultiTimeframeConfluenceService`, a bearish signal becomes `NEUTRAL` with status `CONFLICT` when `counts.bullish() > 0`. That happens in TREND_FOLLOWING (line 136) and DEFENSIVE (line 231).
- `isBullishContext` (line 337) counts a higher-timeframe signal as bullish **if its decision is BUY or its `trend_score ≥ BULLISH_TREND_MINIMUM`**. So a 1h **WATCH** with a high trend score is enough to neutralise a 5m STRONG_SELL. The exact branch depends on strategy and trend score; see I3-002.

**How the BUY got through:**
- The 1m BUY's HTF assessment then reads only `getDecision()` for 5m = NEUTRAL (`HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:20,50`). With 1h = WATCH, the path is `BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M_WATCH_1H`.
- So a mask meant to stop a bearish signal acting against a bullish higher timeframe is **reused as permission to enter long**.
- The FIX-154 audit store records `five_minute_raw_decision = signal.getDecision()` (`BuyHtfAssessmentAuditStore.java:87`), which is the masked value, so even the audit would show NEUTRAL.

**Why the exit came when it did:**
- At 10:00 the 1h signal changed to NEUTRAL. 5m signal 176934 then had status MIXED and was no longer masked. It surfaced as STRONG_SELL and produced the SELL_CONFIRMED exit about 15 s after its generation (10:05:03.926 → 10:05:18.504).

**Classification:** the candidate logic defect from iteration 2 is strengthened (an asymmetric use of `decision` versus `original_decision`). Confirming it still needs I3-001 and I3-002.
