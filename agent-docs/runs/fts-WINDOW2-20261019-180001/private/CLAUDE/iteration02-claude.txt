## FINDINGS

### F-0 (correction to iteration 1): the query timestamps are KSA, not UTC [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
My iteration-1 table added +3h to values that were already KSA. That was wrong.

- **What proves it in this run:**
  - `wallet_trade` 344 has `execution_price_observed_at = 2026-10-11 20:55:00.032`. Its message, written by Java as an Instant, says `priceObservedAt=2026-10-11T17:55:00.032Z`.
  - Trade 158 shows the same thing: the column reads `02:35:00.328` and the message reads `T23:35:00.328Z` on the previous day.
- **Why it happens:** the columns are `TIMESTAMP(6)` (I1-007). The application writes in UTC (`application.yml:22`, `SET time_zone='+00:00'`). The investigation session displays values in its own SYSTEM zone, which is KSA. Earlier studies (WINDOW1 runs) found the same.
- **Rule from here on:** displayed value = KSA, and UTC = displayed − 3h. The literals in my iteration-1 queries were read in the same session zone, so those results are internally consistent.

### F-2: Most targets were closer than their stops (structural) [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + I1-001/002]

`AtrRiskService.java:34-45` sets the stop distance as `max(2·ATR, 0.50% of price)`, where 0.50% is `ATR_MINIMUM_STOP_PERCENT` (`application.yml:143`). The target distance is always `3·ATR`. So when ATR is below 0.1667% of price, the risk:reward ratio is below 1.

| ID | ATR% | Stop distance | TP distance | R:R (stored) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 137 | 0.107 | 0.50% (floor) | 0.322% | 0.644 |
| 143 | 0.270 | 0.54% (2 ATR) | 0.811% | 1.500 |
| 158 | 0.055 | 0.50% (floor) | **0.164%** | **0.327** |
| 162 | 0.073 | 0.50% (floor) | 0.219% | 0.438 |
| 172 | 0.124 | 0.50% (floor) | 0.372% | 0.744 |
| 182 | 0.080 | 0.50% (floor) | 0.241% | 0.482 |

- **No gate reads R:R:** `riskRewardRatio()` is used only when the value is persisted (`AnalysisService.java:540`). This is project evidence.
- **Fee impact (external research and agent inference):** a regular Binance spot account pays about 0.1% per side, so about 0.2% per round trip. On that basis:
  - Trade 158's whole target (0.164%) is smaller than the fees.
  - Trades 162 and 182 would net roughly 0–0.04% even if they hit TP.
  - Paper fills record `fee_usdt = 0`, so these trades look better on paper than they would live.
- **Fee estimate for this set:** total notional is 130 USDT. At 0.2% that is about 0.26 USDT, which would roughly double the gross loss of −0.2732 USDT.

### F-3: Trade 182 bought into a 5m STRONG_SELL that had been relabelled NEUTRAL [PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-004 + code] [AGENT INFERENCE re: deployed build]
- **The 5m signals:** from 09:00 to 09:55 KSA every 5m ACEUSDT signal had `decision=NEUTRAL`, but `original_decision` was SELL or STRONG_SELL. That includes 176655 (generated 09:55:03, the latest 5m before entry), whose original decision was STRONG_SELL with score 25.
- **How the entry passed:** the 1m BUY entered through `BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M_WATCH_1H`, with 25% intent and 15 USDT actual. That path takes 5m=NEUTRAL from `HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.decision()`, which reads only `getDecision()` (`HigherTimeframeBuyAssessmentService.java:20,50`). So `5M_BEARISH_VETO` (line 22) never saw the bearish original decision.
- **Contrast:** many other paths in `ExecutionIntelligenceService` (for example lines 355, 1368, 1425, 1542) check `getOriginalDecision()` as well.
- **Context at entry:** regime WEAK_DOWNTREND (60%), strategy TREND_FOLLOWING on a BUY, price 2.15 ATR above SMA20.
- **Outcome:** no price event after entry printed above the 0.1884 entry; the highest was 0.1883, so maximum favourable excursion was ≤ 0. The trade exited when the next 5m signal (176934) came through as unmasked STRONG_SELL.
- **Classification:** this is a candidate logic defect, not yet confirmed. Two things are still unknown:
  - which build was deployed on 2026-10-12 (AGENTS.md describes FIX-154 as not implemented, yet the code exists in the working tree);
  - why the 5m SELLs were downgraded (most likely 1h confluence).

### F-4: Trade 143 chased an extended move, then the profit lock gapped through to a loss [PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-001/003/008/009]
- **Entry:**
  - The 1m signal was WATCH, BREAKOUT, RSI 84.3, RVOL 21.9, price 4.35 ATR above SMA20, entry type `WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, recommended position 0%.
  - Execution Intelligence `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR` overrode this, citing "the fresh 5m BUY". That BUY is 159285 (5m candle 22:30, generated 22:35:01.553). Its successor 159407, which is **WATCH**, was generated 2 ms after the 1m signal and 1.5 s before the fill.
  - The trade was a re-entry 22 s after the same symbol's previous position (wallet 171) closed by PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT at 3.996. The re-entry filled 0.93% higher, at 4.033.
- **During the hold:**
  - TP was extended twice: 4.0647 → 4.0805 at 22:40:41, then → 4.1043 at 22:41:13.
  - Price reached a maximum of 4.101 (+1.69%), which is 95.35% of the extended TP distance.
- **Exit:** consecutive applied price events went from **4.100 (22:43:27.038) to 4.027 (22:43:35.021)**: −1.78% with an 8 s gap and nothing in between. The next events printed 4.018, 4.016 and 4.007. So the lock at 4.0758 was skipped over and the exit filled below entry.
- **The prior position did the same:** wallet 171 exited at 3.996 against a lock level of 4.0255.
- **Interpretation:** this looks like a genuine sharp drop, because prices kept falling after the exit. A stalled feed or a delivery gap is not ruled out yet; I2-003 and I2-004 test this.
- **Classification:** two parts.
  - The entry was low quality (a chase) but allowed by the rules.
  - The exit followed the rules, but a profit lock cannot guarantee a profit when prices jump past it. In live trading, slippage would make this worse.

### F-5: Trade 172 is affected by SHIB's tick size [PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE]
- SHIBUSDT's tick of 1e-8 at a 5.49e-6 price is 0.182% per tick. ATR14 was 6.8e-9, which is **less than one tick**.
- To be hit, the TP (5.5104e-6) needs 5.52e-6 (+3 ticks), and the stop (5.46255e-6) needs 5.46e-6 (−3 ticks).
- Price stayed between 5.48 and 5.50 (±1 tick) for 39 minutes, and the trade exited one tick below entry.
- ATR, RSI and trend indicators carry little meaning at this resolution.
- **Classification:** a valid loss under the current rules, with a design gap: nothing gates on tick size or price resolution. The tick size itself is an assumption to confirm against Binance `exchangeInfo`; it is not stored in the database.

### F-7: Patterns across the six trades [PROJECT EVIDENCE]
- **Volatility and size:** all six were LOW volatility, and all were reduced-size entries of 15–25 USDT.
- **Entries on WATCH:** half the entries (143, 158, 162) came from 1m signals with decision **WATCH**, admitted through Execution Intelligence override paths.
- **No stops hit:** the lowest price in every hold stayed above the stop. Losses ranged from −0.13% to −0.34%; the early-exit logic limited them below stop distance.
- **TP rarely hit:** only trade 143 reached TP, and that TP had been extended.
- **Overall:** the losses come from setup geometry (R:R < 1 at near-tick scale) plus weak or masked context, not from execution delay or stale data.
- **Selection gap explained (iteration-1 F-5 resolved):**
  - After 2026-10-12 07:05 UTC, 6 more CLOSED positions all had non-negative P&L, and 4 STOPPED positions were negative and excluded by the charter.
  - The last open was 2026-10-15 00:08 UTC (03:08 KSA). That leaves about 4.6 days with **no new positions** before the snapshot, cause UNKNOWN.

### F-8: Minor timing observations [AGENT INFERENCE]
- **Paper close lags wallet execution on the live path.** On the live price path, `paper_position.closed_at` is 1.2–3.2 s after `wallet_trade.executed_at`; on the signal path (162) the two are nearly simultaneous. These may be separate transactions or delayed stamping. If they are separate, that matters for the AGENTS.md requirement that paper effects commit atomically. To confirm in the code next iteration.
- **Possible host clock skew.** Some events have `created_at` 130–160 ms *before* the Binance `observed_at`, which points to host clock skew of about 150 ms. This does not matter at these freshness scales.
- **Charter timestamps disagree slightly.** The charter's snapshot KSA (18:00:01) and UTC (15:00:03) are 2 s apart. Trivial.
