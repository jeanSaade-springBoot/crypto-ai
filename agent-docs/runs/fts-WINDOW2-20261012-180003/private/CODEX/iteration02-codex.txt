QueryId: DBQ-CODEX-I2-004  
Purpose: Obtain directly linked position-management events and all symbol-level price-event evidence during each frozen position’s exact holding interval. Price events remain symbol-level evidence, not proven per-position triggers. Timestamps returned are UTC.

```sql
SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    p.symbol,
    p.opened_at,
    p.closed_at,
    pea.wallet_position_id,

    pme.id AS management_event_id,
    pme.event_type,
    pme.old_value_usdt,
    pme.new_value_usdt,
    pme.market_price_usdt,
    pme.reason AS management_reason,

    mpe.source,

    mpe.source_sequence,
    mpe.source_received_at,
    mpe.delivery_status,
    mpe.ordering_slot,
    mpe.outcome_reason
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.production_exit_audit pea
    ON pea.paper_position_id = p.id
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.position_management_event pme
    ON pme.wallet_position_id = pea.wallet_position_id

   AND mpe.observed_at <= p.closed_at

ORDER BY p.id, pme.occurred_at, mpe.observed_at;
```
