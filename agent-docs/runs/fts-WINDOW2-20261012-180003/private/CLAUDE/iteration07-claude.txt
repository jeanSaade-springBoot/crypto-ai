**F60. U32 resolved: the 162 counterfactual is confirmed by code.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `:777-778, 1171-1181, 987-989`, I6-005)

- **The route does apply the score block:** ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE runs `applyInitialEntryQualityGuard` (`:777`), which blocks any score below 50 (`:1173`).
  - So with the age penalty (`:989`, more than 20 min → −5), 162's EQ would have been 45 and the entry blocked as CHASE_ENTRY_BLOCKED.
- **The boundary keeps being hit:** I6-005 shows three executed opportunities sitting at 1259.906 s, 1259.955 s and 1259.976 s, all just under 21 min.
  - Both ends of the age (`started_at` = the first signal's `generated_at`, `:2541`, and the entry `generated_at`) are processing timestamps, so the age always lands on whole minutes ± processing jitter.
  - Whether the penalty applies at whole-minute thresholds therefore comes down to roughly a coin-flip on processing delay.
- **Result:** F50 is strengthened. This is structural, not a one-off.

  - The contradiction is in how the query client displays them, not in the data.
