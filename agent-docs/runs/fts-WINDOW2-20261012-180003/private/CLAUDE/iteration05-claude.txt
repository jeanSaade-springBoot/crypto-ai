**F39. The EQ score at entry, and the size actually used** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: `wallet_managed_position.entry_quality_score` from I1-004, wallet messages from I1-003, code)

| Trade | Route | EQ | Approved % | ATR-recommended % (1m) | Wallet % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 137 | IMMEDIATE_VALIDATION, `FIX-11G` | 58 | 25 | 100 | 25 |
| 143 | SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR | 60 when decided, 70 stored (F42) | 25 | **0** | 25 |
| 158 | SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP | **50** | 25 | n/a (I5-005) | 25 |

| 182 | IMMEDIATE_VALIDATION, `FIX-11G` | **50** | 25 | 60 | 15 |

- **Sizing formula:** wallet % = ATR-recommended % × approved % (`PaperTradingService.java:328-333, 358-360`). For example, 60% × 25% = 15% for 182 and 162.
- **What EQ does in code** (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1171-1222`):
  - below 50, the entry is blocked;
  - otherwise the size is capped at 25% (EQ under 70), 40% (EQ 70–84) or 50% (EQ 85 and above).
  - FIX-11G, the setup wake-up and the setup-ATR route already start at 25% or less, so an EQ of 50–69 has **no effect at all** on them. For these routes the score works as a simple pass/fail at 50.
- **Weaker context gets the looser threshold:** FIX-11G's threshold of EQ ≥ 50 (`TradeExecutionValidationService.java:49, 266`) is looser than the sibling `BALANCED_NEUTRAL_5M` exception. That one requires EQ ≥ 70 and confidence ≥ 72 (`:234-236`), even though its higher-timeframe context is *stronger* (1h bullish rather than 1h WATCH).
  - This is a design inconsistency, not a defect against the coded rules.
  - It decided 182: with EQ 50, it would have failed the 70 threshold.

**F40. The score already marked five of the six entries as poor, mostly because of risk/reward.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code; the per-trade breakdown is AGENT INFERENCE)

- An R:R below 1 always costs 30 points (`:991`). R:R was below 1 for 137, 158, 162, 172 and 182 (I1-002), so none of them could score above 70 (75 with the breakout bonus).
- The breakdowns that reproduce the stored scores:
  - **137 = 58:** 100 − 30 (R:R) − 12 (recent-rejection penalty, `:995-1003`). No other combination of the coded penalties gives 58, so 137 most likely entered in a **recently rejected high zone**.
  - **143 = 60 / 70:** 100 − 10 (price expansion 1.5%) − 30 or − 20 (ATR extension 6.6× or 5.6×). This assumes a 30-minute low of about 3.972, which matches the opportunity anchors at 3.972–3.975 between 22:31 and 22:34 KSA (I4-003).
  - **158, 162, 182 = 50:** that score needs −20 beyond the R:R penalty, or the rejection floor that pins a score at 50 (`:1000-1001`). The components are unconfirmed (I5-001).
- **Classification:** the score did its job. The defect class is that a score in the high 50s only caps size, and the cap is idle on 25% routes (F39).

**F41. Sizing inversion: an ATR verdict of "wait for a pullback" gets the *largest* size.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + data; whether it is intended is unknown)

- **The code:** `PaperTradingService.java:328-330` treats `atr_recommended_position_percent <= 0` as **100**.
- **The effect:**
  - Trade 143's 1m signal 159408 was `WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT` with a recommended size of 0% (I4-004), and was sized at 25%.
  - 182 and 162 (`REDUCED_POSITION` at 60%) were sized at 15%.
  - The previous EGLD entry 159321 (0%) and DOGE 164118 (0%) also got 25% (I1-003/004).
- **Possible intent:** for the setup-ATR route, the 5m plan might be meant to own sizing. But the code uses the 1m execution signal's value, and treats 0 as "no data". The size was 25% where the 60% scale used for 182 and 162 would have given 15%.
- **Classification:** candidate sizing defect. How often it happens: I5-005.

**F42. Two EQ values for the same entry (143), and the price re-check contradicts the route's own rule.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- **When deciding:** the setup route scores EQ with the 5m ATR (`:634`), giving 60 / ACCEPTABLE (wallet_trade 358 message).
- **At the fill:**
  - `revalidateAtExecutionPrice` → `assessEntryQualityAtPrice` uses the **1m** ATR (`:857-858, 1167`).
  - `PaperTradingService:377-385` stores that second score, so `wallet_managed_position` 172 holds EQ **70**. The stored score is not the one that governed the decision.
- **The route's comment doesn't match these figures:**
  - The comment at `:631-633` assumes a "tiny 1m ATR".
  - Here the 5m ATR (0.009073, from the 22:30 KSA bar *before* the breakout) was smaller than the 1m ATR (0.010899).
- **Impact:** no size change for 143, because the cap was 25% either way. It is a consistency and observability defect: anyone investigating trade quality from the stored EQ will see an inflated score.

**F43. Trade 143's entry: a stale 5m setup was reused at a much higher price.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE + AGENT INFERENCE for intent)

What was known at the decision (1m signal 159408, generated 19:40:01.634 UTC → 22:40:01 KSA):

- **1m context:**
  - WATCH, RSI 84.3, 4.35 ATR above SMA20, `atr_overextended=1`;
  - `WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT`, recommended size 0%.
- **The 5m authority it relied on:**
  - 159285 (BUY, 22:30 KSA candle) assessed "immediate entry allowed, 100%" at a price of **3.986** (I4-004).
  - Price at the decision was 4.032: **+1.15%, or 5.1 times the 5m ATR**, above that plan.
  - Nothing re-checks the 5m plan at the new price.
  - `FIVE_MINUTE_MAX_AGE` = 20 minutes (`:66`), so "fresh" covers up to four bars old.
- **A newer 5m signal said the opposite before the fill:**
  - 159407 (22:35 KSA candle): WATCH, `PULLBACK_ENTRY`, immediate entry **not** allowed.
  - It was committed at 22:40:02.94 KSA, before the fill at 22:40:03.115, but was excluded by the selection rule (F33/F35).
- **The same 5m authority had just been used:**
  - It was used for the previous EGLD entry (opportunity 36481, `five_minute_decision=BUY`).
  - That position had profit-locked out 22 s earlier, after a 4.046 → 3.996 whipsaw.
  - Opportunity 36524 records 7 pieces of evidence, but only 1 BUY.
- **Verdict:** a poor entry, using only information available at that moment. It is a **route-design defect candidate**: the setup ATR authority is never re-checked against price drift, never marked as consumed, and never compared with a newer 5m bar.

Purpose: Rebuild the EQ reference, recent high and rejection count from the 1m signals in the 30 minutes before each entry signal (F40, U23)

### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-003
Purpose: Gross and fee-adjusted (10 bps per side) outcome by target-distance bucket across closed positions (F44, U26)
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN s.take_profit / p.entry_price - 1 < 0.002 THEN 'A_TP_LT_0.20' WHEN s.take_profit / p.entry_price - 1 < 0.004 THEN 'B_TP_0.20_0.40' ELSE 'C_TP_GE_0.40' END AS tp_bucket, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(p.realized_pnl > 0) AS winners, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl), 6) AS gross_pnl, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl - 0.001 * p.quantity * (p.entry_price + p.exit_price)), 6) AS net_pnl_10bps FROM paper_position p JOIN trade_signal s ON s.id = p.signal_id WHERE p.closed_at IS NOT NULL AND p.exit_price IS NOT NULL GROUP BY tp_bucket ORDER BY tp_bucket
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-004
Purpose: Outcome by stored entry EQ band (F39, F40, U26)
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN m.entry_quality_score <= 50 THEN 'EQ_50' WHEN m.entry_quality_score < 60 THEN 'EQ_51_59' WHEN m.entry_quality_score < 70 THEN 'EQ_60_69' ELSE 'EQ_70_PLUS' END AS eq_band, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(p.realized_pnl > 0) AS winners, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_sum, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl - 0.001 * p.quantity * (p.entry_price + p.exit_price)), 6) AS net_pnl_10bps FROM wallet_managed_position m JOIN paper_position p ON p.signal_id = m.entry_signal_id WHERE p.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY eq_band ORDER BY eq_band
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-005
Purpose: How often the sizing inversion occurs and how it turns out (ATR-recommended size 0 treated as 100), plus the six entry signals' ATR plans (F41, U24)
SQL:
SELECT id, atr_recommended_position_percent, atr_entry_type, atr_immediate_entry_allowed, atr_at_signal FROM trade_signal WHERE id IN (156723,159408,165412,167668,171589,176710);
SELECT CASE WHEN s.atr_recommended_position_percent <= 0 THEN 'REC_0' ELSE 'REC_POS' END AS rec_bucket, s.atr_entry_type, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(p.realized_pnl > 0) AS winners, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_sum FROM paper_position p JOIN trade_signal s ON s.id = p.signal_id WHERE p.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY rec_bucket, s.atr_entry_type ORDER BY rec_bucket, s.atr_entry_type
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-006
Purpose: For each SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR buy, the 5m signal chosen by the `generated_at` rule versus the latest closed 5m candle, and the outcome (F43, F46, U27)

SELECT b.id AS buy_id, b.signal_id, m.candle_open_time, (SELECT f.id FROM trade_signal f WHERE f.symbol = m.symbol AND f.interval_code = '5m' AND f.generated_at <= m.generated_at ORDER BY f.generated_at DESC LIMIT 1) AS used_5m_id, (SELECT f.id FROM trade_signal f WHERE f.symbol = m.symbol AND f.interval_code = '5m' AND f.candle_open_time + INTERVAL 5 MINUTE <= m.candle_open_time + INTERVAL 1 MINUTE ORDER BY f.candle_open_time DESC LIMIT 1) AS latest_closed_5m_id, b.price_usdt, p.realized_pnl, p.status FROM wallet_trade b JOIN trade_signal m ON m.id = b.signal_id LEFT JOIN paper_position p ON p.signal_id = b.signal_id WHERE b.side = 'BUY' AND b.status = 'EXECUTED' AND b.execution_reason = 'SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR' ORDER BY b.id LIMIT 50
