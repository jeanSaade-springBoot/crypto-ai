**F49. The EQ score is anti-predictive, and its largest component is the reason.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I5-004, I2-003; mechanism is AGENT INFERENCE)

| EQ band | Trades | Win rate | P&L per trade | Net of 10 bps per side |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 56 | **57%** | +0.0020 | −0.040 |
| 51–59 | 21 | 57% | −0.0000 | −0.046 |
| 60–69 | 36 | 42% | −0.028 | −0.085 |
| 70+ | 73 | **37%** | −0.046 | −0.137 |

- **Win rate doesn't depend on size, and it falls steadily as EQ rises.**
- **Why:** the score always takes 30 points off when R:R is below 1 (`:991`). In this system, R:R < 1 simply means a low ATR where the 0.5% stop floor applies (F9). Those trades have *close* targets that are hit more often: 57% winners versus 40% (F30). So the score penalises the trades that win most often.
- **Two confounds:**
  - EQ 70+ allows larger sizes (up to 40%), which inflates its P&L per trade.
  - The stored EQ is the score at the fill (F42).
- **Classification:** a **calibration defect** in the EQ model. It is not a defect against the coded rules.

**F50. Three of six entries were decided by millisecond `generated_at` timing, not by market evidence.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE for the timings and code; counterfactuals are AGENT INFERENCE except 143)

- **143:** the 5m signal 159407 (WATCH) was stamped 2 ms after the 1m signal. So the route kept the replaced 5m BUY 159285 (F35).
  - Had 159407 been chosen, `setupTimeframeAtrAuthoritySignal` would have returned null at `:1486` (`!isBullish(WATCH)`), so this route would not have fired. That much is code-confirmed.
- **158:** the 1m signal was stamped 21 ms before the 5m. That let the wake-up path find a current 1m and execute (F35, `:420-428`).
- **162:** opportunity age is computed as `Duration.toMinutes()` from the opportunity start to the signal's `generated_at` (`:948-951`).
  - The age was 20 min 59.976 s, which rounds down to 20 and is under the "more than 20" threshold, so the −5 penalty was not applied.
  - With the penalty, EQ would have been 45 (CHASE_ENTRY), under the `< 50` block in `applyInitialEntryQualityGuard` (F39).
  - The 162 counterfactual is AGENT INFERENCE: I have not traced that guard's application on the ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE route line by line in this iteration.
- **Pattern:** decision boundaries are keyed on the *processing* timestamp, not on candle time.
  - At the 5m boundaries, the 1m signal is stamped first 86% of the time (F46).
  - FIX-141 already fixed this class of problem for BTC context by selecting on candle time (F35).
- **Classification:** **systematic design defect candidate (timing nondeterminism).**

**F51. Trade 182 has a confirmed causal defect chain: confluence masking hid a 5m STRONG_SELL from the bearish veto.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE + code counterfactual)

- **Classification:** confirmed mechanism, with intended behaviour disputed. **Logic-defect candidate.**

**F52. Trade 143 has a confirmed causal chain: the route is built to override the 1m "wait" verdict, then uses a 5m signal that has already been replaced.** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- **Classification:** route-design defect candidate. Causal for 143.

## RESEARCH

These findings are EXTERNAL RESEARCH:

- **Intraday technical rules in crypto:** profitability after costs is unstable and has shrunk over time.
  - One 1-minute range-breakout study found no outperformance of buy-and-hold after costs.
  - Studies across about 15,000 rules found breakeven costs above typical crypto fees, though mostly for daily rules.
  - This fits F54 and the zero-gross-edge result. Note that most of these studies are on BTC and cover 2013–2021.
- **Binance spot fees:** standard VIP 0 is about 0.1% per side, or about 0.075% paid in BNB. I didn't reach the official fee page, so the account's actual tier is still unknown (U25).
- **Multi-timeframe practice:** the standard rule is to use higher-timeframe values only from bars closed by their close time. Sources say nothing specific about ordering at the same timestamp. That supports selecting by candle time rather than `generated_at`, which is my own inference.

QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-001
Purpose: Prevalence and outcome of BUYs whose audited 5m signal had a bearish original_decision masked to non-bearish (F51, U29)
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN f.original_decision IN ('SELL','STRONG_SELL') AND f.decision NOT IN ('SELL','STRONG_SELL') THEN 'MASKED_BEAR_5M' ELSE 'NOT_MASKED' END AS bucket, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(p.realized_pnl > 0) AS winners, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl), 6) AS pnl_sum FROM paper_position p JOIN buy_htf_assessment_audit a ON a.id = (SELECT MAX(a2.id) FROM buy_htf_assessment_audit a2 WHERE a2.reference_signal_id = p.signal_id) JOIN trade_signal f ON f.id = a.five_minute_signal_id WHERE p.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY bucket ORDER BY bucket
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-004
Purpose: P&L by exit reason and holding time across all closed positions (U31)
SQL:

### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-005
Purpose: How often an entry's opportunity age sat within 1 s of an EQ age threshold (20/40/60 min), measuring F50's timing sensitivity
SQL:
SELECT o.id, o.symbol, o.started_at, s.id AS signal_id, s.generated_at, TIMESTAMPDIFF(MICROSECOND, o.started_at, s.generated_at)/1000000 AS age_s, p.realized_pnl FROM execution_opportunity o JOIN trade_signal s ON s.id = o.latest_signal_id JOIN paper_position p ON p.signal_id = s.id WHERE o.status = 'EXECUTED' AND p.closed_at IS NOT NULL AND (ABS(TIMESTAMPDIFF(MICROSECOND, o.started_at, s.generated_at)/1000000 - 1260) < 1 OR ABS(TIMESTAMPDIFF(MICROSECOND, o.started_at, s.generated_at)/1000000 - 2460) < 1 OR ABS(TIMESTAMPDIFF(MICROSECOND, o.started_at, s.generated_at)/1000000 - 3660) < 1) ORDER BY o.id LIMIT 50
