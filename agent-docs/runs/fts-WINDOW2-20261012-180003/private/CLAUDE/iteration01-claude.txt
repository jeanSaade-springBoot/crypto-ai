**F2. Which code path closed each trade** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code)

- **Live price path (SELL_CONFIRMED, HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED):** these two reason codes are only produced by `PositionExitPolicy.evaluateNormalExit` (`src/main/java/com/crypto/position/service/PositionExitPolicy.java:29,42`). They are applied on each live price tick by `LivePositionProtectionService.onPrice` (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:235-238`). So trades 182, 158, 137 and 172 were closed by the live price path, not by a signal arriving.
- **Signal path (STRONG_SELL):** this reason is written by `PaperTradingService` (`PaperTradingService.java:197-208`), which stores the signal's decision name after `validateSell` passes. So trade 162 was closed when a signal arrived, at that signal's `latestPrice`.
- **PROFIT_LOCK_HARD_EXIT (trade 143):** three places can produce this:
  - the live path when the lock has triggered and the price is below entry × 1.0005 (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:207-216`);
  - the live path for the take-profit-extension hard floor (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:191-198`);
  - the signal path (`PaperTradingService.java:190-194`).
  
  Which one fired is UNKNOWN until `production_exit_audit` and the logs are checked.

**F3. Trade 143 lost money on a "profit-lock" exit** (AGENT INFERENCE from code)

- Entry × 1.0005 is the price that *triggers* the exit, not a guaranteed fill. The exit fills at the tick price (or the signal price), and that can already be below entry.
- The lock activates at 70% progress toward take-profit. For it to trigger within 3m31s, the position must have reached 70% of the way to TP within about 3 minutes. That suggests a very tight TP distance.
- This needs confirmation from `wallet_managed_position`, `position_management_event` and the price events (I2-008/009).

**F4. The live exit check doesn't check signal freshness or whether signals came after entry** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code; impact is AGENT INFERENCE)

- `LivePositionProtectionService.java:95-97` takes the newest `trade_signal` for each interval (`findTopBySymbolAndIntervalOrderByGeneratedAtDesc`).
- It doesn't filter on freshness, on `signal_mode` (it could pick up a stale or historical-only signal), or on whether the signal came after the position opened.
- `PaperTradingService` uses `PositionPriceAuthorityPolicy` for prices, but `PositionExitPolicy` only receives decisions and applies none of those checks.
- So a 1h SELL from before entry, or a stale signal, could drive SELL_CONFIRMED or HTF_SELL_CONFIRMED. This is a hypothesis, not a confirmed defect. DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-007 will show which signals were the newest at each close time.

**F5. Timing pattern** (PROJECT EVIDENCE for timestamps; AGENT INFERENCE for meaning)

- Every entry happened 4–6 s after a 1-minute close, which fits 1m signal processing time.
- 137, 143 and 158 opened at 5-minute boundaries (:55, :40, :35).
- Several closes came 6–20 s after a 5-minute boundary: 158 at 03:30:06, 137 at 21:00:12 (also an hour boundary), 182 at 10:05:20. That suggests a newly saved 5m SELL completed the SELL_CONFIRMED condition.
- 162 closed at 04:26:04, matching the 04:25 1m candle's signal.
- 172 closed at 07:15:45, mid-minute, which fits a live tick.

**F6. 137 and 182 exited within 5–8 minutes of entering** (AGENT INFERENCE)

- At entry, BUY validation requires a 5m signal that isn't bearish (`5M_BEARISH_VETO`).
- SELL_CONFIRMED needs a bearish 5m signal. So the 5m view must have flipped to bearish on the very next 5m close or the one after.
- That is a possible entry-timing or quality concern. It is not yet classified as a defect.

**F7. The study only covers non-stop-loss losses** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: DBQ-FTS-002, FTS-005)

- The selection rule is `status='CLOSED'`. All 73 STOPPED rows have negative P&L, and they are excluded by design.
- So the six frozen trades are all losses from signal-driven or profit-lock exits. This is not a substitution of trades, but it limits what the conclusions can say.
