## FINDINGS

**PROJECT EVIDENCE — cohort and evidence state:** The frozen cohort remains exactly `paper_position` IDs **182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137**. The permitted `db/CODEX` directory contains **zero** evidence artifacts, so no route can be assigned to any individual trade.

**PROJECT EVIDENCE — entry route:** A new paper position is created only after the signal-processing coordinator claims fresh durable work, obtains a fresh canonical price, and `WalletAutoExecutionService.executeBuy(...)` reports success. The resulting `paper_position` stores the entry signal, execution price, stop, target, and `opened_at`. [PaperTradingService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/service/PaperTradingService.java:292)

**PROJECT EVIDENCE — live protection route:** Direct price protection persists a `PENDING` price event, validates observed-time age and ordering inside the protection transaction, performs protection, then marks that same receipt `APPLIED`. A mechanical TP/SL/profit-lock/HTF exit closes the paper position only if wallet mechanical execution returns `true`. [LivePositionProtectionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/position/service/LivePositionProtectionService.java:53) [WalletAutoExecutionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/wallet/service/WalletAutoExecutionService.java:475)

**PROJECT EVIDENCE — signal-driven close route:** `PaperTradingService.completeClose(...)` records the paper close before invoking the signal-linked wallet exit. The wallet method is `void` and can return without a wallet mutation where no open managed position or sellable quantity exists. This is a source-level reconciliation risk, not proof that any frozen trade is affected. [PaperTradingService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/service/PaperTradingService.java:487) [WalletAutoExecutionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/wallet/service/WalletAutoExecutionService.java:211)

**PROJECT EVIDENCE — attribution limits:** An `APPLIED` price event proves a committed protection callback, but may be a no-op with no open position; it does not itself prove a paper-position close. Conversely, a paper close needs reconciliation to wallet trades and `production_exit_audit`. [MarketPriceEventService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/market/service/MarketPriceEventService.java:81)

**PROJECT EVIDENCE — timing risk to test:** Normal live protection selects the newest 1m/5m/1h signals without constraining them to `generated_at <= observedAt`; only the near-TP path explicitly performs an at-or-before lookup. A delayed but still age-valid price observation could therefore be evaluated against later signal context. This is a code-route risk requiring timestamp evidence, not a confirmed defect or trade cause. [LivePositionProtectionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/position/service/LivePositionProtectionService.java:95) [LivePositionProtectionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/position/service/LivePositionProtectionService.java:251)

**AGENT INFERENCE:** The six losses cannot yet be classified as valid strategy losses, entry defects, protection defects, or accounting inconsistencies. Their exact exit route, trigger evidence, and wallet reconciliation remain unproven.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CODEX-I4-003  
Purpose: Reconcile each frozen paper position to its managed-wallet position and executed wallet BUY/SELL evidence, including mechanical exits that have no signal ID.  
SQL:
```sql
SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    p.symbol AS paper_symbol,
    p.signal_id AS entry_signal_id,
    p.exit_signal_id,
    p.opened_at,
    p.closed_at,
    a.wallet_position_id,
    wmp.entry_signal_id AS managed_entry_signal_id,
    wmp.status AS managed_status,
    wmp.average_entry_price_usdt,
    wmp.stop_loss_usdt,
    wmp.take_profit_usdt,
    wmp.highest_price_usdt,
    wmp.profit_lock_active,
    wmp.profit_lock_price_usdt,
    wmp.profit_lock_state,
    wmp.entry_stage,
    wmp.opened_at AS managed_opened_at,
    wmp.updated_at AS managed_updated_at,
    wt.id AS wallet_trade_id,
    wt.signal_id AS wallet_signal_id,
    wt.execution_key,
    wt.side AS wallet_side,
    wt.quantity AS wallet_quantity,
    wt.price_usdt,
    wt.decision_price_usdt,
    wt.execution_price_observed_at,
    wt.cost_basis_usdt,
    wt.realized_pnl_usdt,
    wt.fee_usdt,
    wt.execution_reason,
    wt.status AS wallet_trade_status,
    wt.executed_at
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.production_exit_audit a
  ON a.paper_position_id = p.id
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_managed_position wmp
  ON wmp.id = a.wallet_position_id
  OR wmp.entry_signal_id = p.signal_id
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_trade wt
  ON wt.signal_id IN (p.signal_id, p.exit_signal_id)
  OR wt.execution_key LIKE CONCAT('POSITION:', a.wallet_position_id, ':%')
WHERE p.id IN (182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137)
ORDER BY p.id, wt.executed_at, wt.id;
```
