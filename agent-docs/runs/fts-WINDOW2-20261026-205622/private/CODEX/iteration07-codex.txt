QueryId: DBQ-CODEX-I7-001  
Purpose: Reconcile each frozen paper position to exact entry/exit signals and wallet executions; specifically prove or disprove C2 for signal-driven trade 162 and mechanical-exit ledger evidence for the other five.

SQL:
```sql
SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    p.symbol,
    p.opened_at AS paper_opened_at_utc,
    p.closed_at AS paper_closed_at_utc,
    p.entry_price,
    p.exit_price,
    p.realized_pnl,
    p.close_reason,
    a.wallet_position_id,
    a.close_trigger,
    a.source_signal_id AS audit_source_signal_id,
    entry_signal.id AS entry_signal_id,
    entry_signal.interval_code AS entry_interval,
    entry_signal.decision AS entry_decision,
    entry_signal.generated_at AS entry_signal_generated_at_utc,
    exit_signal.id AS audit_signal_id,
    exit_signal.interval_code AS audit_interval,
    exit_signal.decision AS audit_decision,
    exit_signal.latest_price AS audit_signal_latest_price,
    exit_signal.generated_at AS audit_signal_generated_at_utc,
    wt.id AS wallet_trade_id,
    wt.execution_key,
    wt.signal_id AS wallet_signal_id,
    wt.side AS wallet_side,
    wt.status AS wallet_status,
    wt.quantity AS wallet_quantity,
    wt.price_usdt AS wallet_price,
    wt.decision_price_usdt,
    wt.execution_price_observed_at,
    wt.cost_basis_usdt,
    wt.realized_pnl_usdt,
    wt.fee_usdt,
    wt.execution_reason,
    wt.executed_at AS wallet_executed_at_utc,
    wt.execution_message
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
JOIN crypto_ai.production_exit_audit a
  ON a.paper_position_id = p.id
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal entry_signal
  ON entry_signal.id = p.signal_id
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal exit_signal
  ON exit_signal.id = a.source_signal_id
LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.wallet_trade wt
  ON wt.signal_id IN (p.signal_id, a.source_signal_id)
  OR wt.execution_key LIKE CONCAT('POSITION:', a.wallet_position_id, ':%')
WHERE p.id IN (182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137)
ORDER BY p.id, wt.executed_at, wt.id;
```

QueryId: DBQ-CODEX-I7-002  
Purpose: Test C1 directly by locating any accepted direct-price receipt whose processing record was created after its observation but after a same-symbol 1m/5m/1h signal had become available. Returned rows are candidates only, not proof of the signal selected.

SQL:
```sql
SELECT
    p.id AS paper_position_id,
    p.symbol,
    mpe.id AS market_price_event_id,
    mpe.observed_at AS observed_at_utc,
    mpe.created_at AS receipt_created_at_utc,
    mpe.price AS observed_price,
    s.id AS later_signal_id,
    s.interval_code,
    s.decision,
    s.generated_at AS later_signal_generated_at_utc
FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p
JOIN crypto_ai.market_price_event mpe
  ON mpe.symbol = p.symbol
 AND mpe.observed_at >= p.opened_at
 AND mpe.observed_at <= p.closed_at
 AND mpe.source = 'BINANCE_KLINE_DIRECT_FIX153'
 AND mpe.delivery_status = 'APPLIED'
JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal s
  ON s.symbol = p.symbol
 AND s.interval_code IN ('1m', '5m', '1h')
 AND s.generated_at > mpe.observed_at
 AND s.generated_at <= mpe.created_at
WHERE p.id IN (182, 172, 162, 158, 143, 137)
ORDER BY p.id, mpe.observed_at, mpe.id, s.generated_at, s.id
LIMIT 5000;
```
