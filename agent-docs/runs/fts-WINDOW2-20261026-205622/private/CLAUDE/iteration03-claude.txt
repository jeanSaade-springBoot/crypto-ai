## FINDINGS

**F-11 — Trade 143: the price swept past the lock; protection reacted correctly** (PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH)

*Recorded price observations (I2-003; all APPLIED, no rejected or pending rows):*

| UTC time | Price | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 22:43:27.038 | 4.100 | last observation before the drop |
| — | — | nothing recorded for 8 s |
| 22:43:35.021 | 4.027 | first observation after the drop |

- Wallet SELL #359 committed at 22:43:35.045, **24 ms** after that observation.
- The exit went through the lock-trigger branch, then the hard-floor branch (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:207-215`):
  - 4.027 is below the lock (4.07579).
  - 4.027 is also below the hard floor, entry × 1.0005 = 4.0350165.

*Binance public trades for EGLDUSDT, 22:43:27–22:43:36 UTC (= 01:43:27–01:43:36 KSA on 10-12):*
- **No trades at all** from 22:43:27.000 to 22:43:34.341. That explains the empty gap: the app does not filter duplicate prices; every 1m kline message becomes a row (`BinanceKlineService.java:91-108`).
- At 22:43:34.816 one batch of aggressive sells swept the book from 4.094 down to 4.073.
- By 22:43:34.998 the price was 4.025–4.027.
- The lock level was crossed inside that same ~200 ms sweep.

*Caveats on the Binance data:*
- Binance documents a 2000 ms update speed for kline streams. I did not confirm whether it skips pushes when there are no trades; the 2 s spacing seen here fits that pattern.
- The trade list passed through a summarising fetch tool, so the individual rows should be re-pulled programmatically if they will be relied on.

**Assessment:**
- This is gap risk, not a protection defect or stale data.
- With 2-second kline updates, no recorded price between 4.0758 and 4.035 ever existed.
- An exit at exactly the lock price would not have been a realistic fill. This **resolves U-6** and withdraws the "possible defect" in Iteration 2 F-7.

**F-12 — Trade 143's entry depended on a 2 ms race at the 5-minute boundary** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + DB; the counterfactual is AGENT INFERENCE)

*How the route picks its 5m signal:*
- `setupTimeframeAtrAuthoritySignal` takes the latest 5m signal with `generated_at ≤` the 1m signal's `generated_at`, up to 20 minutes old (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:66, 1485, 2455-2461`).

*What it picked for trade 143:*

| Signal | Interval | Candle (UTC) | Decision | Generated (UTC) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 159408 (the entry signal) | 1m | — | — | 22:40:01.633945 |
| **159285 (used)** | 5m | 22:30 | BUY | 22:35:01.55 |
| 159407 (newer, excluded) | 5m | 22:35, closed 22:40:00 | WATCH | 22:40:01.635944 — 2 ms later |

- The route accepts only BUY or STRONG_BUY (`:2634-2636`).
- **Inference:** had 159407 been visible, the route would have returned null and trade 143 would not have opened.
- The entry used an ATR plan one bar out of date, while the newest 5m bar was already closed and no longer BUY.
- This is not look-ahead; the code ordering is deliberate. It is a timing weakness at boundaries, not a confirmed bug.

**F-13 — Which 5m signal is "latest" depends on commit timing, not `generated_at`** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- For trade 137, 5m signal 156721 was generated at 20:55:01.558, which is 18 ms *before* the 1m signal (20:55:01.5757).
- Even so, `confluence_higher_signal_generated_at` = 20:50:01.42 (signal 156594).
- So the `≤ generated_at` query sees only committed rows; `generated_at` comes about 1.3 s before `created_at` (I2-001).
- **Replay parity hazard (inference):** Replay sees all rows, so it would pick 156721 where Production picked 156594. Both were NEUTRAL, so there was no decision difference here.
- For trade 143 both systems would agree, because 159407 is excluded by `generated_at` anyway.

**F-14 — Live exits wait for the next price event** (PROJECT EVIDENCE; the cause is AGENT INFERENCE)

| Trade | Last exit condition became true (UTC) | Wallet SELL (UTC) | Lag | Avg seconds between observations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 158 | 1m #166819 created 03:30:02.688 | 03:30:04.217 | 1.5 s | 3.8 |

| 172 | 5m #172588 → NEUTRAL, generated 07:15:04.578 | 07:15:43.492 | 37.7 s | 11.8 |

- `onPrice` runs only when a price event arrives (`LivePositionProtectionService.java:54-71`). Thinly traded symbols (SHIB, ACE) therefore wait longer.
- Exit prices for 172 and 182 equalled their signal prices, so no price cost was found. For 137, 3.987 against 3.988.
- The 5m `created_at` values are unknown; I3-002 checks them.

**F-15 — Trade 158 entered through the wake-up route** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- The 1m work item 165412 completed at 02:35:02.882 with `paper_position_id` NULL (I2-007).
- 5m BUY #165415 was generated at 02:35:01.490, 21 ms after the 1m signal.
- Wallet BUY #392 followed at 02:35:02.988 with `entry_stage` = SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP.
- So the wake-up route made up for the same boundary race as F-12, using a *fresh* 5m BUY. Trade 143 had no such correction because its fresh 5m was WATCH.

**F-16 — Trade 182: the bearish 5m was downgraded to NEUTRAL** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- From the 09:00 candle (generated 09:05) through the 09:55 candle, every 5m signal had `original_decision` SELL or STRONG_SELL but `decision` NEUTRAL (I2-005, 176655 and 176792).
- Both the entry confluence ("5m=NEUTRAL") and the exit policy read the downgraded value.
- The exit fired only when 5m #176934 kept STRONG_SELL (10:05:03.926).
- The reason for the downgrade is unknown (I3-005 checks it).
- **Entry assessment:** the entry was taken against 55 minutes of raw bearish 5m evidence that the system had available.

**F-17 — Trade 143's target extensions fired on exact price events** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- First extension, event 51 at 22:40:41.0497: on the 4.067 observation at 22:40:41.030, which was at or above the original target 4.0647.
- Second extension, event 52 at 22:41:13.0356: on the 4.081 observation at 22:41:13.029.
- **Outcome (inference):** without the continuation extension the trade would have exited at about 4.067, roughly +0.21 USDT before fees.
- After the second extension, progress was about 67%, below the 70% activation level. By code, that should start a FIX-118 rebase. The final state was ACTIVE with no rebase timestamp, so a rebase may have started and cleared. Unconfirmed without the `[FIX-118]` logs.

**F-18 — Rejected price observations leave no database row** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- `recordPending` returns null on STALE_PRICE or FUTURE_TIMESTAMP and writes only a log line (`MarketPriceEventService.java:52-57`).
- A rolled-back input transaction also leaves no row.
- So the database alone cannot prove a gap had no dropped messages. For trade 143, the Binance trade data closes this off (F-11).

**F-19 — Paper close lags the wallet SELL by 1.2–3.2 s** (PROJECT EVIDENCE; cause unknown)
- `closePaper` stamps `closed_at` with `Instant.now()` after `executeMechanicalExit` returns, in the same `onPrice` call (`:391-406`).
- The lag is therefore time spent inside the wallet exit path, not a separate commit. Its cause needs logs or a code trace (next iteration).
- Trade 162 (signal path) shows the reverse order: paper close 04:26:04.116, then wallet SELL 04:26:04.134.

**F-20 — Freshness at entry and exit confirmed** (PROJECT EVIDENCE)
- Every 1m signal's `candle_open_time` is the minute before it was generated, so it analysed the just-closed candle, 1.4–2.8 s after close.
- `data_quality_valid = 1` on all 12 entry and exit signals.
- BTC context age was 1–3 s, except 60 s on the irrelevant NEUTRAL signal 159487.
- DOGE 5m and 1h signals ran 5–10 s after the boundary, against about 1.5 s for the others. Minor.
