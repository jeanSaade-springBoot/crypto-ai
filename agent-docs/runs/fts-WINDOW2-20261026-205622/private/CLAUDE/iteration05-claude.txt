**F-30 — Trade 137's STRONG_BUY 91 depended on sentiment and fundamentals being left out of the score** (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + I1-005/I1-007; the counterfactual is AGENT INFERENCE)

*How the score was built:*
- RANGE_MEAN_REVERSION weights are trend 15, volume 18, momentum 22, sentiment 15, fundamentals 15 (`application.yml:360-367`).
- Signal #156723 scored raw 50 out of the 55 available points (sentiment and fundamentals "unavailable/stale; excluded from normalization"), so normalized = 91 (`MarketStrategyService.java:96-105`).

*Why that mattered:*
- Price was at 68.28% of the Bollinger range, above the 55% limit for a range BUY (`RangeEntryLocationService.java:35`).
- It passed only through the expansion exception, which needs STRONG_BUY *and* a normalized score ≥ 90 (`:37, 96-103`; decision path step 4).
- With sentiment counted: (50 + S) / 70 ≥ 90% needs S ≥ 13 out of 15. At S = 10, the score is 86: a BUY, not a STRONG_BUY (threshold 88). The exception fails, and the location guard blocks the entry.

*Why S = 10 is a reasonable guess:*
- DOGE and SHIB signals the next day carried "Sentiment 10/15; raw=0.380" (I1-005).
- The identical raw value suggests a market-wide feed, but the EGLD value at 20:55 UTC is unknown. I5-004 checks it.

*Two more points:*
- Leaving a missing component out of the normalization raises the score whenever the available components are near their maximum. Here volume and momentum were both at maximum.
- The 1m entry was in RANGE, but the 1h was WEAK_DOWNTREND with trend 11/25.

*Assessment:* a candidate scoring weakness, an artefact of leaving missing data out. It is not a code defect against the current rules, but it turned a data gap into entry authority.

  - Together with F-22 and F-29, trade 182 is the weakest entry in the set.
  - It is not a valid "clean" losing trade; it is a design-permitted, low-quality entry.
