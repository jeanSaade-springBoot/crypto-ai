## In short

1. **The whole system has no gross edge.** Across all 193 closed positions, gross P&L was −4.17 USDT on 6,195 USDT traded, with 47% wins. Estimated fees of 9.3–12.4 USDT are 2–3 times that loss. The six frozen trades are typical members of that population, not outliers (F-36).
2. **My Iteration 5 idea that low Entry Quality causes the losses is wrong on this data.** Positions with Entry Quality (EQ) of 70 or more lost the most per dollar traded; EQ 50–54 was slightly positive. Planned reward:risk does not separate winners from losers either (F-37).
3. **Trades 172 and 182 share one candidate logic defect.** A 5m veto is lost when the 5m signal is passed to the 1m entry gates (F-38). I5-003 now confirms the 172 half: its 5m "confirmation" #171559 was blocked by `RANGE_ENTRY_LOCATION`.
4. **Trade 137's counterfactual is now near-certain.** When sentiment data is available it is market-wide and was 10/15 in every row. Counting it gives 137 a score of 86 (BUY), not 91 (STRONG_BUY), so the range-location guard would have blocked the entry (F-39).

## FINDINGS

### F-36 — No gross edge, and costs set the sign (PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH; arithmetic is AGENT INFERENCE)

*From I5-001/I5-002 (both total n = 193, so the joins did not duplicate rows):*
- **Gross result:** P&L −4.1707 USDT on notional 6,195 USDT, which is −0.067% per dollar traded. There were 90 wins.
- **Fees:**
  - **EXTERNAL RESEARCH:** Binance's official schedule for a Regular user is 0.100% maker and taker, or 0.075% each with the BNB discount ([Binance fee schedule](https://www.binance.com/en/fee/schedule)). Secondary sources broadly agree ([Binance.US](https://support.binance.us/hc/en-us/articles/360047428113-Binance-US-Launches-Trading-Fee-Discount-and-VIP-Program-With-BNB), [Finhay](https://www.finhay.com.vn/en/phi-giao-dich-binance)).
  - **AGENT INFERENCE:** a round trip therefore costs about 0.15–0.20%, or about 9.3–12.4 USDT across these positions. That puts the net result at about −13.5 to −16.6 USDT.
- **Frozen trades:** each lost 0.13–0.35% before fees (I2). That is ordinary scatter around a negative-after-cost average.
- **Fees are not recorded:** `fee_usdt = 0` (F-3/F-4), so the dashboards understate the loss by about 2–3 times.

*Assessment:* the main economic root cause is a system-level absence of edge. It is not a single-trade defect.

### F-38 — A 5m veto is lost on the way to the 1m entry gates (PROJECT EVIDENCE; frequency not yet measured)

Both trades show the same pattern: the gate reads a cleaned-up 5m field and loses the 5m's own veto.

*Trade 182:*
- 5m `original_decision` was STRONG_SELL, but the stored `decision` was neutralised by a weak 1h WATCH (trend 16/25).
- `hasHardBearishOpposition` and FIX-11G read only `decision` (F-22).
- Elsewhere the code does read the raw value through `strongerDecision(decision, original_decision)` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:2092, 2100, 2449`).

*Trade 172:*
- The 5m #171559 was BUY 80 but had `final_entry_allowed=0` with `primary_blocking_stage=RANGE_ENTRY_LOCATION` (I5-003).
- `validateBuy` granted `BALANCED_STRONG` from the 5m decision alone. The wake-up route does refuse such a 5m (`:402`).
- All six RANGE-regime 5m BUY/STRONG_BUY signals in I5-003 were blocked at `RANGE_ENTRY_LOCATION`. That closes U-25.

*Assessment:* a candidate logic defect, inconsistent with sibling guards. It stays "candidate" until the frequency check (I6-004) and a Replay test are done.

### F-39 — Leaving missing data out of the score acts as optimistic imputation, and it decided trade 137 (PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + I5-004; counterfactual is AGENT INFERENCE, now high-confidence)

*What the data shows (I5-004, 10-11 20:00 to 10-12 10:10 UTC):*
- Sentiment was available in 287 of about 850 1m rows **for every symbol alike**. When available it was always exactly 10 (min = max = 10). So it is one market-wide feed that is only intermittently available.
- EGLD never had fundamentals.

*How the score is built:*
- `sentimentScore` maps the raw value to `round((raw + 1) × 7.5)` (`AnalysisService.java:955-958`): 0.38 gives 10.
- `MarketStrategyService.score` divides by the maximum of the *available* components only (`MarketStrategyService.java:99-105`).
- Leaving a component out is the same as assuming it scored at the same rate as the components that are present. Here that rate was 91%, while the feed's real level was 67%.

*The counterfactual for 137:* (50 + 10) / 70 = 85.7, rounded to 86. That is a BUY, not a STRONG_BUY, so the ≥90 range-expansion exception fails and the 68.28% range position blocks the entry (F-30).

*Further implication:* identical setups get different decisions depending on whether the sentiment feed happened to be up. DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-003 measures the effect across all positions.

*Assessment:* a scoring-design weakness that turned missing data into permission to enter. It is not a defect against the current rules.

### F-40 — The route-threshold inconsistencies are real but not shown to cost money (PROJECT EVIDENCE: I5-001)

- **FIX-11G:** n = 35, 18 wins, −0.288 USDT (−0.032%). That is no worse than average. FIX-112B has n = 2, too few to compare.
- **Worst groups per dollar:**
  - `IMMEDIATE_VALIDATION/BALANCED_STRONG`: −0.171%, 6 wins in 16
  - `SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR`: −0.145%
  - `SETUP_CONFIRMATION_WAKEUP/BALANCED_STRONG`: −0.059%, 15 wins in 40
- **The only clearly positive group** is `ACCUMULATED_EVIDENCE/BALANCED_EARLY`, +0.88 on 22 trades. That is trade 162's own route.
- **Sample size (AGENT INFERENCE):** with groups of 40 or fewer and per-trade spread around ±0.2 USDT, differences of about 1 USDT are roughly one standard error. Route-level conclusions are not statistically supported.
- **Conclusion:** F-29 and F-33 stay design inconsistencies, not proven loss drivers.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-001
Purpose: Realized versus planned payoff by status and exit reason, to test whether winners are cut short (F-37, U-29). Note: stop and target may have been moved after entry.
SQL:
SELECT p.status, LEFT(p.close_reason, 40) AS reason, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(p.realized_pnl > 0) AS wins, ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN p.realized_pnl > 0 THEN (p.exit_price - p.entry_price) / p.entry_price * 100 END), 4) AS avg_win_pct, ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN p.realized_pnl < 0 THEN (p.exit_price - p.entry_price) / p.entry_price * 100 END), 4) AS avg_loss_pct, ROUND(AVG((p.take_profit - p.entry_price) / p.entry_price * 100), 4) AS avg_tp_pct, ROUND(AVG((p.entry_price - p.stop_loss) / p.entry_price * 100), 4) AS avg_sl_pct, ROUND(SUM(p.realized_pnl), 4) AS pnl, ROUND(SUM(p.entry_price * p.quantity), 2) AS notional FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p WHERE p.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') GROUP BY p.status, reason ORDER BY p.status, n DESC LIMIT 40;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-002
Purpose: How far STOPPED losses overshoot their stop (F-41, U-30).
SQL:
SELECT COUNT(*) AS n, ROUND(AVG((p.entry_price - p.exit_price) / (p.entry_price - p.stop_loss)), 3) AS avg_loss_to_stop, SUM((p.entry_price - p.exit_price) > 1.5 * (p.entry_price - p.stop_loss)) AS n_over_1_5x, ROUND(MAX((p.entry_price - p.exit_price) / (p.entry_price - p.stop_loss)), 3) AS max_ratio FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p WHERE p.status = 'STOPPED' AND p.entry_price > p.stop_loss;
SELECT p.id, p.symbol, p.closed_at, ROUND((p.entry_price - p.stop_loss) / p.entry_price * 100, 4) AS sl_pct, ROUND((p.entry_price - p.exit_price) / p.entry_price * 100, 4) AS loss_pct, p.realized_pnl FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p WHERE p.status = 'STOPPED' AND p.entry_price > p.stop_loss ORDER BY (p.entry_price - p.exit_price) / (p.entry_price - p.stop_loss) DESC LIMIT 10;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I6-004
Purpose: How often F-38 occurs, and its outcome. For each 1m-signal position, take the latest 5m signal at or before the entry signal (10-minute bound) and flag a hidden bearish 5m or a blocked bullish 5m (U-32).
SQL:
SELECT x.hidden_bear_5m, x.blocked_bull_5m, COUNT(*) AS n, SUM(x.realized_pnl > 0) AS wins, ROUND(SUM(x.realized_pnl), 4) AS pnl, ROUND(SUM(x.notional), 2) AS notional FROM (SELECT p.id, p.realized_pnl, p.entry_price * p.quantity AS notional, COALESCE(f.decision IN ('NEUTRAL','WATCH') AND f.original_decision IN ('SELL','STRONG_SELL'), 0) AS hidden_bear_5m, COALESCE(f.decision IN ('BUY','STRONG_BUY') AND f.final_entry_allowed = 0, 0) AS blocked_bull_5m FROM crypto_ai.paper_position p JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal s ON s.id = p.signal_id LEFT JOIN crypto_ai.trade_signal f ON f.id = (SELECT f2.id FROM crypto_ai.trade_signal f2 WHERE f2.symbol = s.symbol AND f2.interval_code = '5m' AND f2.generated_at <= s.generated_at AND f2.generated_at >= DATE_SUB(s.generated_at, INTERVAL 10 MINUTE) ORDER BY f2.generated_at DESC LIMIT 1) WHERE p.status IN ('CLOSED','STOPPED') AND s.interval_code = '1m') x GROUP BY x.hidden_bear_5m, x.blocked_bull_5m LIMIT 10;
