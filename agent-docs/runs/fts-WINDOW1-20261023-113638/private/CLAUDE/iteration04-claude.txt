- **Trade 137 is now settled.** The exit needed the 1m STRONG_SELL (156850) plus the 5m SELL (156852); the 1h context (156853) was NEUTRAL. That fits SELL_CONFIRMED, so the exit followed the policy.

- [AGENT INFERENCE] Exit latency depends on how often ticks arrive. In these four trades it was harmless because price did not move during the wait. It is a latent risk for fast markets.

**F-I4-02: My earlier "stop-fill slippage" claim (F-I3-07) was wrong.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: I3-006]
- Across all 75 STOPPED trades, the average fill past the stop is 0.075% and the maximum is 0.559%.
- The −0.60% figure in I3 was the loss measured from entry, not slippage past the stop.
- Every stop fill (75 of 75) is below the stop. That is expected for a tick-triggered stop, but on average the slip is small. [AGENT INFERENCE] The 0.56% maximum matches the kind of gap seen in trade 143.

**F-I4-03: The 1.2–3.2 s gap between wallet and paper records is portfolio work inside the same transaction. This is now confirmed for both entries and exits.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + I3-004] (closes U-10)
- **Entry path.**
  - `WalletAutoExecutionService.executeBuy` (`:178`) stamps `executedAt`.
  - It then calls `currentPortfolioValue()` (`:190`) and `captureSnapshot()` (`:193`).
  - Only after that does `PaperTradingService` set `openedAt=Instant.now()` (`PaperTradingService.java:412`).
- **The snapshot rows match the paper timestamps to within 5 ms:**

| Trade | Snapshot | Captured (UTC) | Paper timestamp (UTC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 182 entry | 452 | 09:57:06.473 | opened 09:57:06.476 |

| 137 exit | 347 | 21:00:12.118 | closed 21:00:12.122 |
| 143 exit | 360 | 22:43:36.227 | closed 22:43:36.229 |

- **Where the time goes.** There are only 20 `wallet_asset` rows, 1 of them non-zero. Each portfolio pass still runs one `candleRepository.findFirstBySymbolAndIntervalCodeAndClosedTrueOrderByCloseTimeDesc` per asset (`WalletService.java:607`), plus `totalRealizedPnl` and `netInvested` aggregates (`:603`). Each trade runs at least 2–3 such passes.
- [AGENT INFERENCE] About 2 s for roughly 40–60 small queries suggests at least one query is not index-backed. DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-001 checks this.
- **Impact.** This is not an atomicity defect. It is a lock-hold and latency cost on every entry and exit. The fill price is fixed before this work starts, so P&L is unaffected.

**F-I4-04: Signal-path exits fill at the signal's decision price, not a fresh market price.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code]
- `PaperTradingService.closeFromSignal` uses `signal.getLatestPrice()` (`:449`).
- `WalletAutoExecutionService.executeSignalLinkedExit` uses the same value (`:230`).
- Entries, by contrast, must use a fresh canonical price ("TradeSignal.latestPrice … must not be reused as the wallet fill", `:74-77`). The SELL side is asymmetric.
- For 162, the fill is the close of the 04:25 UTC candle, used at 04:26:04 UTC (07:26:04 KSA), about 4 s later.
- [AGENT INFERENCE] The cost here is probably near zero; DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-005 measures it. It is a design inconsistency, not a confirmed defect for this trade.

**F-I4-05: After a non-stop-loss exit, the "evidence boundary" closes the opportunity record, but the next entry's evidence still counts signals from before the exit.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + I2-005]
- `completePositionOpportunity` (`ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:821-839`) marks the opportunity row `COMPLETED`. Its stated rule is "pre-exit opportunity evidence is consumed and cannot justify a new position."
- But `evidence()` (`:2241-2310`) recounts every 1m signal in a 30-minute window (`EVIDENCE_WINDOW`, `:47`). It stops early only on:
  - a 1m STRONG_SELL (`:2274`), or
  - the FIX-122 boundary (`StopLossEvidenceService.eligible`, `:99-103`), which is set only after stop-loss exits.
- **Trade 143.** The prior EGLD position 171 exited by PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT at 22:39:41 UTC (01:39:41 KSA). Only one new 1m signal (159408, at 22:40:01) existed after that exit. Yet the new opportunity 36524 recorded evidence_count=7 (1 BUY, 5 WATCH). Six of those seven came from before the exit.
- **Was it decisive?** [AGENT INFERENCE] Probably not for 143. The SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR route (`:1472-1491`) only needs the current 1m to be supportive (a WATCH above the score and confidence thresholds, `:2214-2218`), a fresh 5m BUY that permits ATR entry, and a 1h that is not bearish. Evidence count matters only in the neutral-timing branch.
- **Classification:** a confirmed mismatch between code and its own stated rule. Whether it changed any outcome is unproven. DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-002/003 test it.

**F-I4-07: Replay feeds protection one stored production price tick at a time, so it can reproduce the tick gaps. Signal visibility differs, though.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code] (closes U-15)
- `ShadowProductionReplayService.java:91-159` replays the stored production `market_price_event` stream in `observed_at` order. Each tick goes through `evaluateLivePrice` (`:387`), before each signal is made visible.
- That means the 143 gap fill at 4.027 and the 15–40 s exit waits are reproducible.
- **The difference.** Replay makes a signal visible at `generated_at` (`:129`). Production makes it visible at commit, which is `created_at` (+1.3 to 2.3 s), and only on a tick after that.
  - **158:** Replay would exit on the 03:30:02.320 tick. Production exited on the 03:30:04.319 tick. The price was the same (1.409), so the P&L matches but the exit time differs.
  - **Other cases:** 182, 172 and 137 match exactly, because no tick fell between generation and commit.
- **Clock mixing.** Replay compares Binance `observed_at` with the host's `generated_at`. These clocks are offset by about 130–150 ms (F-I2-09). [AGENT INFERENCE] This can move a tick to the other side of a signal at a boundary.
- **Parity verdict:** P&L parity is likely for all six trades. Exit-time parity holds for 182, 172 and 137 and fails for 158. 162, a signal-path exit, depends on whether Replay prices its signal exit at `latestPrice` (unchecked).

**F-I4-08: Trading stopped because the system ran intermittently, not because entries were all vetoed.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: I3-005] (narrows U-14)
- **13 Oct:** 1m candles stop at 00:51 UTC (03:51 KSA).
- **14 Oct:** 18,491 candles stored but only 3 signals.
- **15 Oct:** runs until 03:29 UTC (06:29 KSA).
- **16 Oct:** 16,881 candles stored and **0 signals**.
- **17 Oct:** ends at 03:26 UTC (06:26 KSA). After that there are no candles or signals up to the snapshot.
- [AGENT INFERENCE] Days that have candles but no signals look like backfilled or recovered candles, which by design never dispatch analysis (AGENTS.md recovery rule). Read that way, the app was largely down. This is outside the frozen set; it matters operationally.

**F-I4-09: Overall classification of the six trades so far.** [AGENT INFERENCE]
- **No stale data, no execution delay that cost money, and no code defect that caused any of the six losses.**
- **Shared root cause: the geometry.** Floor-stop trades had reward-to-risk of 0.33–0.74 (F-I2-03), and the targets are near the size of round-trip fees (F-I2-04). For SHIB and ACE, the price step is coarse relative to the targets (F-I2-07).
- **Trade 143.** A design outcome: the target was extended instead of taking profit, then a tick gap made the fill worse. Its entry route was coded behaviour, plus the F-I4-05 evidence mismatch, which was probably not decisive.
- **Code issues confirmed but not tied to these losses:**
  - F-I4-03: lock-hold latency.
  - F-I4-04: the signal-exit price asymmetry.
  - F-I4-05: the evidence-boundary mismatch.
  - F-I4-07: Replay's signal-visibility timing.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I4-004
Purpose: Across the system, how often a re-entry on the same symbol within 5 minutes of a non-stop exit happens, and how those re-entries turn out (generalises F-I4-05).
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN prev.status='STOPPED' THEN 'AFTER_STOP' ELSE 'AFTER_NON_STOP' END prev_kind,
       COUNT(*) n, SUM(nx.realized_pnl>0) wins, ROUND(SUM(nx.realized_pnl),6) pnl
FROM paper_position nx
JOIN paper_position prev ON prev.symbol=nx.symbol AND prev.closed_at IS NOT NULL
 AND prev.closed_at <= nx.opened_at AND prev.closed_at >= nx.opened_at - INTERVAL 5 MINUTE AND prev.id<>nx.id
WHERE nx.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY prev_kind LIMIT 10;
### END_DB_REQUEST
