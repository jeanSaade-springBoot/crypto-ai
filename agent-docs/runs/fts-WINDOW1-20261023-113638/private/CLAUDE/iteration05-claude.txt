# CLAUDE: FTS WINDOW1, Iteration 5 (entry quality, self-review)

I scored each of the six entries using only what was known at the decision moment. The outcome is shown separately. Two new problems turned up:
- **Trade 143's entry depended on a 2 ms race.** The route that let it in only worked because a newer 5m signal had been generated 2 ms too late to be seen.

Across the six, the main entry-quality problem is that the trades were set up to lose money before fees were even counted.

## FINDINGS

**F-I5-01: Trade 143's entry route depended on a 2 ms race between the 1m and 5m signals.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: I4-002, I1-012, I2-005, code]
- **How the 5m signal is chosen.** The route (`setupTimeframeAtrAuthoritySignal`, `ExecutionIntelligenceService.java:1485`) takes the latest 5m signal through `latestAtOrBefore`. That function filters on `generatedAt <= current.generatedAt` (`:2455-2461`, `:2606-2608`), not on candle close time.
- **What happened at entry:**

| Signal | Interval / candle (UTC) | Generated (UTC) | Decision | ATR position % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 159408 (entry) | 1m, 22:39 | 22:40:01.633945 | WATCH | 0 (WAIT_FOR_RETRACEMENT) |
| 159407 | 5m, 22:35–22:39:59 (already closed) | 22:40:01.635944 (**+2 ms**) | **WATCH** | 60 |
| 159285 (used) | 5m, 22:30–22:34:59 | 22:35:01.553 | BUY | 100 |

- The opportunity row (36524) records `five_minute_decision=BUY`, so the older 159285 was the 5m authority.
- **Had 159407 been visible, this route could not have fired.** It needs a BUY or STRONG_BUY 5m signal (`:1486`; `isBullish` at `:2634-2636`), and 159407 was a WATCH. Whether another route would have admitted the trade is unknown.
- **Price gap.** The 5m BUY was decided on a candle that closed at 3.986. The entry filled at 4.033, which is 1.18% higher. By then the next 5m candle had closed at 4.032 with a WATCH verdict.
- **Contrast with 137.** At the same kind of 5m boundary, 137 did see its fresh 5m signal (audit 1938: 5m age 1 s). Which 5m signal the system sees therefore depends on which analysis finishes first.
- **Classification.** [AGENT INFERENCE] Confirmed design defect: timing nondeterminism at 5m boundaries. The data-visibility rule uses analysis wall-clock time rather than closed-candle identity. Replay (`replayScope.latestAtOrBefore`) uses the same rule on stored `generated_at` values, so it will reproduce this outcome but not the race itself. DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-002 measures how often this happens.

**F-I5-02: F-I4-05 is confirmed. Six of the seven evidence signals behind 143 came from before the previous position closed, and one was itself not allowed to enter.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: I4-002, I2-003, I2-005]
- **Where the evidence walk stopped.** The 30-minute window walked back to the STRONG_SELL 159257 at 22:34 UTC (01:34 KSA, 12 Oct) and stopped there (`:2274`).
- **What it counted:**
  - 159281 BUY at 22:35 UTC, with `final_entry_allowed=0` and confidence 49.
  - WATCH signals 159321, 159337, 159361 and 159383, all generated during position 142's life.
  - 159408, the only signal after the exit.
- **Position 142's life.** Paper 142 / wallet 171 opened at 22:36:04 UTC on signal 159321 and exited by PROFIT_LOCK_EXIT at 3.996 at 22:39:42 UTC.
- **Opportunity 36524 was created fresh** at 22:40:01.634 UTC, already carrying evidence_count=7. The rule that "pre-exit evidence is consumed" was not enforced.
- **The whipsaw.** Price went 4.046 high → 3.996 exit → 4.033 re-entry, all within about 60 s.
- **Impact.** Not decisive for 143, because the route does not use evidence count. It is a confirmed mismatch between the code and its own stated rule.
- **System-wide (I4-004).**
  - 31 re-entries within 5 minutes of a non-stop exit: 14 wins (45%), net −1.07 USDT.
  - 6 re-entries after a stop-loss exit: 2 wins, net −0.48 USDT.
  - [AGENT INFERENCE] Quick re-entries lose money in aggregate. Sample is small.

**F-I5-05: Trade 182's fill price may not match the market at fill time (unverified anomaly).** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: I1-003, I1-012, I1-009, code]
- **The fill.** Paper entry was 0.1884, and the managed position's highest price was also 0.1884. The wallet BUY was at 09:57:04.232 UTC (12:57:04 KSA).
- **The market.** The 09:57 1m candle (row created 09:57:02.468) ranged only 0.1881–0.1882. 0.1884 was the close of the 09:56 candle.
- **How the price is chosen.** `ExecutionPriceAuthorityService.resolve` (`:46-48`) picks the latest `market_price_event` at or before `Instant.now()` that is no more than 15 s old.
- **Likely explanation.** [AGENT INFERENCE] The fill used a tick from 09:56:5x, even though a 09:57 tick existed or was about to be stored. Two possible reasons:
  - (a) the roughly 150 ms host-versus-Binance clock offset (F-I2-09) made the newest tick look "future-dated";
  - (b) the newest tick was not yet APPLIED.
- **Cost.** About 2 price steps (≈0.106%) against the buyer, which is half of the trade's total loss.
- **Classification.** Not confirmed; DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-001 settles it. This does not contradict F-I4-04: entries do use a fresh tick, but which tick counts as "freshest" is in question.

**F-I5-06: No entry gate rejects trades that are uneconomic before fees.** [PROJECT EVIDENCE: code + F-I2-03; AGENT INFERENCE]
- No route checks reward-to-risk ≥ 1, or that the target distance is larger than round-trip cost.
- Fees are not recorded (`fee_usdt=0`), so the system cannot see this cost in its own P&L.
- With 4 of 6 frozen targets at or below roughly 0.2%, this is the main entry-quality root cause.
- This is a design gap, not a code defect against any stated rule. The only ≥1:1 comment (`PaperTradingService.java:533-536`) covers a route that did not fire here.

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-002
Purpose: How often the 5m-boundary race happens: the 5m signal for the just-closed 5m candle being generated after the 1m signal at the same boundary (F-I5-01, U-20). First query: 159407's commit time. Second: the race rate across all boundary 1m signals on 11–12 Oct. Third: the race among executed entries.
SQL:
SELECT id, interval_code, candle_open_time, generated_at, created_at, decision, final_entry_allowed, atr_entry_type, atr_recommended_position_percent FROM trade_signal WHERE id IN (159407,159408,159285,156721,156723) ORDER BY id;
SELECT COUNT(*) n, SUM(f.generated_at > s.generated_at) five_after_one, SUM(f.generated_at <= s.generated_at) five_before_one
FROM trade_signal s JOIN trade_signal f ON f.symbol=s.symbol AND f.interval_code='5m' AND f.candle_open_time = s.candle_open_time - INTERVAL 4 MINUTE
WHERE s.interval_code='1m' AND MINUTE(s.candle_open_time) % 5 = 4
  AND s.generated_at >= '2026-10-11 00:00:00' AND s.generated_at < '2026-10-13 00:00:00';
SELECT pp.id, pp.symbol, pp.realized_pnl, s.generated_at s1m, f.id f5m_id, f.generated_at f5m_gen, f.decision f5m_decision
FROM paper_position pp JOIN trade_signal s ON s.id=pp.signal_id AND s.interval_code='1m' AND MINUTE(s.candle_open_time) % 5 = 4
LEFT JOIN trade_signal f ON f.symbol=s.symbol AND f.interval_code='5m' AND f.candle_open_time = s.candle_open_time - INTERVAL 4 MINUTE
WHERE pp.closed_at IS NOT NULL ORDER BY pp.id LIMIT 60;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-003
Purpose: System-wide entry outcomes by execution route, by overextension and by regime/strategy (U-22). Tests whether the frozen-set entry patterns generalise.
SQL:
SELECT eo.execution_source, eo.decision_code, COUNT(*) n, SUM(pp.realized_pnl>0) wins, ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl),6) pnl
FROM paper_position pp JOIN execution_opportunity eo ON eo.latest_signal_id=pp.signal_id AND eo.status='EXECUTED'
WHERE pp.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY eo.execution_source, eo.decision_code ORDER BY n DESC LIMIT 30;
SELECT ts.atr_overextended, ts.decision, ts.market_regime, ts.selected_strategy, COUNT(*) n, SUM(pp.realized_pnl>0) wins, ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl),6) pnl,
       ROUND(AVG((ts.take_profit-ts.latest_price)/ts.latest_price*100),4) avg_tp_pct
FROM paper_position pp JOIN trade_signal ts ON ts.id=pp.signal_id
WHERE pp.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY ts.atr_overextended, ts.decision, ts.market_regime, ts.selected_strategy ORDER BY n DESC LIMIT 40;
### END_DB_REQUEST

### DB_REQUEST
QueryId: DBQ-CLAUDE-I5-004
Purpose: How many closed trades had a target at or below a 0.20% round trip, and how they turned out (F-I5-06).
SQL:
SELECT CASE WHEN (ts.take_profit-ts.latest_price)/ts.latest_price*100 <= 0.15 THEN 'TP<=0.15'
            WHEN (ts.take_profit-ts.latest_price)/ts.latest_price*100 <= 0.20 THEN 'TP<=0.20'
            WHEN (ts.take_profit-ts.latest_price)/ts.latest_price*100 <= 0.30 THEN 'TP<=0.30' ELSE 'TP>0.30' END tp_bucket,
       COUNT(*) n, SUM(pp.realized_pnl>0) wins, ROUND(SUM(pp.realized_pnl),6) gross_pnl,
       ROUND(SUM(pp.quantity*pp.entry_price*0.002),6) est_fee_0_20pct
FROM paper_position pp JOIN trade_signal ts ON ts.id=pp.signal_id
WHERE pp.closed_at IS NOT NULL GROUP BY tp_bucket ORDER BY tp_bucket LIMIT 10;
### END_DB_REQUEST
