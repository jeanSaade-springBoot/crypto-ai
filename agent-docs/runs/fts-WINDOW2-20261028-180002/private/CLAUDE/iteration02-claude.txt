## FINDINGS

### F1. The risk plan is inverted on 5 of 6 trades (PROJECT EVIDENCE; code + DB)

- In `AtrRiskService.java:34-45` the stop distance is `max(2×ATR, 0.50% of price)` (`minimum-stop-percent: 0.50`, `application.yml:143`). The target is `3×ATR` with **no matching floor**, and I found no minimum-R:R gate (grep for one returned nothing).
- So when ATR% is below about 0.25%, the planned R:R falls below 1.
- DBQ-CLAUDE-I1-002 and I1-005 show the effect. Break-even win rate is SL ÷ (SL + TP), before fees.

| ID | ATR % | SL dist | TP dist | Planned R:R | Break-even win rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 158 | 0.05% | 0.49% | 0.17% | 0.35 | ~74% |
| 162 | 0.07% | 0.50% | 0.22% | 0.44 | ~70% |
| 182 | 0.08% | 0.50% | 0.24% | 0.48 | ~68% |
| 137 | 0.11% | 0.50% | 0.32% | 0.64 | ~61% |
| 172 | 0.12% | 0.50% | 0.37% | 0.74 | ~57% |
| 143 | 0.27% | 0.57% | 1.77% | 3.13 | ~24% (TP from the 5m ATR plan) |

- All six had `volatility_level` LOW.
- **AGENT INFERENCE:** this is structural rather than a code defect. It works as coded, but in low-volatility regimes it builds negative expectancy into every trade.
- I'm not classifying it as a defect until the intended policy is confirmed (U-New3). It is the strongest systemic finding so far.

### F2. Fees are larger than the targets (EXTERNAL RESEARCH + PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- Paper notional is about 15–25 USDT (for example 79.6 ACE × 0.1884 ≈ 15.0; 6.2 EGLD × 4.033 ≈ 25.0).
- Binance's standard spot fee is 0.1% per side (general knowledge, not fetched this iteration), so the round-trip cost is about 0.2%.
- That exceeds the planned TP distance of 158 (0.17%) and is close to 162 and 182.
- TRADING_RULES §6 says paper P&L excludes fees, so the realised losses understate the true cost.
- DBQ-CLAUDE-I2-003 checks `wallet_trade.fee_usdt`.

### F3. SHIBUSDT's ATR is smaller than one price tick (EXTERNAL RESEARCH + PROJECT EVIDENCE)

- Binance `exchangeInfo` gives SHIBUSDT a tickSize of `0.00000001`, which is 0.182% of the 0.00000549 entry.
- Trade 172's ATR14 was `0.000000006806`, or **0.68 ticks**.
- The TP needed +3 ticks and the SL sat at −3 ticks. The loss was exactly **1 tick** (549 → 548).
- The ATR plan carries no real information at this resolution.
- ACE is also coarse: tick = 0.053% of price and ATR = 1.5 ticks.
- **AGENT INFERENCE:** this is a symbol-suitability gap. It may need a ticks-per-ATR eligibility check, as a recommendation only.

### F4. Trade 143: the lock was breached by a flash wick, not by drift (PROJECT EVIDENCE + EXTERNAL RESEARCH)

- **Price path (Binance 1m klines, UTC):**
  - 19:40 candle: open 4.033, high 4.067, close 4.056
  - 19:41 candle: high **4.101**, matching `highest_price_usdt` 4.101
  - 19:42 candle: low 4.077, which stayed above the lock price 4.07579
  - **19:43 candle: open 4.086, low 3.994, close 4.026**
- **The exit:** at 19:43:36 UTC it filled at 4.027. That is **4.9 cents below the lock** (4.0758) and below the hard floor (4.0350).
- **Was it a defect?** The 19:43 low of 3.994 was below the SL of 4.0102, so a stop-loss would otherwise have fired. The 4.027 fill was better than the SL would have given.
- **Lock level checks out:** `profit_lock_price` 4.07579 = entry + 60% × TP distance (4.033 + 0.6 × 0.071317). The stored progress of 95.35% = (4.101 − 4.033) ÷ 0.071317.
- **Classification:** most likely a valid loss caused by market microstructure, in which price moved past the lock between two observed ticks. Not yet proven: whether the price-event feed had a gap or delay at 19:43 (DBQ-CLAUDE-I2-005/006).

### F5. Trade 143: profit-lock activation is inconsistent with the stored TP (PROJECT EVIDENCE; unresolved)

- `profit_lock_activated_at` displays 22:40:23, which is 19:40:23 UTC, 19 s after entry.
- Activation at 70% of the stored TP distance needs price ≥ 4.0829, but the 19:40 candle's high was only 4.067.
- Activation does reconcile with the **1m signal TP** of 4.0647: 70% of that distance is about 4.0549.
- Hypothesis: the lock activated against the 1m TP, and the TP was then raised to the 5m-ATR value 4.1043 (SETUP_TIMEFRAME_ATR or a FIX-118 extension). The lock may therefore have been armed on a different TP than the one stored.
