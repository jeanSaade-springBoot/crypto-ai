- Protection is independently driven by observed price, not delayed candle analysis. - Live protection evaluates exits in this order: TP/continuation, absolute stop loss, profit-lock safety floor, profit-lock breach policy, normal multi-timeframe SELL policy, then near-TP logic. [LivePositionProtectionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/position/service/LivePositionProtectionService.java:95)

- A live mechanical exit writes the wallet exit first and only then closes the matching `paper_position`; this route is materially stronger evidence of a committed paper-wallet exit. [WalletAutoExecutionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/wallet/service/WalletAutoExecutionService.java:475) · [LivePositionProtectionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/position/service/LivePositionProtectionService.java:392)

- Conversely, the signal-driven close route persists `paper_position` before attempting the wallet exit and catches wallet exceptions. Therefore, a closed `paper_position` alone does **not** prove the corresponding wallet SELL committed for a signal-path exit. This is a code-route evidence limitation, not a confirmed defect in any frozen trade. [PaperTradingService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/service/PaperTradingService.java:487)

- Both paper-position and wallet exit calculations use price-minus-entry times quantity, preserving any prior near-TP partial realization; the paper wallet writes zero fees. A negative `paper_position.realized_pnl` is therefore not, by itself, a fee-inclusive execution reconciliation. [PaperTradingService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/service/PaperTradingService.java:487) · [WalletAutoExecutionService.java](/C:/workspace/crypto-ai-trader-mysql/src/main/java/com/crypto/wallet/service/WalletAutoExecutionService.java:475)
