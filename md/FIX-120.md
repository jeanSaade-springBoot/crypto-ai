# FIX-120 — Profit Lock geometry reset at Take-Profit extension

## Status

IMPLEMENTED — narrow Production/Replay trading-semantic correction to FIX-118.

## Frozen invariant

Whenever continuation approves a new Take Profit, the previous executable Profit Lock is no longer a valid calculation baseline. The position is evaluated against the newly-approved TP geometry.

- If current/highest progress against the new TP is below the configured activation threshold, the position enters or remains `TP_EXTENSION_REBASE`; the historical lock remains persisted for audit/legacy compatibility but is non-executable.
- If progress remains at/above activation, the position remains `ACTIVE`, but the executable lock is recalculated from the new geometry by calling the existing `ProfitLockPolicy` with `previouslyActive=false` and `previousLock=null`.
- At the TP-extension boundary, raw `extensionPrice` is passed as `current`, while `extensionHighest` is passed as the previous-highest seed.
- When `TP_EXTENSION_REBASE` later re-earns activation, reactivation also creates a fresh current-geometry lock rather than restoring the historical lock.
- After the fresh geometry baseline is established, existing monotonic Profit Lock trailing resumes unchanged.

## Why the lock may move down or up

The prior lock was calculated against a different target distance. Therefore FIX-120 deliberately allows the freshly-calculated absolute lock to be lower or higher than the historical lock. Correctness is defined by the existing `ProfitLockPolicy` result for the new geometry, not by comparison with the superseded lock.

This exception exists only at a TP geometry boundary. Normal Profit Lock evaluation between extensions remains monotonic and is unchanged.

## BICOUSDT #1055

Historical final extension:

- Entry: `0.022990`
- Previous TP: `0.023294198734`
- New TP: `0.023446298101`
- Extension price/highest: `0.023310`
- New-geometry progress: `70.129593%`
- Historical lock: `0.023218149051`

Because progress remains above the 70% activation threshold, the correct state is still `ACTIVE`. But the old lock is discarded as the policy baseline and a fresh lock is calculated from the new target:

`0.022990 + 40% × (0.023446298101 - 0.022990) = 0.023172519240`

At the historical Profit Lock exit price `0.023200`, the fresh lock is not triggered because `0.023200 > 0.023172519240`.

## Preserved behavior

FIX-120 does not change `ProfitLockPolicy.evaluate()` itself, the configured 70% activation / 40% initial lock / 10% trail step, TP-extension qualification or formula, Stop Loss, FIX-118 protected-profit hard floor, normal SELL authority, FIX-117/Near-TP, entry logic, sizing, FIX-119, or execution priority ordering.

## Regression coverage

The regression suite covers: ICP #1010; ICP #1014; exact BICO #1055; a valid geometry transition where the fresh lock is lower; a valid geometry transition where the extension tick causes the fresh lock to be higher; normal monotonic trailing after the new baseline; REBASE→ACTIVE fresh reactivation; repeated extensions while REBASE preserving the original timestamp; normal SELL authority during REBASE; and Production/Replay parity for both ACTIVE→ACTIVE and ACTIVE→REBASE cases.
