# FIX-118 — Profit Lock TP-extension rebase

## Status

IMPLEMENTED — narrow Production/Replay trading-semantic correction.

## Goal

When continuation approves a wider take-profit target, a Dynamic Profit Lock earned against the superseded target must not remain artificially tight merely because Profit Lock is normally monotonic within one unchanged TP geometry.

## Original defect

ICPUSDT #1010 and #1014 showed that an ACTIVE historical lock could remain executable after TP extension even when progress against the wider current target fell below the configured activation threshold. FIX-118 introduced `TP_EXTENSION_REBASE`, making that historical lock non-executable until the current TP geometry re-earned activation, while preserving Stop Loss and a direct protected-profit floor.

## BICO #1055 follow-up — corrected by FIX-120

BICOUSDT managed position #1055 exposed a second edge case on 2026-09-07 KSA. Entry was 0.022990. The fourth continuation extension changed TP from 0.023294198734 to 0.023446298101 at market price 0.023310. Progress against the new target was 70.129593%, barely above the configured 70% activation threshold, so the original FIX-118 implementation remained `ACTIVE` and retained the older lock 0.023218149051. Production later exited around 0.023200 through `POSITION_PROFIT_LOCK` even though that lock belonged to the previous TP geometry.

FIX-120 subsequently froze the stronger invariant: **every approved TP extension establishes Profit Lock state/lock from the new geometry rather than carrying the superseded lock as the policy baseline**.

- If current/new-TP progress is below activation, enter/continue `TP_EXTENSION_REBASE`. The historical lock stays persisted for audit compatibility but is non-executable.
- When a rebasing position re-earns activation, calculate a fresh executable lock from the current TP geometry. Do not carry the historical pre-extension lock through the policy's monotonic-lock rule.
- If extension-time progress is already at or above activation, remain `ACTIVE` but immediately replace the superseded lock with a fresh current-geometry lock.
- Stop Loss remains absolute. The existing FIX-118 `entry × 1.0005` protected-profit floor remains available during `TP_EXTENSION_REBASE`.
- FIX-117 Near-TP thresholds, state machine, priority, and partial-harvest behavior are unchanged.
- Normal signal/thesis exits remain available.
- Production and Replay use equivalent semantics.

For BICO #1055, the fresh lock under the final TP geometry is 0.023172519240 rather than the superseded 0.023218149051. Therefore the historical 0.023200 exit is no longer produced by Dynamic Profit Lock solely because of the stale pre-extension lock.

## Regression coverage

`DynamicProfitLockServiceTest` covers:

- ACTIVE → `TP_EXTENSION_REBASE` when new-geometry progress is below activation.
- Historical ICP #1010 and #1014 locks remain non-executable during rebase.
- Rebase reactivation rebuilds the lock from current geometry rather than retaining a tighter stale lock.
- Historical BICO #1055 final extension at 70.129593% recalculates the ACTIVE lock to 0.023172519240 and does not trigger at the historical 0.023200 exit price.
- Repeated extensions while already rebasing retain the original rebase timestamp.
- The standalone protected-profit floor remains available.

`LivePositionProtectionServiceFix118RebaseTest` continues to verify the standalone hard floor and that normal SELL authority remains available during rebase.
