Trading configuration

Entry validation

1m remains the only execution timeframe. The profile changes only confirmation strictness and BUY size. Bearish 5m/1h context and existing risk vetoes still block the entry.

Dynamic Profit Lock

Default: activate at 70% of the target, protect 40%, then raise the protected level by one trail step as the best price advances. Once active, normal SELL signals are suppressed above the protected level; stop-loss, take-profit and the profit-lock trigger remain immediate.

Dashboard header performance

This controls the frozen Dashboard header: net realized P&L, wins/losses, win rate, Top Winner and Top Loser. Only executed closed wallet trades are included.

Dashboard intervals

4h and 1 day are display-only views derived from stored 1h candles. The trading engine remains fixed at 1m / 5m / 1h.

When the daily limit is enabled, the BUY budget is calculated from available USDT divided by the configured maximum. When Unlimited is selected, every BUY uses the configured trade amount until available USDT reaches the reserve. SELLs never count as new positions.
